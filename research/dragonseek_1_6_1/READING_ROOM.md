## 5. Release and provenance semantics
`COLLABORATOR_REPORTED` is a contributor/source-origin status; `CORROBORATED`, `NOT_CHECKED`, etc. concern independent verification; `NOT_CONNECTED`, `INGESTION_TESTED`, `OPERATIONAL` concern runtime status. Do not promote a source merely because it appears in a feed catalogue. No model weights, licensed provider credentials or private Drive documents are exposed in this central public reading room.
