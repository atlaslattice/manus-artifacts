## Crisis-to-recovery timeline (proposed simulation contract)
1. **Pre-disaster 30–365d:** firebreaks/roof retrofits, stormwater/shoreline maintenance, source-qualified public risk census, resident evacuation/medical access plan, 7-day water/power/food service reserves, staff training and false-alarm tests.
2. **Watch 72–0h:** official CMA/MEM hazard notices with signed attribution and issued/valid time, probabilistic forecast uncertainty, shelters, accessible emergency transport, supplies and communications redundancy. Model-derived forecast is NOT an official order.
3. **First 0–72h:** independent islanding at authorized facilities, potable/emergency water pathways, cooling/refrigeration, mobile communication, human incident command, evacuation/medical care, roadway status; no AI physical dispatch.
4. **Days 4–7:** critical services 168h target, generator/BESS SoC, solar replenishment under adverse clouds, common-cause failure, accessibility and medical refill operations.
5. **Week 2 to months:** verified shelter/home rebuilding, waste and contaminated debris segregation, clean recovery supply chains, port logistics after recovery, anti-displacement and community governance.
6. **Months to years:** soil/reef/groundwater cleanup, permanent renewable electrical services, wetland/coast restoration, QOL, insurance/legal assistance, measurable recovery equity.

## Explicitly missing / not yet claimable as "fully incorporated"
- Distinct as-built Lahaina microgrid meter time series, protective devices, weather hours and topology; full survey with community consent.
- Full hazard GIS/census data legitimately accessible for coastal Dongjiakou and Maui; local storm-surge/flood/tsunami/currents physical models need calibration and coastal datum transform.
- Complete service/restart dependencies and independent black-start tests under staffing, fuel, food and water constraints; multi-hazard compound Monte Carlo sensitivity.
- Housing materials, EPS and algal treatment are separate regulated product/permit branches, not immediate reconstruction supply.
- Genuine regional dashboards are not yet connected to official CMA/NSMC, police, emergency services, hospital or CII networks.
