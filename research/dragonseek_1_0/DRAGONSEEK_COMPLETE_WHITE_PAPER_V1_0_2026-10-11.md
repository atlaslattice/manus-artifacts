## 2. Four-level evidentiary hierarchy / Evidence hierarchy
- **MEASURED / 实测:** instrument readings with date, calibration, sampling protocol, QA/QC, independent accountability and physical boundary.
- **REPORTED / 发布:** official annual report or stated historic operator figure; valid only for publisher's specified geography, period, definition and reporting basis.
- **DESIGNED / 设计:** nameplate/permit/design physical maximum; never imply operation, availability, utilization, export rights or actual demand.
- **SCENARIO / 情景:** user-supplied or synthetic hypothesis, explicitly marked, not cash, output or official inventory.
- **UNKNOWN / 待核实:** no evidence. Absence must not silently become zero, potential or expected performance.
- **QUARANTINED / 待隔离:** unsupported or contradicted assertions (e.g. Hongwu award and '50 Mt refined products') until primary receipts.

**No DOUBLE COUNT:** city agricultural total includes straw/manure/film categories; city total includes industrial/MSW/ag/construction/sludge/hazard classes. No cross-dataset addition unless categories are demonstrably disjoint. 'Used' may include older inventories. A shipment cannot be both source surplus, destination new supply and new municipality-wide generation. Flag material ID, year, producer, gate receipt, owner, contract, destination and allocation fraction.

## 3. Scenario mathematics: bounds not forecasts
**A. Qingdao 200,000 t/y city-wide food-waste hypothesis** (unverified as actual food-waste generation/collection): Illustrative eligible fractions 0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100% give 0, 50k, 100k, 150k, 200k t/y *gross eligible*, before contamination, contracts, losses and existing treatment obligations. This is a deliberately unconstrained sensitivity sweep, NOT capacity available to DJK. A Dongjiakou allocation is **zero until authorized shares/transport/plant capacity are known**, but that zero is a *model booking rule* rather than a claim that the true physical supply is zero.

**B. Xiaojianxi design capacity:** 500 t/day × 365 days = 182,500 t/y *365-day design-year ceiling*; × 300 assumed operating days = 150,000 t/y hypothetical scenario. Historic cumulative processing 260,000 t (2023 through late-2024 reported) is not annual utilization. Xiaojianxi catchment does not include Dongjiakou by reported design; interdistrict diversion cannot be assumed.

**C. Qingdao collectible straw accounting:** 3,655,000 − 3,523,000 = 132,000 t apparent citywide difference. That difference is **NOT** unallocated feedstock because of quality, timing, stocks, soil-retention constraints, price and physical transport. Any economic surplus requires field and contract receipts. Crop-specific seasonal balances and soil organic-carbon/erosion safeguards required.

**D. Desalination design-only scenario:** 100,000 m³/day × 365 = 36.5 Mm³/y design; electricity at claimed 2.2 kWh/m³ *this scenario* = 80.3 GWh/y. Historic supply >50,000 m³/day (2022 reporting) is noncontemporaneous and should not be used as 2026 measured utilization. Reject any 'zero discharge' claim unless the actual brine salt and liquid balance closes.

**E. Oil terminal:** 25.5 Mt/y design phase-II capacity, **oil/cargo handling**, not local acid production, sulfur availability, refining conversion, energy recovery or a chemical feedstock credit.

**F. Area throughput:** 741 Mt and 32.89m TEU refer to *all Qingdao port*, not DJK. Terminal-specific actual flow remains UNKNOWN.

## 4. BIO01-E: auditable physical ledger
Suggested mandatory fields per physical movement/event:
`event_id, physical_boundary_id, administrative_scope, parent_geography, site_id, origin_party_id, receiving_party_id, material_code, hazard_class, transaction_id, mass_wet_t, moisture_fraction, mass_dry_t, carbon_nitrogen_phosphorus_sulfur_assays, salt_content, energy_content, sampling_method, uncertainty_interval, origin_and_destination_time, truck_weighbridge_ticket, legal_transfer_basis, contract_id, prior_sink_commitment, environmental_permit_id, QA_state, source_document_uri, provenance_hash`.

**Invariant wet/dry:** `dry_t = wet_t × (1 - moisture_fraction)` with dry+water=wet and constraints [0,1]. **Sorting:** `gross_wet = eligible_wet + reject_wet + measurement_adjustment_wet`; require reconciliation, do not conceal adjustments. **Conversions:** all outputs/residues/emissions/retained inventories must close MASS, elemental (C/N/P/S), water, energy and economics (including purchased external inputs), within documented measurement tolerance. Never equate avoided disposal to released fertilizer/food without assays and certification. Mycoprotein/fertilizer/feed require separate food safety, contaminants, species, permits and market eligibility reviews. **Land:** model residue return and soil health as an ecological veto before allocating straw to plants. **Water:** salinity load, effluent fate, residual concentrated salts, thermal load and mixing zone; ecological water needs are positive requirements, not waste.

**Spatial gate:** Qingdao_CITY or Shandong_PROVINCE material cannot be treated as measured DJK_ONSITE or DJK_CONTRACTED_INBOUND. Valid cross-boundary movement needs `source_id + origin/destination + weighbridge + time + actual contract/approval + no existing destination displacement`. No automatic national summing of port throughput and raw-material use.

## C3. 硫化学计量 3.059 与石膏项目 0.40 t/t 是**不同分母，不能直接比较产率**

元素硫质量守恒给出：
\`M(H2SO4) / M(S) ≈ 98.079 / 32.065 ≈ 3.059 t pure H2SO4 per t elemental S\`，属于以**纯硫元素吨数为分母**的理论化学计量。

宜昌市生态环境局2026-03-27第二次环评公示：湖北宜化楚星项目在宜都化工园拟**处理100万吨/年磷石膏，生产40万吨/年工业硫酸，并副产硅酸盐掺和材料**。
https://hbj.yichang.gov.cn/content-42531-996867-1.html

因此 **40/100 = 0.40 t industrial acid per t incoming phosphogypsum** 是**规划设计产品量/进料量**，不是实际运行测量、不是普适“实践收率上限”，更不是每吨硫产酸比率。还须确认磷石膏进料湿基/干基、CaSO4·2H2O含量、工业酸浓度、产物计量与副产品收率。

参考化学计量：1 mol CaSO4·2H2O 含1 mol S；纯二水硫酸钙含硫质量约18.6%，故每吨**纯且干燥的二水石膏**对应理论纯硫酸约 **98.08/172.17 ≈ 0.570 t/t**；混有水和杂质的实料目标显著不同。相比之下0.40 t/t设计不自动证明转化效率0.40/0.570，除非明确基准及酸浓度。

## New issue independently identified: at least two tce accounting conventions
The v0.7 prototype uses **29,307,000,000 J/tce** as a fixed recipe; DeepSeek recommends **29,307,600,000 J/tce** citing GB/T2589-2020 and engineering calorific conversion. The Chinese **National Bureau of Statistics Energy Statistics Department** specifies **29,271,200,000 J/tce** for current statistical reporting (2022-09-29 public reply). Therefore:
1. Never silently use a universal tce energy equivalent in cross-domain federation.
2. Require an explicit `accounting_policy`, effective reporting period, source standard, and whether the value is an accounting energy equivalent or measured physical energy.
3. In v0.8 *research prototype*, `GB_T_2589_2020_ENGINEERING` maps to 29,307,600,000 J/tce and `NBS_2022_ENERGY_STATISTICS` maps to 29,271,200,000 J/tce. Choice must be made by competent domain owner, not guessed. Historic v0.7 remains unchanged, with the old factor deprecated for new calculations.
4. The difference is **36.4 MJ/tce** (~0.1243% of NBS convention). This is an accounting basis discrepancy, not evidence that measured calorific content differs.

# Report A: Measurement Semantics, Data Interfaces and Solid Waste Policy

According to the latest publicly available information, several developments directly affect DragonSeek 2.0's semantic architecture and calibration of the Dongjiakou node, particularly in three areas: **measurement-semantic standards, data-interface specifications and solid-waste management policy**.

## I. Measurement-semantic standards: two national standards directly address gaps

### GB/T 47851-2026 — Identification of Units of Measurement for Computer Processing

This is **the most direct answer to v0.7's request for a semantic unit contract**. The standard reportedly sets out basic principles, system architecture, semantic description methods and exchange-format requirements for identifying measurement units in computer-processing environments. Its goal is to ensure that unit identifiers can be interpreted unambiguously by computer systems.

The project proposal says the absence of uniform identifier specifications has long caused inconsistent expressions, coding rules and semantic interpretation. The standard is intended to fill that gap and improve interoperability across systems and fields.

**Implication for v0.7:** The proposed \`unit_contract\`—unit symbols, aliases, canonical dimensions, versions and effective periods—closely matches its purpose. DragonSeek should align with the national standard rather than unilaterally define a competing format.

### Revised GB 3100 — The International System of Units and Its Application (consultation draft)

The revised draft reportedly advises avoiding language-dependent \`ppb\` and \`ppt\`, disallows informal abbreviations such as \`hrs\` and \`sec\`, and clarifies \`ppm\`. It gives preference to unit symbols such as \`m\`, \`kg\`, \`s\` and \`A\` in engineering and scientific writing and discourages mixtures of Chinese-unit characters and unit symbols within a composite unit.

**Implication:** v0.7 was right to reserve \`ppm\` for a specialized contextual protocol rather than treating it as an unambiguous physical unit. The draft should be tracked as a future mandatory framework **if and when it is promulgated and takes effect**, not as a presently enacted replacement.

## II. Environmental monitoring data semantics: HJ 212-2025 is effective

**HJ 212-2025 — Technical Requirements for Data Transmission of Pollutant Automatic Monitoring and Control Systems**, reportedly effective January 1, 2026, supersedes HJ 212-2017.

The report describes end-to-end traceability for collection, transmission and storage, a unified digital identity framework, rules for data acquisition, calculation and validity checks, and requirements for network security and key management.

**Implication:** \`MONITORING-QUALITY-01\` and \`AUDIT-EVENT-01\` should map to the standard's actual applicable acquisition, transport and traceability fields. This does **not by itself establish** that the standard specifies our analytical \`CENSORED_LEFT\` and uncertainty fields; that requires a clause-by-clause crosswalk.
