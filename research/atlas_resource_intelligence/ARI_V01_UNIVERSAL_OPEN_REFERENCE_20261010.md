# Atlas Resource Intelligence (ARI) — Universal Open Reference Architecture v0.1
Date: 2026-10-10
Status: PROPOSED · NON-CANON · ZERO REALIZED CREDIT
Scope: Research specification and simulation reference; not a deployment, authorization, affiliation, or official policy implementation.

## Purpose and independence
Atlas Lattice / Atlas Resource Intelligence is an independent, fully open-source, forkable, jurisdiction-neutral reference model for regenerative industrial symbiosis, resource intelligence, resilience, and human quality of life. It is offered as a gift for voluntary adaptation anywhere; no country, AI company, vendor, or national network is a required dependency. Governments and local operators control their own deployments, software, data, policy, security, procurement, and governance. Model compatibility does not imply adoption, endorsement, operational access, or funding.

## Reference implementations and portability
Dongjiakou/Qingdao (China) and Lahaina (Hawaii) are parallel locality models. More than 100 other locations and dialects are reported in the project's broader research, subject to repository-by-repository audit. No locality is a global upstream authority. Universal schemas + local policy overlays + interchangeable adapters + offline-first autonomy + versioned scenario definitions enable independent forks.

## Three layers
1. Physical resources: audited batches and custody records for municipal organics, agricultural residues, retail losses, wastewater, sludge, waste oil, industrial co-products. Food rescue/donation precedes conversion.
2. Provenance and computation: mass-based batch identity, optional Kunyu-like traceability adapter, optional 1+M+N-style distributed compute adapter; both independently replaceable. Cryptographic audit receipts without assuming blockchain is mandatory; privacy/data sovereignty by design.
3. Optimization: constrained material, energy, economics and resilience optimizer with independent pathways for fungal protein (Fusarium), acidogenic fermentation and Schizochytrium SCP/DHA, PHA from qualified recovered oils, anaerobic digestion/CHP, nutrients/struvite, insect protein, enzymes, biochar/hydrochar, compost and agriculture. No double-counting of wet mass, dry mass, C/N/P/S/K, heat/electricity, feed, oil, emissions, or recovered carbon.

## Evidence classes
MEASURED_SITE, MEASURED_STUDY, REPORTED_OPERATION, REPORTED_POLICY, SIMULATED_ASSUMPTION, PROPOSED, QUARANTINED. Include source, timestamp, geography, wet/dry basis, uncertainty and units. National expansion scenarios are stress tests, not forecasts. Published model claims are not realized results.

- Identify separate nonoverlapping agricultural postharvest, retail, restaurant, household, sewage, oil, and forestry pools.
- For every process define input composition, sorting loss, qualified solids, yield coefficients, coproduct allocation, downstream processing, utilities, contaminant limits and economics.
- Retain Tianjin organic-acid figures (258.23 kg/t food waste; 37 g/L; 90% acid consumed in 48h) as *reported study*, not final protein yield.
- Preserve distinction among 90% collected/accessible FLW, 90% acid consumption and actual kg protein/t feedstock.
- Independently validate feed/nutrition substitution and oil coproduct constraints; quantify domestic vs foreign fertilizer/sulfur demand separately.
- Treat waste-oil to PHA company yields and prices as reported and gross, not site-verified profits. Biochar 455.7 MtCO2e/y is a mixed-feedstock national study and must not be attributed solely to food waste.
- Benchmark economy-only, resilience-weighted and disaster/islanding scenarios. Monetize no unmeasured resilience reward.

## Scope C: ATLAS-DATA01 digital spec
Versioned batch schema: jurisdiction, source class, timestamp, owner/custodian, consent/license, geospatial precision, mass wet/dry, moisture, assays C/N/P/S/K/Mg/Na/contaminants, energetic content, admissible uses, prices, capacity, process map, coproducts, units, provenance, access permissions. Pluggable adapters: local CSV/Parquet, SQLite/Postgres, national compute API, traceability ledger, secure sensor ingestion. Human approval for physical action and deployment. Test disconnected operation.

## Execution governance
- Original files never overwritten; changes via additive paths and draft review/PR.
- GPT: modeling, audit, cross-source synthesis. DeepSeek: Chinese-language review / local regulatory interpretation, no implication of official representation. Manus: bounded tool execution with checklists and verification receipts. Other models are substitutable; no exclusive dependence or autonomous permission.
- Local forks retain local authority. Proprietary platforms or governments may independently adopt or fork under the actual applicable repository license; audit license terms, dependencies and sensitive-data exclusions before offering as universally reusable.
- No claimed funding, official partnership, plant construction, operational integration or national policy adoption.
