## Five Maui research modules
1. `MAT01`: Clean source-separated mineral C&D sorting, conventional aggregate, optional experimental 3D printable mix; fire ash/mixed debris prohibited as assumed structural feedstock.
2. `W01`: Water balance by **watershed**, permitted withdrawals, instream requirements, reclaimed water quality, agricultural use, coastal outflow. East Maui diversion and West Maui sources remain separately bounded.
3. `E01`: Critical-service islanding (refrigeration, communications, essential water services), solar/BESS and backup. Hourly or 15-minute metered coincidence; no annual solar self-sufficiency shortcuts.
4. `AGR01/BIO01`: Existing land use, soil restoration, sustainable biomass residue fraction, nutrient recovery, crop water intensity, food security. No dedicated energy crops in waste-only biofuel base case.
5. `ECO01/SOC01`: Reef health, sediment/nutrient runoff, cultural stewardship, community-led approvals, affordability, ownership retention, displacement risk, quality of life, and GRV.

## Transfer gates
All inherited core gate concepts apply. Additional Maui-specific blocking vetoes:
- `M-ENV`: contamination, hazardous materials, air/sediment/reef impact, safe product leaching, lawful sampling
- `M-WATER`: stream flow/water rights/consumption and treatment quality, no dry-season service harm
- `M-LAND`: parcel ownership, public access, zoning, coastal hazard and cultural-heritage constraints
- `M-STRUCT`: independent structural/material certification, building code and coastal hazard suitability
- `M-COMMUNITY`: Native Hawaiian stewardship, meaningful local consent and benefit allocation

A status of UNKNOWN or UNRESOLVED blocks `LOCALITY_CLEARANCE`; it does **not** imply misconduct or failure. All blocks unresolved at initialization.
