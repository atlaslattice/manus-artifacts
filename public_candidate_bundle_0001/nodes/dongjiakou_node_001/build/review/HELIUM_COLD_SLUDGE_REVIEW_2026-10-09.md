## The proposed cascade needs a coupled design

The transcript moves from two separate project announcements to a confirmed series: helium → power/cooling → air separation → freezing → water → compute. That connection is **PROPOSED**, not established by the sources. Separate BOG feed does not establish absence of competition for refrigeration, electricity, heat exchange or terminal capacity. Neither project has been demonstrated here to provide a free residual cold budget for the added stages.

Model each cold consumer with inlet/outlet temperature, pressure, flow, duty, minimum approach temperature, parasitic power, availability and allocation rights. Reconcile mass, enthalpy and exergy balances across shared exchangers and operating cases. Separate the26GWh electricity forecast from8GWh avoided electricity; neither is a thermal duty that can be passed downstream unchanged. Air separation product temperatures and freeze-concentration duties require an explicit temperature-compatible arrangement, not merely a list of valuable outputs. Helium liquefaction is a separate refrigeration requirement; the PSA paper does not establish liquid-helium production.

Most importantly, **helium-bearing domestic liquefaction off-gas and imported LNG at a receiving terminal are not interchangeable feeds**. The2018 process paper describes volatile helium concentrating in upstream flash gas while the liquid product lacks it [S10]. That does not prove Qingdao has zero helium; it means a generic1% BOG assumption cannot be imported from another plant. Obtain terminal LNG/BOG assays, cargo history, upstream removal records and measured flow before any local recovery yield. Existing BOG may already serve fuel/compression/recondensation functions; do not assume it is routinely flared or unused.

## Closed-loop helium: inventory and credit discipline

Containment, purification and customer recovery are sound design objectives. Measured system loss, capture ratio and product purity are different metrics. Supplier-reported5N purity does not establish99% recovery, and neither establishes a system loss below1%. All such local targets remain proposed until measured over a stated boundary and period.

Maintain one conserved helium inventory across fresh feed, recovered stock, customer consumption, return streams, purge, vents, leaks and storage change. An internal return is a transfer, not new primary production. Recycling can provide another service cycle and demonstrably reduce fresh make-up demand, but the same quantity cannot repeatedly receive primary import-substitution credit. Compare measured fresh make-up against a matched counterfactual and preserve transfer IDs to avoid duplication. All credits remain zero in this review.

The quoted dollar/cubic-metre prices, scaled¥300–500million CAPEX, less-than-three-year payback, import shares and crisis statistics remain unverified. No delivered-price/product basis, site quotation, full operating cost or reliable cash-flow model was supplied. ## Residuals as resources: qualification before routing

Preserve the user's circular design aim as **seek a qualified destination for every residual stream**. Actual residuals retain UNKNOWN/HOLD/disposal routes until product and environmental receipts close; do not report achieved zero waste, zero pollution or net-positive discharge from a proposed destination.

Keep precipitated brine solids, municipal biosolids and industrial effluent sludge separate. In the brine study,25% binder replacement improved reactive-magnesia mixes under the stated curing conditions, while Portland-cement strength decreased; favorable experimental and environmental comparisons cannot be applied to every sludge or mix. A Dongjiakou route needs feed sampling, composition/speciation, contaminants and leaching, salt/chloride compatibility, relevant biological hazards, durability and mechanical testing, process emissions, lifecycle inputs and qualified offtake. Heavy metals are not removed from accountability merely by embedding them in a construction product. Failed material has a recorded managed route and associated cost.
