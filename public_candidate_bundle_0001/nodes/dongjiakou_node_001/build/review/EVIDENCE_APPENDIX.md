## Adaptation to any physically supportable biological resource need

Resource recovery is parameterized by demand/product basis, qualified feed inventory, moisture, allocation, delivery, acceptance, yield, constituent/stoichiometric ceiling, capacity, market and energy. The sulfur/gypsum example can be replaced by a biological or hybrid nutrient, protein or other resource route with the same evidence discipline. It is not a claim that every resource can be made biologically.

The eight module receipts cover yield, selectivity, energy, reagents, material life, residual fate, market ceiling and ecology. Eight loop receipts cover source, mass, quality, right to transfer, timing, displacement, residual fate and allocation. UNKNOWN propagates as null; a failed receipt blocks the route. A user-supplied PASS is not authenticated by the program. Conditional output remains simulated and realizes zero credits.
