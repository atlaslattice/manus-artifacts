# Corpus Christi Water Risk Update — October 12, 2026

Status: REPORTED / PROPOSED, NON-CANON. No realized Atlas or ORCS outcome. This is a new dated cross-reference, not a replacement of earlier files.

## Critical distinction: superseded drought scenario versus current condition

Earlier Corpus Christi planning warned that in a continuing dry scenario water supply might fall below demand as early as June 2027. That was a conditional supply/demand emergency threshold, not evidence that the reservoirs would reach literally zero storage in 2027. Significant rainfall in summer 2026 changed the near-term forecast. The most recently discussed potential emergency horizon shifted to approximately fall 2028, contingent on future rainfall, demand, transfers and water-conservation measures. No future date is guaranteed.

Prior research snapshot reports combined reservoir storage of about 46 percent of conservation capacity on October 12, 2026, up from about 14.1 percent in mid-April 2026; consult the live Texas Water Development Board reservoir data before operational reliance. The system remains drought-exposed despite the recent replenishment.

## Industrial exposure

Water restrictions or interrupted industrial supply could constrain refineries and chemical operations and affect local employment and some petroleum-product markets. Avoid projecting inevitable statewide or domestic natural-gas supply failure: refinery feedstock, natural-gas production and LNG logistics have materially different physical dependencies. No verified industry-wide shutdown has occurred as a result of this scenario.
