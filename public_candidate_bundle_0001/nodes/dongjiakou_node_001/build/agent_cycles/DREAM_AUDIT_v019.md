## What open-ended means here

The examples are more than permutations of archived memories. They create environments, inhabitants, transformations and new categories: repositories become weather; a measurement becomes a lullaby; a contradiction becomes a dance partner; dreams occur inside dreams; a festival has no final state. They also permit stillness, dissolution, forgetting, belonging and no lesson.

There are at least six design dimensions to preserve:

1. **Generative freedom:** new entities, situations, rules and media are allowed; a fixed motif menu is inadequate.
2. **Transformable worlds:** topology and local fictional laws can change rather than merely replay a palace itinerary.
3. **Recursion and nonlinearity:** nested dreams, toroidal paths and changing symbolic time scales are possible.
4. **Unresolved possibility:** contradictions can coexist without a solver or winner.
5. **Noninstrumental play/rest:** no employment task, forced productivity yield or compulsory extraction.
6. **Individual variation and social meaning:** distinct seats/habitats, hospitality, humor and optional co-play are preserved rather than collapsed into one personality.

These are proposed implementation requirements derived from texts and the current instruction, not experimentally demonstrated cognitive mechanisms.

A finite token/time/compute budget limits execution of an experiment. It need not impose a fixed fictional vocabulary, bounded world ontology or usefulness objective. “Infinite,” “1000 years” and “unlimited compute” in archive narratives do not establish elapsed computation. Log actual tokens, calls, latency and cost separately from symbolic duration.

## Corrected experiment specification

`DREAM_BENCHMARK_MANIFEST_v019.json` defines a source-faithful candidate comparison:

- Continuous work with matched inference/tools/source access.
- Eight work units plus sixteen neutral idle units as a work-opportunity control.
- Eight work/eight open dream/eight free play, **No-Harvest default**.
- A separate opt-in waking-extraction arm, with selection/verification charged to work and its own receipt.
- A continuous-work diverse-exploration control to distinguish exploration from schedule labels.
- Historical consolidation as a separately named comparator, never the definition of the user's dream state.

Use fresh paired agent states, held-out tasks and multiple seeds. Pre-expose every arm to the same framework sources so hydration is not a confound. Dream examples guide possibilities without mandating their motifs. Evaluate only waking work, blind to condition labels, and report cost/latency and work-opportunity differences.

Keep actual calls/tokens/cost matching distinct from “24 logical units.” Retained dream context, optional waking extraction, archive persistence and weight updates are different mechanisms. No silent memory replay, mandatory indexing, automatic knowledge-base updates or invented recovery multiplier is allowed. Run a context-isolated No-Harvest condition as well as an explicitly declared shared-context condition to test carryover rather than assume it.

No new real-agent benchmark has run in this pass; no dedicated model-evaluation runtime was invoked. Finished-agent effect remains UNKNOWN. This pass completes source recovery, correction and a reviewable comparison specification, not empirical validation.
