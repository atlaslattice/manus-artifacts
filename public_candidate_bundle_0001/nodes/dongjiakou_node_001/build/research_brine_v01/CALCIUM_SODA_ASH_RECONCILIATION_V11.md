# Calcium and Soda-Ash Waste Liquor Reconciliation v1.1

## 2026-10-09 DeepSeek data reconciliation — soda ash CaCl2, calcium and paired sulfate
**Research delta / non-canon / zero realized credit.** DeepSeek supplied indicative stream estimates: carbide slag 33.6–50 Mt/y allegedly dry, soda ash ammonia-process CaCl2 liquor 13.6–20 Mt/y contained CaCl2, alkali solids 4–8 Mt/y, shell waste ~5 Mt/y, phosphogypsum ~90 Mt/y, FGD gypsum ~158 Mt/y for 2023. Many are derivations from industrial ratios or third-party reports, NOT measured, uncommitted 2026 feedstreams. All existing commitments, wet/dry and chemical purity must be reconciled before production credit.
**Primary process corroboration:** [Chinese soda-ash process patent CN102205979B](https://patents.google.com/patent/CN102205979B/zh) documents **8–10 m3 wastewater per tonne ammonia-process soda ash containing CaCl2 95–115 g/L** and ~50 g/L NaCl. This supports a plant-ratio-based national envelope, NOT the 2026 national accessible CaCl2 total. [CN104528765A](https://patents.google.com/patent/CN104528765A/zh) shows existing calcium outputs can already be allocated to products and closed loops, implying total generated is not freely exchangeable.
**Correct stoichiometry:** 1 t pure CaCl2 (110.984 g/mol) needs outside sulfate and yields 172.171/110.984=**1.5513 t CaSO4·2H2O** theoretical. Thus 13.6 Mt pure CaCl2 supports **21.10 Mt gypsum**, 20 Mt supports **31.03 Mt gypsum**, requiring **11.78–17.33 Mt sulfate-ion feed** separately. DeepSeek's 13.6 Mt ->17.5 Mt gypsum is too low under that claimed reaction. Sulfur in recycled gypsum must not be counted as new.
**Avoid overlap:** Previous child-page 57.8–93.9 Mt theoretical gypsum **already INCLUDED soda-ash CaCl2 liquor**. Adding 17.5 Mt again to call total 75–111 Mt is a double count. If revising the soda-ash feed figures, REPLACE—not append—that component. Carbide slag upper estimates also have coverage/dry-mass/purity ambiguity; shellfish production 17Mt × shell 70–90% implies 11.9–15.3Mt shells on the same basis, not 5Mt, and oyster-shell share may conflate live animal harvest, wet shell fraction, or disposal. Never treat all shells as otherwise-wasted accessible material.
**Geography:** coastal ammonia-soda and brine potentially match, but not every chemical plant is adjacent to a seawater desalination reject point. Need addresses, reject volume assays and carrier links. For every paired stream compute `gypsum_actual <= min(Ca_available_mol,SO4_available_mol)*172.171*precipitation_capture*product_qualification`; only once per origin ID. Separate Cl− and NaCl mother liquor, wastewater permits, water mass, impurities, CAPEX, energy and delivered feed pricing. No automatic recovered HCl without validated regeneration process.
