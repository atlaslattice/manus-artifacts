# Dongjiakou resilience and delivered-acid comparator v1.6
**2026-10-09 | SYNTHETIC / NON-CANON / ZERO REALIZED OUTPUT, GRV, QOL, EXPORT OR FINANCIAL CREDIT.**
## Objective
Screen marginal qualified H2SO4 produced from desalination sulfate precipitated using soda-ash CaCl2 liquor against alternate delivered smelter acid or sulfur-burning acid, under normal/disrupted market purchase costs. Not a demonstration of qualified acid, plant availability, actual future prices, commercial resilience service, or market shortage.

See `djk_resilience_model_v16.py` plus `djk_resilience_assumptions_v16.json` on this research branch. Downloadable XLSX workbook and 48-scenario CSV built in the conversation, not yet in GitHub. Prior snapshots v1.5 and previous papers preserved. Latest v1.5 market evidence sampled Shandong May sulfur-burner use 64.05% → late June32.33% and July 36.02%; sampled utilization NOT purchase availability probability. **The model's shortage availability factors are explicitly synthetic and cannot be inferred from utilization.**
## Base model inputs: synthetic, not measured
Brine 150,000m3/day; brine operational factor 75%; dissolved sulfate 4.5g/L; capture 35%; soda-ash 300,000m3 annual CaCl2 liquor at 95kg/m3; available calcium precipitation 80%; gypsum product qualification 90%; qualified acid after gypsum conversion 0.4t/t and conversion availability80%. CaCl2 waste liquor delivered ¥90/m3; precipitation ¥85 per t gypsum; acid conversion ¥700 per t pure acid; finishing ¥135/t; fixed O&M ¥4m/y; installed incremental capex ¥160m; financing comparison discount10% over 15years. Normal delivered alternative smelter ¥1050/t, sulfur-burning ¥1350/t; disruption smelter ¥1950/t, sulfur-burning ¥2700/t; synthetic 80% normal/20% disruption weighted purchase cost. These are **test assumptions**, NOT current or contracted/verified quotes. User's physical actual DJK assays not received.
## Corrected mass balances and initial result
150000×365×0.75×4.5/1000 = **184,781t/y sulfate ion in concentrate fixture**, capture 35%=64,673t/y. Sulfate-limited gypsum ~115,916t/y; soda-ash liquor contained CaCl2 28,500t/y, ×80% availability and CaCl2-to-gypsum molar ratio172.171/110.984 gives calcium-limited gypsum ~35,370t/y, then ×90% product qualification = **31,833t/y gypsum**. ×0.4t pure acid/t gypsum ×80% conversion availability = **10,187t/y qualified hypothetical acid**, ~3,330t/y elemental sulfur. **CALCIUM-LIMITED**, not sulfate-limited.
Cash annual operating cost **¥42.21m**, ¥4,144/t acid; annualized capital adds ~¥2,065/t; full levelized **¥6,209/t acid**. Baseline 80/20 normal/disruption delivered smelter replacement cost = ¥1,230/t, so expected scenario NPV ~**−¥385.76m** on 15y 10% pretax annuity. Under these deliberately conservative and costly assumptions, route is far from competitive; no project approval. Lower liquor cost and conversion cost alone may not solve yield/capital bottleneck.
Commercial accounting uses forgone external delivered acid prices as **avoided expenditure**, not chemical output sales plus avoided procurement for the same ton; capital scope, electricity, byproduct handling and regulatory permitting need full EPC review, and no guaranteed dual benefit. Waste-to-acid is not proven by chemical theoretical mass alone.
## Next model gate / engineering design
1. Confirm actual acceptable CaCl2-bearing waste liquor, annual uncommitted volume and price, grade, trace impurity, wastewater ownership/permit, logistics, chloride mother liquor.
2. Confirm physical brine flow and sulfate assay, antiscalant sensitivity, calcite/gypsum crystallization, wash water, product COA and offsite gypsum acceptance.
3. Obtain actual gypsum-to-acid conversion feed tests and plant thermal energy/CO2/contaminant controls plus signed installed CAPEX/OPEX estimates.
4. Compare low-throughput standalone acid conversion **versus sending gypsum to existing commissioned conversion partner** and direct existing acid purchased; alternative CaCl2 product and shell routes separate.
5. Obtain Shandong regional grade-specific firm offtake and delivered smelter acid shipping quotes under normal and disruptions. Quantify demonstrated unmet orders, don't infer from posted sulfur-burning prices.
6. Independent full public benefit of firm supply during real outage with probabilities and displacement; track GRV / resilience / QOL separately. No realized value until receipts.
## Alternative procurement constraint
A modelled reliability shortfall of 35% in disruption is not derivable from a 67% sampled utilization. Utilization measures plant production relative to capacity, not probability of delivered acid. Do not label such assumption OBSERVED.
