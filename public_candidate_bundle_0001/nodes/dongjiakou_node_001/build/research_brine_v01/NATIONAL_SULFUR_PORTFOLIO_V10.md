## Major category and accounting corrections
- China's 167 desalination **projects** (end-2025) are not 167 Atlas Lattice-deployed nodes. Ministry of Natural Resources 2025 Seawater Utilization Report gives combined **3,076,805 t/day *freshwater production design capacity***, not measured brine discharge. Province: Shandong964239, Zhejiang823906, Hebei490700, Tianjin456000, Liaoning161984, Guangdong98016, Jiangsu41510, Fujian29950, Hainan9750, Guangxi750. Top four 88.886% of freshwater capacity; not sulfur recovery production.
- Earlier 167 identical DJK clones require 25.05million m3/day brine nominal, physically unjustified compared with 3.08million t/day national freshwater design capacity. Reject concentrate ratios vary by membrane recovery, intake, thermal vs RO, pretreatment and industrial uptime.
- A calcium atom is not a sulfur atom. **Carbide slag, shells, CaCl2, lime are calcium reagents**, not sulfuric-acid sulfur feedstocks; their sulfur contribution is ZERO unless analyzed. **Phosphogypsum and FGD gypsum** are sulfate-sulfur bearing, but sulfur may be sourced from acid formerly purchased/imported or fossil-fuel sulfur already captured. Recovered acid credits only for net fresh sulfur feed displaced after sulfur provenance and competing uses. Their aggregate gypsum tonnages should never be added to acid totals without CaSO4·2H2O purity, accessibility, current usage, impurity and plant-scale conversion qualification. Phosphogypsum Ca fraction is ~23.28% on dry CaSO4·2H2O, elemental sulfur ~18.62%, maximum 0.56967t pure acid/t pure gypsum. At assumed 90Mt dry 100% pure gypsum national upper bound ~51.27Mt pure acid, not 32Mt *automatically*. 75Mt similarly ~42.72Mt upper bound. These are extreme ceilings, not output.
- DeepSeek reported 'carbide slag ~25-30Mt CaO = 40-50Mt sulfuric acid' is not defensible without another explicitly quantified sulfate/sulfur source. 'Oyster shells ~3Mt acid' also assumes an external sulfur source. FGD ~27Mt acid figure is not a defensible fully loaded or accessible recovery forecast.
- Brine calcium and sulfate concentrations 680mg/L and 4500mg/L in DJK fixture are not national measurements. Equilibrium, antiscalants and capture unknown; cannot infer feasibility from sulfur availability.
## Executed capacity-weighted sensitivity
Script `national_sulfate_portfolio_v10.py`, CSV `national_sulfate_portfolio_v10.csv` use provincial design-capacity data, implied reject-brine ratios and assumptions, independent by province. Three exploratory bundles all at 75% use factor:
1. Constrained: brine/feedwater ratio 0.5, sulfate 2.7g/L, sulfate recovery 10%, gypsum-to-acid sulfur conversion 40% -> ~46,439t pure acid/y, ~15,182t elemental S.
2. Intermediate: ratio 1.0, sulfate 4.5g/L, recovery 35%, conversion 70% -> ~948,126t pure acid/y, ~309,971t elemental S.
3. Expansive: ratio 1.5, sulfate 4.5g/L, recovery 70%, conversion 70% -> ~2,844,379t pure acid/y, ~929,914t elemental S.
Ratios to produced freshwater are imposed not water engineering estimates, and 2.7/4.5g/L sulfate are ASSUMED, not fleet assays. Different technologies have different reject rates. These cases are possible parameter arithmetic, not probabilities, production plan, or guaranteed economic recoverability. Total current operational gypsum/acid production by Atlas = 0.
## National portfolio classification and gate logic
(1) **NEW sulfur potentially recovered:** previously discharged dissolved brine sulfate, non-committed flue-gas sulfur, other independently proven waste sulfur; (2) **RECYCLED sulfur:** phosphogypsum, spent H2SO4, FGD gypsum where prior feedstocks already included; (3) **calcium-only support:** shell, carbide slag, other lime, CaCl2; (4) **not available:** already used feedstock, environmental exclusion, transported uneconomically, incompatible impurity or no conversion hub.
Every sulfur stream must have unique origin ID and grade, mass sulfur in vs recovered vs product vs tailings, counterfactual fate, actual annual accessible mass, existing commitments, full incremental CO2/emissions, energy and freight. Do NOT sum resource classifications as independent sources if they refer to the same sulfur atoms at different process stages. Inventory stock ≠ annual accessible flow.
Formal sustainability constraints: status receipt; stream offtake; chemistry measured; water law; kiln feed COA; local demand and reserves; ecological/soil factors; energy and GHG impact; at-cost transfer internally eliminating consolidated transfer revenue; same-commodity import replacement and government export authority.
