### Reactor claims evidence audit
Quorn reports continuous **air-lift fermenters ~40m tall, 5-week continuous runs**, using maize/wheat glucose + ammonium + phosphate, monitored oxygen, pH and temp: https://www.quornnutrition.com/mycoprotein/what-is-mycoprotein. Peer reviewed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11954786/ and 2023 TEA https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/sustainable-food-systems/articles/10.3389/fsufs.2023.1204307/full .
Nosh Bio confirms its food-grade infrastructure retrofit strategy (but **Koji Protein different organism/process**): https://www.nosh.food/technology. Third-party reports 50–70% lower CAPEX for a Nosh brewery retrofit: https://foodtechforesight.com/reducing-fermentation-costs-by-retrofitting-brewery-vessels-nosh-bios-innovations ; NOT independently verified retrofit savings at 155m³ Quorn fermenters or 2t/h installed systems. Different oxygenation and strain could erase projected savings; do not multiply 2023 $108m reference factory by 0.3–0.5 as a bankable plant price. 2–10L laboratory reactor quotations not suitable scale analog.
SSF may reduce some fermenter investment and is used in Asian edible fermented foods, but does **not** automatically produce interchangeable high-protein Quorn-style biomass at the same continuous-process tonnage or measured 12% wet protein: https://doi.org/10.1146/ANNUREV-FOOD-111523-121802 . Separate SSF product specification, contamination, process heat/oxygen, RNA management, solids handling, yield and Chinese food regulation. **Fermentation route choice is an engineering qualification gate**, not only lowest quoted vessel price.
