## Scale screen from reported phosphogypsum
DeepSeek estimates 90Mt/y phosphogypsum generated, utilization 66.7%, implying **29.97Mt potentially unused gypsum** on a simple annual arithmetic basis. Assumes 100% pure dry CaSO4·2H2O and NO prior offtake: theoretical acid mass ratio 98.079/172.171=0.56966, S ratio32.065/172.171=0.186239. Any real feed will require certified dry purity, industrial acceptance, net recovery, energy, capex, off-gas, emissions, competing uses and economic capacity. Combined accessible+conversion share within this hypothetical unused fraction of **10% ⇒ 1.71Mt pure H2SO4/y (0.558Mt sulfur)**; 20% ⇒3.41Mt acid (1.116Mt S); 30% ⇒5.12Mt acid (1.674Mt S). Historic 4.649Mt acid exports imply **1.52Mt elemental S**, equivalent to ~27.2% of the *unused* 29.97Mt hypothetical gypsum fully acid-regenerated on pure chemistry. This is an order-of-magnitude comparison **not proof of new acid export surplus**. Every phosphogypsum sulfate atom already came from earlier sulfuric acid. Only NET imported fresh feed displaced counts towards sulfur self-sufficiency; regenerator recycled acid may or may not be incremental to baseline supply. **Soda liquor CaCl2 11.5-14.2Mt, carbide slag 33.6Mt, shell 9.87Mt do not themselves supply S**; they may capture otherwise uncaptured sulfate separately. FGD gypsum 100-120Mt is another potential recycled sulfur stream, but its actual available/unallocated fraction and acid plant compatibility are unknown. Do not sum hypothetical pathways without origin-ID deduplication.

## Explicit distance-to-surplus formula
For each matched acid grade and region, `G=max(0,domestic_dependable_requirement+reserve_refill+normal_losses-existing_dependable_domestic_acid_supply)`. Fresh sulfur feedstock shortfall must be modeled in elemental-S equivalents, preventing using recovered sulfate twice. For scenario incremental qualified acid X, `physical_margin=X-G` only after reliable grade, location, acid plant offtake and sulfur origin audits. **Verified surplus and export legality require national certification and competent government authorization, not just mathematical X>G.** National G is currently UNKNOWN; actual Atlas acid produced=0; existing export restrictions should not be construed as a universal legal ban with confirmed end date.
