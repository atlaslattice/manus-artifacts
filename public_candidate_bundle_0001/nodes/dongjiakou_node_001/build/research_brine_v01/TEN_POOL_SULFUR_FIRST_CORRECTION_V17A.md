## v1.7 correction — sulfur-first, no discretionary gypsum diversion (2026-10-09)

Agricultural gypsum is an optional secondary product ONLY on separately justified surplus/rejected-but-soil-qualified material after acid demand is met. Never assume rejects are agricultural-safe. Gypsum production itself remains potentially calcium- and sulfate-limited; correct distinction is feedstock limitation versus downstream converter capacity. No claim that scaling the hub is feasible or cost-effective absent EPC and process receipts. Grain can cohabit through nearby farming, water, heat, and fertilizer supply without consuming acid-designated gypsum.
