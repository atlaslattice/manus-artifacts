# QOL / Eden — first-class outcome standard v0.1

STATUS: REVIEW PUBLIC CANDIDATE | CANON: no | DEPLOYMENT: no | AUTHORITY: none | PROOF: no | PUBLIC_RELEASE: candidate

Date: 2026-10-08 America/Chicago (2026-10-09 UTC). This additive specification extends the published Manus-derived simulator. It makes human outcomes and ecological condition explicit review objectives. It does not commission facilities, certify a standard, establish a health intervention or award an ecological credit.

## Integration

QOL01 is a `FUNCTIONAL_AGGREGATE`: an outcome, access and stewardship register, not a factory process. The review snapshot adds it as a twentieth boundary unit (nineteen internal plus EXT01). The existing twenty-three material streams and five reported physical streams stay intact. ECO01/P01/E02/C01/W01 each have a separate proposed observation/service link to QOL01. These five links do not count as material transfers or realized benefits.

`python simulator.py qol` emits the default human/ecological outcome vector and missing receipts. `python simulator.py current` includes that vector with the locality graph. `python simulator.py gap ...` includes it alongside resource-gap arithmetic. Resource production alone can therefore never stand in for human benefit. This is an evaluation/reporting extension, not a solved multi-objective optimizer.

There is no composite QOL number. The thirty-two indicators retain their domain, unit, direction, evidence, claim dimension and cohort. Six default strata are residents, workers, migrant workers, shift workers, disabled people and low-income households. These groups overlap and must not be added into a population total. Denominators and local baseline are UNKNOWN. A complete comparison requires all declared metric/cohort pairs; the default requires192 pairs. An absent record is null, not zero.

## What is measured

| Domain | Indicators | Required distinction |
|---|---|---|
| Nature/access |Green area, green space per person,800m and10-minute walking access |Gross greening is not safe public access, habitat recovery or a health effect. Survey routes, barriers, opening hours and population boundaries. |
| Air |PM2.5, PM10, NO2 and SO2 |Use stated averaging windows, calibrated monitors and receptor locations. Ambient concentration is not an attributed health outcome. |
| Noise/heat |Day/night LAeq, heat-stress exposure hours, heat-island delta |Define day/night periods, reference weather, heat protocol and population/receptor coverage; do not infer cooling from installed plants. |
| Water/food |Safe blue-space and drinking-water access, affordable nutritious food, cold-chain compliance |A water feature is not safe contact water; storage capacity is not food security. Product/contact quality requires separate receipts. |
| Housing/work |Housing cost burden, crowding, injury rate, secure decent-work access |Do not confuse gross floor area with usable space or a design aspiration with a medical minimum. Worker dignity/safety cannot be offset by output. |
| Community/accessibility |Step-free public access; culture/education access |Measure actual affordable, accessible use and service hours, not only buildings. Tourism/education remain hypotheses until public benefit is measured. |
| Experience |Restorativeness/place experience; life satisfaction, worthwhile activity, happiness and anxiety as separate items |Local translation, cognitive testing, voluntary survey, nonresponse and matched instrument are required. Aesthetic experience is not productivity, clinical health or ecological credit. |
| Ecology |Native species richness, protocol-defined habitat condition and receiving-water quality |Species counts alone are not biodiversity integrity; carbon density is not ecosystem welfare. No net-positive claim from absence of measured harm. |
| Stewardship |Maintenance cost and irrigation load |Lower cost/water is a screening direction, not permission to under-maintain a public asset. Measure staffing, replacement, runoff and lifecycle obligations. |

Registry directions are transparent screening conventions, not universal optima. One-time direction checks do not identify a desirable maximum or minimum. The two0–1 condition indicators require a locally specified protocol; their units are placeholders for normalized protocol outputs, not validated universal indices.

## Non-compensable safeguards

Six QOL gates cover worker safety, ecological protection, privacy/consent, access/non-displacement, water/product safety, and maintenance/lifecycle. Any FAIL blocks the QOL proposal; UNKNOWN holds it. A measured cohort decline also holds review even if another cohort improves. All six existing locality vetoes remain separately unresolved, including INV-19 and INV-20. A supplied QOL gate PASS cannot clear a locality veto.

The engine rejects unknown-as-zero, inverted intervals, product/unit confusion, duplicate metric/cohort records, mismatched local boundary/period and unexpected personal fields. Baseline/followup protocol identities must match for an attributed-change label. Reference and synthetic inputs remain reference/scenario only. An uncontrolled before/after series may show change but cannot establish node-attributed improvement.

The engine does not authenticate receipts, audit study design, perform statistical causal inference or certify survey validity. Even complete supplied inputs only produce `ELIGIBLE_FOR_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_ONLY`; locality clearance stays `NOT_ESTABLISHED` and realized credit stays zero. Interval changes are endpoint envelopes, not automatically confidence intervals.

## Eden / beautification pilot

Recover the existing research instead of rebuilding it: [Living Well synthesis](../../../../china_research/notion_archive/072-3f20c1de73d98132893ae61d44225d38.md), [QOL measurement register](../../../../china_research/notion_archive/082-3f20c1de73d981ed9ddbdc2cb7634475.md), [northern coastal greening](../../../../china_research/notion_archive/112-3f20c1de73d981269657e5179d2d07e5.md). These are archived research inputs, not reviewed local performance receipts.

Candidate pilot: flowering and locally adapted coastal landscape cells, sheltered walking/rest areas and accessible public spaces, with a conventional-treatment and no-greening comparison. Verify plant provenance, salt spray, wind, dust, contaminated soils, irrigation chemistry, runoff, structural/corrosion interactions and invasive risk before selecting species or locations. Establish baseline and preregister outcome/stop rules before construction. Track a complete seasonal cycle, survival/replacement, actual use, perceived restorativeness, maintenance labor and water/cost burden. Ecological outcomes require separate local assessment.

Use reclaimed water only after fit-for-purpose quality and legal/site permissions are established. Do not presume that W01's contracted water is available for irrigation, that a cooling pond is safe for public contact or that E02 cooling is allocated to public thermal comfort. The baseline can win; stop or redesign a pilot that increases burden or fails safeguards.

C01 may help summarize environmental measurements and flag missing data. No individualized workplace scoring, diagnosis, policing or automated eligibility decisions are introduced. The public interface accepts aggregate records only; no names, precise personal location or individual medical fields. The minimum public cohort size of10 is a project safeguard, not a claimed statutory threshold. Cohort size alone is not an anonymization guarantee. Retain raw research data in a separately governed environment; obtain qualified local review of permissions, retention, access and sensitive-data handling before field collection.

## Resource-portfolio correction

See [machine-readable portfolio review](RESOURCE_PORTFOLIO_REVIEW.json). Import percentages, price jumps and export restrictions in the new DeepSeek handoff remain unverified and do not enter demand/supply inputs.

Iodine is a feed-assay-gated candidate: seawater concentrate must not inherit oilfield-brine concentrations or a demonstrated yield from another chemistry. Helium likewise requires actual recoverable feed. [Natural-gas process research](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0360544218307308) separates helium into upstream flash/NRU streams during liquefaction; [an LNG-cold-assisted PSA study](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acs.iecr.5c03057) supports a process possibility, not a Dongjiakou bolt-on installation. Cold energy is not a source of helium. Obtain LNG/BOG composition, upstream removal history, flow, contractual cold allocation and process/energy receipts.

Struvite can recycle P in a qualified feed. It does not turn every tonne of recovered P into a tonne of substitutable phosphate rock or battery-grade phosphate. Cobalt/chromium/copper are not biologically created; qualified scrap may support circular or bio-assisted recovery, while missing-feed lanes use substitution, diversification and reserves. “No biological creation”does not mean “no circular pathway.” Classify feed/process maturity separately from strategic dependence.
