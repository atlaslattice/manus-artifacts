# RES07 — Lahaina two-networked-system field correction

**Date:** 2026-10-10  
**Classification:** USER-REPORTED FIELD TESTIMONY · NON-CANON · ZERO REALIZED DONGJIAKOU CREDIT

The user's firsthand correction states that **two networked solar-battery systems** sustained limited essential relief loads during the 2023 Lahaina disaster, with batteries **continuously replenished by solar**. Operating essential loads throughout the outage establishes practical independence from the failed external supply during the period described. The topology is **not specified**: systems might be permanently off-grid or grid-connected with suitable islanding/backup capability. Do not equate "operated islanded" with "permanently off-grid".

The interconnection between the two systems is reported, but their inverter arrangement, energy-sharing mechanism, common protection, black-start capability, islanding switches, capacity, and N-1 redundancy remain unverified. Networking does not by itself prove either cross-charging or fault tolerance; model those as testable capabilities.

**Engineering implications:** Compare a single PV+BESS system with two interconnected energy nodes under matched total capacity, independently islanded nodes with selectable intertie, and a common-bus alternative; test common-cause failures, isolation and protection, communication loss, partial PV damage, excess PV transfer, load prioritization, emergency connection interfaces, and reserve-replenishment time. Continuous generation and controlled essential demand are the mechanism behind sustained service; neither perpetual operation through arbitrary poor weather nor realized Dongjiakou performance is claimed.
