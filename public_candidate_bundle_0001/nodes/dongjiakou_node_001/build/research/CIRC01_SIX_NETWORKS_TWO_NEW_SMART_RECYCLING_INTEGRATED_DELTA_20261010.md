## NEW CIRC01 — Intelligent Community Recovery Network
**Proposed**, not an existing installation: each building/neighborhood has accessible trained-operated collection points (no unverified AI recognition performance); neighborhood transfer nodes preserve chain of custody, weights and contamination classes; contracted sorting/reuse/repair centers recover materials, with qualified outputs only to licensed industrial processors. Design data schema for point_id, service population, collection_accessibility, stream_code, input_mass_kg, sorted_mass_kg, contamination_kg, verified_downstream_kg, recovered_reused_kg, reject_mass_kg, energy_kwh, maintenance_hours, cost_rmb, staff_fte, collection_trip_km, output_legal_license, feedstock_buyer_contract, hazardous handling rules, source and timestamps. Every mass stream obeys balance including storage change; no **zero waste** claim without proof. Waste hierarchy: reduction → reuse/refill/repair → recycling → separately justified recovery/disposal. Protect material quality; separate batteries/chemicals/e-waste and avoid unsafe mixed processing.

Possible household/retail systems: reuse centers, repair cooperatives, smart bins/weight capture and route optimization under privacy safeguards, commercial backhaul, community compost only with permitted contamination controls, EPS/PET and C&D sorted flows only with verified local volume. Landlord-funded QOL professional maintenance teams could maintain points and grounds but municipal waste operator/contractor responsibilities remain separately defined. Link relief and disaster clearance pathways to MAU-W01 (excluding contaminated wildfire debris as ordinary recyclable feedstock).
