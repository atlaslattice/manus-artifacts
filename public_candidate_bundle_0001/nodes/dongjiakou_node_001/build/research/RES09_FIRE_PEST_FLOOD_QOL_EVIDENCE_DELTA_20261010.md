## 1. Dependency-graph method
Represent physical implementation dependencies separately from functional service-effect dependencies. Nodes include generation, dispatch, batteries, communications, safe water, drainage, cold-chain, critical cooling, medicine, roads/transport, emergency personnel and industrial processes. The directed graph **may contain cycles**. Compute strongly connected components and flag cycles without a validated restart pathway; do not reject every directed cycle as a design failure. Test failures of power, telecom and water alongside alternate black-start, manual procedures and storage. Keep Tier T0–T4 hierarchy as initial depiction, not a universal DAG constraint. Formalize an independent RES09 service dependency/recovery graph or submodule linked to RES06 human coordination and RES08 hazards. Provenance: DeepSeek candidate architecture; supported in principle by One Earth 2024 service-disruption cascade research https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2590332224001398 .

## 2. Flood/dike and sponge works
Qingdao West Coast New Area *modern water network plan* sets Dongjiakou port city target **50-year flood protection**, **20-year urban waterlogging/drainage prevention**, and general coastal protection of 50-year and important segments 100-year. Source: https://www.xihaian.gov.cn/zwgk/gknr/zfwj/zcxwj/qwj/202309/t20230901_7445844.shtml . Planning standard is not proof installed system meets it. Fuqing Bìtóu River project government update 2026-06-23 confirms **3,259m** river-channel length, four gate openings, 100-year tide and 20-year drainage design; canal substantially complete, sluice construction *still advancing*, so not globally mark fully operational: https://slj.fuzhou.gov.cn/zwgk/gzdt/202606/t20260623_5336934.htm . DeepSeek also reports Dongjiakou cold-chain logistics embankment 2601m and ¥1.37bn and Qingdao sponge city 311km², Suqian 59.1% etc; these require originating issuance, unit and as-of construction stage before promotion. Drainage infrastructure may rely on electricity, controls, telemetry and access; combine defense standards with failure scenarios and rain+surge correlated events.

## 3. Acoustic-assisted BESS fire protection
Suzhou Industrial Park governmental site documents Jiangsu Guanghua acoustic **nitrogen-assisted two-fluid fine water mist** in the Lianyungang Energy Group 150MW/300MWh storage installation connected 2024-11-30. Ultrasound atomizes water into reported droplets **<50 micrometers**; energy absorption and water/nitrogen suppression are physical mechanisms. NOT evidence of sound-only or universal ultrasonic fire extinguishing. Government source: https://www.sipac.gov.cn/kjzszx/xwzx/202412/ed27f6227cdf4cb3902f6dca61006624.shtml ; manufacturer corroboration https://en.yotaienergy.com/news/84.html . Claims of extinguishing performance require independent full-scale tests and thermal-runaway/flammable-gas analysis. For US comparisons consider NFPA 855/UL 9540A, but apply local Chinese codes and authority approval at Dongjiakou. Do not place emerging technology throughout residences absent certification, tested performance, occupant safety and fire authority approval. Standard active protection remains foundational.

## 4. Acoustic pest protection and locust forecast
A 2024 Indonesian rice bird/locust repellent study reports **40kHz** and **3-meter observed effective range**, but this is a narrow experiment, not proven cost-effective scalable pest control or universal optimum. https://garuda.kemdiktisaintek.go.id/documents/detail/4716392 . Earlier research finds high-frequency bird deterrents often ineffective as most birds cannot hear ultrasound: https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/vpc15/26/ . Do NOT bulk purchase devices based on single small study. Limited controlled pilots may compare physical exclusion, habitat management, monitoring, ecological integrated pest management, wildlife impacts and return on investment. China's 2026 national locust forecast estimates East Asian migratory locust incidence 6.5m mu mainly Dongying/Binzhou, not a confirmed Dongjiakou infestation: https://www.agricoop.cn/news/show.php?itemid=30856 . Couple flood/drought breeding-habitat risks to later agricultural supply-chain disruption as conditional research hypothesis rather than deterministic event.

## 5. QOL professional maintenance invariant
The user's proposal: make fire-safety inspections, building maintenance, hygiene/disinfection and integrated pest management a professionally supported baseline for residences, commerce, industrial and community zones; avoid assigning unrealistic maintenance burdens to residents. Design safe, environmentally responsible site care; tier risk inspections, hazard monitoring, scheduled maintenance, training, union/labor conditions, staffing rosters and employment effects. Hygiene is **risk-based cleaning**, not indiscriminate disinfectant use; maintain ventilation/chemical exposure protection. Add budget scenarios (existing QOL maintenance allocations, emergency preparedness appropriations, service fees, voluntary or jurisdiction-authorized payroll levy). Payroll tax is only a PROPOSED financing option, not authorized policy; stress-test regressivity, administrative cost, labor impact, distribution fairness, democratic/local approvals.
