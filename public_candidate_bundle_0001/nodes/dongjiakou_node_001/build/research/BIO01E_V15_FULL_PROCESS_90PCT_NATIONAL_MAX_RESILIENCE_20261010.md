## Complete biochemical process and constraints
1. Fresh edible food prevention/rescue separate from spent food waste; legal and hygienic traceable supply chain.
2. Qualify sources, contracts and assays: wet/dry mass, C, fermentable carbs, N/P/S/K/Ca/Mg, impurities, heavy metals, pathogens, salts, plastics, contaminants and treatment boundaries.
3. Mass-closed separation (a) veg/grain/starch/approved processing byproduct carbohydrate hydrolysis and glucose equivalence; (b) proteins/digestate approved N recovery; (c) P/struvite and nutrients purified to food-medium specs, or agriculture-only output; (d) residual AD/biogas and pathogen-controlled certified compost, agronomic soils; no duplicated elemental outputs.
4. Optional captured CO2 energy-intensive conversion to *qualified* usable substrate; *Fusarium venenatum* A3/5 is heterotrophic, no direct CO2 feed. 1 tonne glucose-equivalent carbon theoretical >=1.4667 tonnes CO2, electricity 12 MWh/t glucose is **unverified parameter** not industrial performance.

Medium airlift fermented culture *F. venenatum* A3/5, aerobic oxygen and cooling, trace minerals/water, sterile food environment, RNA reduction, separation, food safety and China approval; product protein and digestibility independently assay. Unity Gel nutritional integration only after qualified food product.
6. Energy, food water, C/N/P/S/K, culture waste, CO2 emissions, AD output, digestate, compost, price, transport and plant capital operational ledger. No sulfuric acid or gypsum savings unless measured.

## Self-sufficiency / resilience decision objective
Prior [DJK acid procurement vs recovery v1.6](https://app.notion.com/p/3f40c1de73d981669af3d4078b4b8cac) found a synthetic gypsum/brine-to-acid pathway economically inferior to market procurement in base conditions (~¥6209/t levelized vs weighted smelter acid ~¥1230/t); resiliency benefit must therefore be **separately measured**, not dismissed nor automatically monetized. For every recovered N, P, carbon substrate, energy and finished protein compare (A) normal-time NPV, (B) probability-weighted disrupted service, (C) 7/30/90d autonomy, (D) import exposure, diversity, storage and downtime penalties, (E) lifecycle emissions and externalities. Match process purity and contractual grades before calculating protected supply. No realized self-sufficiency or avoided loss credit.
