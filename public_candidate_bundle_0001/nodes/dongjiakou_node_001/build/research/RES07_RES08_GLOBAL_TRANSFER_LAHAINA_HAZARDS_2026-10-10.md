**User's Lahaina field correction:** TWO networked solar-battery systems continuously replenished essential services during weeks of disaster response. User reports operation continued after their departure until grid restoration. Electric topology and capacity have not been measured by this study. **Resilience objective:** Compare 168-hour essential-service continuity with conditional ongoing autonomy beyond seven days when independently available solar, wind, qualified hydro or other power replenishes demand and storage over modeled weather windows. Indefinite service cannot be guaranteed through indefinite simultaneous generation failure. Design against catastrophic and common-cause supply outages; report repeated-outage reserve recovery.

**Global reusable schema:** REGION_PROFILE (AOI, CRS, hazard layer version/resolution, source and reuse rights), HAZARD_SET (wind, flood, surge, seismic, wildfire, hazardous industry), ASSET_GRAPH (islands, generation, load, transport, water, comms), EMERGENCY_SERVICE (must-serve/minimum service, MW, dependency), GENERATION_PROFILE (hourly weather, hydro, curtailment), STORAGE_PROFILE (power, usable energy, SOC, losses, blackstart), SCENARIO (outage duration, geographic impact radius, hazard intensity, correlated failures, repairs), and OUTPUTS (protected services, unserved MWh, hours, 168h threshold, resource replenishment, reserves recovered, regional coverage). Blanket radius coverage is a screening/service planning device; do not conflate with physically uniform hazard intensity.
