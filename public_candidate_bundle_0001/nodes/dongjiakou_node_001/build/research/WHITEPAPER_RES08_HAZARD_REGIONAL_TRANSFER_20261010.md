# RES08 White Paper — Dongjiakou Hazard Data and Portable Regional Simulators
NON-CANON · DATA INTEGRATION RESEARCH · ZERO REALIZED CREDIT

## Basis and accessibility
First National Comprehensive Natural Disaster Risk Survey: six types/23 hazards; four-level zoning; 2,846 counties and >1.7 billion recorded results per China MEM (https://www.mem.gov.cn/xw/xwfbh/2024n05y08rxwfbh/wzsl_4260/202405/t20240508_487313.shtml). Surveys are authoritative, yet a reported county/site analysis is not the same as every underlying GIS layer being publicly available or redistributable. Record citation, issuing body, scale, vintage, license and certainty.

## Specific Dongjiakou figures
Port EIA supports highest observed high tide 5.19m and extreme DESIGN water level 5.91m relative to local theoretical lowest tidal datum (NOT mean sea level or storm surge increment): https://www.mee.gov.cn/ywdt/gsgg/gongshi/wqgs_1/201808/W020180824572868425776.pdf . Other candidate values supplied by DeepSeek require precise originating document/geography: 0.10g/VII seismic, third design group, 0.45s period and potentially liquefiable silty soil; seven typhoons/50yr and reported 1985/2022 events, 16.1 high-wind days/year, 8 rainstorm days/year, 28 West Coast geology points; chemical park six domains/13 metrics and water control 9 areas/2 ditches/5 channels/1 river/13 sluices. The published 0.98 Qingdao coastal inundation index applies to Dagu River estuary, not directly port, whereas a 2026 coastal study does describe exposed reclaimed Dongjiakou industrial shoreline.

## Compound failure
Coastal storm can disable PV, substation, telecom and roads together. Chemical containment may require grid power/controls; earthquake can affect soil/foundation and water utilities. Geographic separation helps only to the extent faults are not correlated. Coverage catchments (illustrative 5,20,50,100km) describe communities the node aims to serve, not a blanket disaster-physics damage radius. Use rasterized hazard intensity when available; synthetic radius stress-tests are labeled as such.

## Globally forkable core
Regional adapters supply polygons/CRS/terrain vertical datum, hazard intensity and return period, asset graph, critical-load service priorities, hourly generation/weather, storage specs, transportation/repair assets, regulatory rules and public-license provenance. Maui may use NOAA, FEMA, USGS, Hawaii/Maui hazard plans; Dongjiakou uses MEM, port EIAs, government GIS and meteorology. Common kernel runs identical seven-day continuity and replenishment tests with local layers swapped. Never expose private relief installation locations or restricted critical infrastructure.

## Source gates
For every field require SOURCE_CONFIRMED / SOURCE_PENDING / USER_TESTIMONY / DERIVED / UNKNOWN and footprint/units/vintage. Research model not a claim of constructed node, operational 7-day performance or disaster losses avoided. Preserve original documents and harmonize datum before mapping 5.91m to installed assets.
