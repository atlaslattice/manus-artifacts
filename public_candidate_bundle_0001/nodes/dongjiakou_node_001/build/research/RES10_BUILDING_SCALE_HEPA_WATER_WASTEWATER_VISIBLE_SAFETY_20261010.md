# RES10 Building-Scale Resilience, Environmental Health and Visible Safety — 2026-10-10
**Status:** PROPOSED · NON-CANON · ZERO REALIZED CREDIT. Additive delta to RES07-09, QOL-MNT01, COAST01 and MAU-W01; no baseline overwrite.

## User-requested policy and design decisions
1. Pest and mold prevention by **trained specialists weekly as a proposed service target**, with inspection intensity adjusted by building risk; periodic professional interior deep cleaning and upkeep of lawns, parks, flowers and greens for residential, commercial and industrial spaces. Does not assign professional maintenance to families, residents or ordinary workforce. Basic personal hygiene remains individual. Acoustic pest suppression is research-only, not scalable deployment credit. Legacy BUG ANNIHILATION user recollection hypothesizes cavitation/excavation, locust damage and phase-specific interference; no validated field performance or safe atmospheric disintegration inferred.
2. Hyperlocal building batteries plus rooftop solar, backup modular islands and a coordinated neighborhood connection. Compare central, individual building and hybrid redundancy using matched total capacity. Require competent protection/black start, anti-islanding, safe fault isolation, critical load inventory, fire code, shared operations and reserve recovery.
3. Long horizon **swimmable coastlines and beaches where physically, ecologically and legally feasible**; not blanket transformation of shipping/industrial ports, hazardous shorelines, protected habitats or high-current exposed waters. Measure actual bathing water quality and safe access, coastal buffering, dunes, wetlands, sediment, ecology, sewage prevention and water recreation. Retain shoreline buffer protections under COAST01.
4. Residential/commercial/industrial interiors: full conditioned floor area heating/cooling, room-by-room setpoints, HEPA particulate filtration sized by clean air delivery and room volumes, ventilation/ERV HRV, humidity control and smoke-event filtration. Favor zone-based high-efficiency mini splits when appropriate; mini splits normally do not supply required outdoor-air ventilation. HEPA alone does not remove gases. Avoid ozone-generating air cleaners. Sources US EPA https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq/improving-indoor-air-quality and https://www.epa.gov/wildfires/indoor-air-filtration .
5. Onsite rain catchment with first flush, storage/filtration and overflow flood-management; segregated greywater collection, appropriate treatment/monitoring for permitted irrigation and toilet use. Condensate from HVAC and outdoor atmosphere as a candidate water source, primarily nonpotable until separately treated, tested, permitted as drinking water. Do NOT credit all vapor capture as potable or minerals automatically. Design mineralization of purified drinking water only under approved health, quality and process controls; consider a separate treated water spec for sensitive horticulture to avoid excess mineral content. EPA https://www.epa.gov/waterreuse/summary-texas-water-reuse-guideline-or-regulation-onsite-non-potable-water-reuse and https://www.epa.gov/ground-water-and-drinking-water/potable-water-reuse-and-drinking-water .
6. Outdoor air/smog mitigation: prioritize preventing emissions at source, green buffers and protected filtered indoor spaces; pilot localized industrial intake/vent capture with mass/energy accounting, not a claim of cleaning open atmosphere at whole-city scale; carbon capture and water vapor capture are distinct processes.
7. Multi-hazard AI early warning for severe storms, tornadoes or waterspouts over ocean, surge, flooding and industrial alerts, always paired with authoritative forecast and human emergency management. Wastewater public health surveillance for pathogens and aggregate drug trends with validated assays, reporting thresholds, safeguards and public health oversight; no individual tracking or criminal attribution. CDC https://www.cdc.gov/wastewater/about/ .
8. Public-space camera safety: visible marked signs, disclosed purpose and administrator, authorized operator access, no covert monitoring or sensitive private spaces; minimize retention, encrypt data, log all access, human-review AI alerts, no automated accusation/punishment, lawful evidentiary integrity with appropriate case holds, retention/disposal policy, no automated face identity default, public audits and appeals. Residential exterior cameras opt-in/owner-resident governed; avoid neighbors' windows/private areas; separate tenancy/consent rules. China State Council public video regulation effective 2025-04-01 https://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/mobile/law/detail?LawID=1762 and PIPL art26 https://en.spp.gov.cn/2021-12/29/c_948419_2.htm .
9. Resilience service continuum: LIFE_SAFETY, HVAC, AIR, WATER, SECURITY, HAZARD_WATCH and QOL staffing link into RES09 service graph, RES07 building/neighbor batteries, RES08 external hazards, MAU-W01 wastewater and COAST01 beaches. Maintain seven-day critical-service target and conditional longer generation-backed endurance.
