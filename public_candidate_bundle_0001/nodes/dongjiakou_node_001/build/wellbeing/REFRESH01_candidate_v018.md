## Complimentary gourmet refreshments on duty

User-selected benefit: **free, unlimited gourmet coffee, tea and snacks while on the job**. Programme design means no employee charge and no routine per-person serving quota during duty hours, with replenished availability across all operating shifts. “Unlimited” describes access, not a target to consume caffeine or calories. Participation and quantity chosen remain voluntary.

Offer quality fresh coffee and a varied tea programme suited to local traditions and worker preferences, alongside decaffeinated coffee, caffeine-free options and drinking water. Specify quality through preparation, freshness, taste/acceptance and supplier standards rather than assuming expensive imported ingredients are required.

Provide a varied snack selection with nourishing options, fruit and other locally feasible items, dietary/accessibility choices and clear allergen/ingredient information. Set the final menu through employee feedback, food-safety review and actual supplier bids. Keep cultural/dietary preferences explicit. No medical or productivity claim follows automatically from gourmet service.

Locate convenient refreshment points and comfortable break areas appropriate to each work zone. Office/self-service areas and staffed cafe options are candidate delivery models. In industrial, laboratory, food-production and clinical settings, use designated safe consumption areas according to applicable hygiene/hazard rules; beverages and food must not introduce process contamination or machinery hazards. Availability does not remove protected breaks or require employees to eat while operating equipment.

Serve workers on night/rotating shifts equally. Required serving, cleaning, procurement and replenishment roles follow the same eight-hour/five-day default, paid training/leave and qualified-capacity policy. Self-service does not erase cleaning/food-safety labor. Do not count refreshment service staff hours twice as other household/cultural services.

## Operations, funding and measurement

Use appropriately authorized food service, safe water, equipment cleaning/maintenance, temperature/storage controls where relevant, traceability and waste handling. Identify required competency and inspection receipts before operation. Specify reliable replenishment and backup provision; no deployed stock, supplier contract or food-service approval is asserted.

Budget demand as variable actual consumption, not a fictional unlimited inventory: beverage ingredients, snacks, preparation equipment, water/power, qualified staffing, cleaning, consumables, repairs/replacement and reserves. Use low/central/high demand sensitivity for affordability while preserving the intended no-charge/no-routine-quota policy. Track menu preferences and aggregate use without compulsory personal caffeine/food surveillance.

Procure sustainably through verified quality, regional availability, transport/packaging and full costs. Reusable serving items and waste prevention are proposed options requiring cleaning/resource accounting. Do not claim that local sourcing or reusable cups necessarily reduces emissions without a measured comparator.

Finance through the established verifiable net gains/savings elsewhere at deployment, with independently receipted allocations and reserves. Estimated productivity or future savings cannot pay a current bill. Essential/emergency/clinical commitments remain protected. “Free to workers” still carries an employer operating cost; the benefit is not approved or funded until those receipts close.

Keep this service separate from NUT01 nutrition formulation and outcome evidence. Ordinary snacks and beverages do not establish nutritional completeness or ingredient-dose equivalence.
