## Twenty-four-hour emergency access

User-selected design requirement: emergency services staffed and available **24 hours/day, seven days/week**, with a **five-minute emergency-vehicle driving-time target** to every occupied residential block and relevant workplace. Evaluate ambulance/medical response, fire/rescue and public-safety dispatch separately; a nearby facility is not proof all services are staffed, available or capable.

Define two mapped directions: responder base to incident/resident entrance and residence to an appropriate emergency receiving facility. A five-minute drive to a clinic is not evidence of emergency ambulance arrival or of a hospital's capability. Target nearby first response and appropriate receiving care; specialist referral/definitive hospital transport is a separate timeline and may exceed five minutes. Never advise self-driving in a medical emergency to meet a planning target.

Total response = call receipt/processing + dispatch/crew turnout + routed travel + on-site access to the person. Log each interval and actual arrival; do not label five-minute travel as a five-minute call-to-care guarantee. Fire station references may measure from dispatch instruction, which is a different clock. Report target compliance/coverage by service and cohort, not only an average.

Map real road networks, gates, vehicle clearances, intersections, truck/rail conflicts, building access, night staffing and peak congestion. Test routes during weather/flood closures, simultaneous calls and first-unit unavailability; design alternate access and mutual-aid cover. Straight-line circles or assumed constant speeds cannot establish coverage. Required coverage includes workers sleeping by day, disabled/older residents, children and visitors, with address/location and accessible communication options.

Provide local-authority dispatch integration, qualified crews and supervision, maintained vehicles/equipment, clear incident command, emergency contact channels, backup power/communications and manual fallback. No badge, resident app, device ownership or research consent prerequisite for requesting emergency help. Scannable identifiers do not delay care or expose private health records. Local partners, service agreements and actual response capability remain UNKNOWN.

## Fire prevention and response

Integrate assessed fire safety into printed-wall or recycled-material housing and site design before occupancy: whole-wall/system fire and smoke performance, compartmentation, protected escape routes, detection/alarm, suppression where required, fire-water/hydrants, emergency lighting, safe utility isolation, appliance/electrical checks and fire-service access. Applicable Chinese requirements and engineered designs need review; a material supplier's fire-resistance statement does not approve the building.

Separate housing from industrial hazards according to assessed safety distances and lawful zoning. Coordinate with port/steel/chemical facilities for process hazards, toxic smoke and appropriate specialist response. Provide resident evacuation guidance, accessible assistance, assembly/accountability arrangements and drills; volunteers and microstations do not replace professional fire crews. Designate safe bicycle/e-bike charging and battery storage with hazard review, separated from egress routes, and manufacturer-compatible maintained equipment.

Qualified inspectors/maintainers must test alarms, suppression, pumps, water supply and access routes, log defects and close work orders. Housekeeping/groundskeeping must keep evacuation routes and responder access clear. No unverified reduction in fire frequency or loss assigned to the simulator.

## Flood, coastal and compound-hazard mitigation

Obtain site elevations, drainage/outfall capacity, flood depths/velocities, rainfall, river, storm-surge/tide and groundwater information; assess compound events and changing future exposure. Site essential emergency facilities and safe shelter/evacuation routes outside unacceptable exposure or protect them through an engineered, reviewed design. Raise/protect critical power, telecom, medical supplies and controls; maintain backup power and communications accessible during outages.

Use an engineered combination of drainage, detention/storage, rain gardens/permeable surfaces where suitable, maintained outfalls, backflow protection, pumps and appropriately designed coastal/flood defenses. Irrigation or aesthetic landscaping is not automatically flood protection. Contaminated industrial soil/runoff and saline coastal water require separate assessment; do not route untreated floodwater into potable or resident-use systems.

Link official weather/flood warnings to clear readiness, evacuation and shelter decisions. Provide evacuation before route inundation, accessible transport/support, safe refuges, pre-positioned supplies, rescue coordination and tested recovery/cleanup plans. People must not be sent through floodwater to preserve a five-minute coverage metric. Inspect drains/pumps and practice blocked-route, flood-plus-fire, outage and multiple-incident scenarios.

Qingdao's 2023 water-network plan lists Dongjiakou Port City planning references of 50-year river-flood protection and 20-year urban-waterlogging recurrence. These are differentiated published planning references, not proof of completed local protection or a final design criterion for this site. A 50-year recurrence corresponds to a nominal 2% annual exceedance for that specified hazard under its statistical assumptions, not a 2% probability of total node failure or a 98% resilience score. Keep surge/tide, waterlogging and river flooding separate and account for events beyond design.

## Staffing, funding and resilience interface

Continuous coverage is delivered by rotating qualified teams while individual workers retain the eight-hour/five-day default, leave and recovery policy. One continuously occupied post requires168 staffed hours/week; dividing by40 gives4.2 gross full-time equivalents before breaks, handovers, leave, sickness, training and simultaneous staffing requirements. This SIMULATED arithmetic is not a hiring number or proof of qualified coverage. Use the OPS01 competency/roster matrix; shortages automatically flag training/recruitment and appropriate mutual aid or service staging. Twelve-hour shifts remain gated temporary exceptions, not the emergency-services default.
