## Housing landscape and groundskeeping companion

User-directed addition: integrate lawns and native botanicals into housing where relevant, with irrigation and upkeep designed and budgeted together, paid groundskeeper jobs, and an enduring beautification standard.

Provide usable lawns for play, gathering and recreation where site/water budgets support them, alongside locally appropriate native trees, shrubs, flowering plants and groundcovers. Species and planting density require a Qingdao/Dongjiakou horticultural site survey for salinity, wind, drought, soil contamination, seasonal needs and resident accessibility. No species list or survival rate is assumed. Keep private dwelling floor area distinct from communal landscaped area.

Design planted courtyards, street shade, cycle/walking links and quiet recreation spaces with clear access, sightlines, drainage, emergency routes and utility clearances. Rooftop/vertical planting remains conditional on structural load, waterproofing, fire/access and maintenance design. Native habitat areas may have seasonal ecological appearance: apply zone-specific standards rather than requiring all vegetation to look like mown lawn.

Irrigation is an operating service: meter water and pumping electricity by zone, use soil/weather-informed scheduling and appropriate drip/micro-irrigation, identify establishment versus mature demand, and include leak detection and maintenance. Rainwater and quality-certified reclaimed water are candidate sources with storage, treatment, backflow/cross-connection and exposure controls. Do not use untreated industrial effluent, saline brine or process water by assumption. Source availability, applicable water quality and permitted use remain UNKNOWN.

Create paid, trained groundskeeping roles for horticulture, irrigation maintenance, seasonal pruning/mowing, tree inspection, integrated pest management, litter removal, planting renewal and resident response. These roles follow the eight-hour/five-day default, leave policy and workforce qualification flag. OPS01 needs landscaped area by task/zone, task hours, qualified staff, seasonal relief, equipment and competency assessment. No invented headcount or jobs-created credit. Voluntary resident gardening supplements service; it cannot be required unpaid maintenance.

**Proposed beautification service standard:** documented design and zone inventory; agreed inspections and seasonal calendar; healthy maintained planting and timely dead-plant replacement; safe clear paths and cycleways; litter removal; managed weeds/invasives; safe tree condition; functioning irrigation with leak repair; no avoidable standing water; resident complaint/repair tracking. Quantitative survival/cover/litter/response-time thresholds are to be set after pilot and local professional review, not presented as existing Chinese acceptance. Record photographic inspections, work orders, plant survival, water/energy use, pesticide/fertilizer inputs and resident satisfaction. Ecological management and accessibility are explicit assessment criteria.

Budget establishment and recurring wages, training, irrigation, water/energy, tools/PPE, maintenance, replacement plants and reserves in FUND01. Verified sector cash gains may finance these costs only after allocation receipts close. Landscaping employment is a cost and proposed job pathway, not an automatic productivity or GRV benefit.

HSG01/GREEN01 proposed interface: private/shared/landscaped area separately; lawn/native cover by zone; site survey and species provenance; water source/quality/permit; irrigation design; metered annual water and electricity; qualified maintenance hours/season; full costs; agreed inspection thresholds; baseline/comparator; primary credit owner. Local observations and benefit coefficients are UNKNOWN; zero realized credit. This addition is documentation/structure-only; no new executable landscape efficacy model.

## Official alignment and evidence boundaries

China's 2024 Water Conservation Regulation Article 33 requires improved efficiency in urban landscaping and calls for locally suited drought-resistant planting and efficient irrigation in water-scarce areas. Qingdao's 2024 garden/forestry programme describes seasonal maintenance calendars and monthly/quarterly assessment. These support a review route, not approval of this project or a verified water-saving coefficient.
