## Stronger recovery and workforce policy

Programme default: **8 hours work, 8 hours family/recreation/personal time, and 8 hours sleep opportunity, with no more than five workdays per week.** This is a design commitment, not a claim that eight hours in bed guarantees eight hours asleep. Commutes, care, breaks, handovers and on-call interruptions must appear in the real time budget. Protect quiet/dark daytime sleep for night workers.

Plan and fund enough assessed, qualified teams for ordinary eight-hour rosters, relief breaks, handovers, paid training and vacation. Continuous industrial service is staffed through rotating teams. Longer shifts cannot be the routine substitute for workforce development.

**Any proposed day above eight active working hours is an exception request. Twelve hours is the maximum total shift span including breaks, not a target.** Only a temporary, documented lack of qualified workers after feasible alternatives are exhausted can enter this programme's exception-review lane. Alternatives include qualified relief/other teams, rescheduling, reduced safe throughput and temporary service staging.

Each exception needs sector/role, quantified coverage gap and competency matrix, alternatives considered, applicable legal/worker-consultation/overtime-pay review, recovery and safe-transport assessment, accountable owner, dated expiry, evidence receipts, and funded training plus external recruitment assessment. Expiry blocks the exception; any extension needs a new review and progress receipts. Unknown gates block the proposed exception. A programme ceiling is not legal permission: ordinary overtime or local approved arrangements may impose tighter limits. Staffing shortage alone does not establish a legal emergency exception.

**Automatic OPS01 workforce flag:** when qualification shortages are reported or inferred from a longer-shift request, open a sector-specific gap assessment and train or recruit additional qualified workers. Missing data is a proposed assessment, not a measured deficit. Recruiting from outside the region is a candidate route; attendance alone does not qualify trainees. Track assessor, qualification dates, relief availability, owner, budget, recruitment lead time and return-to-eight-hour date. Do not count hired/trained capacity until receipts close. Costs feed FUND01; modeled future savings cannot pay an approved current budget.
