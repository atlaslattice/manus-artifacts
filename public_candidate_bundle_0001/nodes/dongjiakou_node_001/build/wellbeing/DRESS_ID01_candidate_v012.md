## On-duty presentation and identification

User-directed workplace policy: business casual appropriate to Chinese professional settings for office, administrative, STEM and public-facing duties, with scannable work identifiers. This is an employer-policy proposal; this review does not establish a single national Chinese business-casual dress specification.

Proposed baseline: clean, tidy, comfortable clothing suitable for the task, such as a collared shirt/blouse or equivalent smart top, trousers/skirt or equivalent practical attire, and appropriate maintained footwear. Provide climate, disability, religious/cultural and pregnancy accommodations; no compulsory cosmetics, manicure/pedicure or gendered grooming expectations. Monthly grooming remains optional. Apply only on duty, not to residents' family/recreation time.

Required safety, hygiene and clinical clothing takes precedence: industrial/port/chemical/construction work uses role-specific compliant PPE; food handling and clinical work use applicable hygiene uniforms and protective equipment. Groundskeeping and housekeeping need suitable task clothing and footwear. Loose items and dangling badges must not introduce machinery, contamination or other hazards.

## Scannable work badge design

Provide a readable role/organization badge with a QR code or equivalent scan identifier. Use a revocable, non-guessable work identifier rather than a national-ID number, home address or direct personnel-record export. A public scan should show only a deliberately approved minimal work verification/status or a contact-verification route. Authorized scanners may retrieve purpose-limited role and access status after authentication; fine-grained permissions and expiry belong in the controlled backend.

A printed/static code is copyable and must not by itself unlock a door or prove the holder's identity. Access control needs separate verified authentication and appropriate authorization. Include issuing authority, lost/replaced badge revocation, contractor/visitor expiry, audit and offline/manual fallback procedures. No biometric or continuous location tracking is implied.

Do not expose health/acupuncture records, AI memory, research participation, grooming use, household details or personal contact information through the badge. Collection and access logs require documented purposes, retention, access controls and Chinese personal-information review. Work identifiers are linkable personal data, not automatically anonymized. No resident-wide social score or mandatory scan of off-duty life is proposed.

Provide work clothing/PPE, laundering or cleaning arrangements, replacement and badges as explicit employer operating costs. Keep donning/doffing, required changing and training time visible in the roster under applicable rules. The 8/8/8/five-day default, workforce-capacity flags and verified-cash funding rule remain intact.
