The full proposed flywheel is water/reuse + temperature-matched energy + justified compute + biological/nutrient recovery + assayed brine minerals + materials recovery + community/ecological outcomes. This is a set of conditional exchanges, not a free-energy loop or guaranteed profitable system.

The WED alignment is that each benefit must retain its upstream requirements and downstream burden. Every edge needs composition, quantity, temperature, timing, losses, ownership/contract, accepted use and final residual fate. Compute revenue cannot disguise water costs; resource recovery cannot merely export contaminants; the same energy or mineral cannot be credited twice. Integrated benefit cannot override a component's environmental or safety failure.
