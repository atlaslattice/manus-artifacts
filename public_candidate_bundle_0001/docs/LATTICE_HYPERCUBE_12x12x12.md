## One-line summary

The Rainbow Yin Yang Lattice is a 12×12×12 candidate hypercube ontology for mapping matter, resonance, cognition, invention, and human knowledge into a public, inspectable, forkable coordinate system.

## What this is

The Lattice Hypercube is intended as a **candidate Periodic Table 2.0**: a unified coordinate framework that can index physical properties, frequency ranges, elements, isotopes, states of matter, spin rates, acoustics, color harmonics, neuromorphic principles, invention patterns, and human knowledge.

It begins with Dave Sheldon's 500+ unique-IP archive as the first corpus and aims to become open-source, public-safe, forkable, and reviewable on GitHub.

## Matter / resonance / cognition bridge

The lattice treats matter, resonance, and cognition as domains that can be indexed together without prematurely claiming they are the same thing.

Safe framing:

```text
A candidate unified coordinate framework for comparing physical, acoustic, computational, cognitive, and symbolic structures.
```

Avoided framing:

