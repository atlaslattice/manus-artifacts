# Atlas Lattice v0.6 — Regenerative Industrial Symbiosis, Strategic Resilience, Quality of Life and Global Replication

**Research white paper | 2026-10-09 | Public review candidate | NON-CANON | NO DEPLOYMENT AUTHORITY | ZERO REALIZED CREDITS**

**Reference locality:** Dongjiakou / 董家口, Qingdao, China. **Proposed scope:** transferable Tier-1 and Tier-2 circular industrial nodes. **Status:** architecture and evidence framework, **not** an operating Atlas Lattice installation or government-endorsed project.

## Abstract

Industrial symbiosis should be evaluated by more than commodity yield or project return. This paper extends the recovered Dongjiakou locality model with five mutually reconciled ledgers: (1) financial economics and domestic value retention; (2) strategic resilience of essential services; (3) Eden / quality of life (QOL) and ecological regeneration; (4) Global Replication Value (GRV) of openly reproducible methods; and (5) physical resource and attribution integrity. Each ledger has separate evidence classifications, uncertainty, counterfactuals and vetoes. The architecture is designed to compare localized biomass, fuels, nutrient, mineral, water and energy pathways without assuming that policy alignment, theoretical resource potential, code publication or a third-party commercial project demonstrates local operation. The objective is **the greatest verifiable human and ecological benefit subject to physical, financial, contractual and safety constraints**.

### Thesis

A node's value is not the tonnes processed alone, but the **additional essential services, community prosperity, healthy ecosystems, industrial continuity and independent replication it delivers**. No single multiplier, import-replacement percentage or beauty score can substitute for evidence.

| Evidence class | Meaning | Typical promotion limit |

## 2. Documented policy and industrial context, with limits

China's 2026 circular-economy plan targets approximately 16% higher resource productivity from the 2025 baseline by 2030; about 4.5 billion tonnes/year of bulk solid waste utilized; 510 million tonnes/year of major recycled resources; and an ¥8 trillion recycling industry output target. The 2026 seawater desalination action plan calls for more than 4.5 million tonnes/day installed desalination by 2030 and research/demonstrations in brine resource recovery. China's 2026 green-fuel report describes, **as of end-2025**, roughly 1.7 Mt/year built green aviation-fuel capacity, 3 Mt/year biodiesel capacity, 0.38 Mt/year green methanol capacity and approximately 1 bcm/year built biomethane capacity across 146 projects. **Capacity is not utilization, realized displacement or a mandate for Atlas Lattice.**

The Sinopec Qingdao LNG cold-energy cascade has been reported in design/construction, with **26 GWh/year projected generation** and a separately projected **8 GWh/year avoided cooling electricity**. These must never be described as 34 GWh/year delivered electricity. Phosphogypsum-to-acid designs in Hubei cite approximately 1 Mt/year phosphogypsum feed and 0.4 Mt/year industrial acid output, which does not establish a universal acid yield without moisture, composition and concentration bases. Qingdao potassium pilot reports and external helium, SAF and green-methanol projects are **comparators**, not Dongjiakou-installed equipment. Regulatory approval of specific Fusarium mycoproteins is not blanket food/feed approval of unspecified organisms or waste streams.

**Other dialogue claims needing further primary reconciliation:** August sulfur import series and supplier shift; a blanket acid export halt; the LNG BOG helium concentration at Dongjiakou; nationwide 2030 biomethane output and theoretical 2060 potential; current SAF export quotas; historical 200,000-tourist Qingdao beautification example; 13% natural-gas-storage policy target; specific helium reserve/geological storage figures; and projected Class-A commodity substitution rates. All remain contextual claims or UNKNOWN where primary receipts are absent.

## 3. Shared resource metabolism: one physical lot, one allocation

Each feedstock lot requires identity, owner, location, wet mass, moisture/dry-matter fraction, contamination, extraction rights, existing uses, seasonal time series, source receipts, ecological reserve, logistics, conversion yield, energy demand, final product specifications and residual fate.

**Allocatable dry matter = qualified dry matter − soil/ecological retention − existing obligations − collection/storage losses.**

A tonne of crop straw cannot simultaneously supply SAF, biomethane, livestock feed and soil carbon. Separate lot fractions may be allocated where physical balances demonstrate it. Nutrients returned via straw are **not** spare merchant potassium. Standard struvite (MgNH4PO4·6H2O) is not potassium chloride. Gypsum-based acid recovery **transforms existing sulfur**; it does not create elemental sulfur. Avoid K vs K2O vs KCl, CaSO4 dry vs wet feed, and industrial acid solution vs H2SO4-equivalent confusion. Prevent double counting of electricity, hydrogen, CO2, tax transfers, ecological offsets and carbon credits.

|---|---|---|
| W01 / desal | Desalination, industrial reuse, brine analysis and qualified mineral recovery | Recovery fraction, stream rights, product buyer, residual salinity and receiving-water ecology |

## 4. Financial and domestic-value-retention ledger

The 2025 import reference values previously discussed—approximately **68.43 Mt LNG** and **$35.47B customs value**—illustrate the scale of fossil-gas trade, not available savings. An example **$126.3B Class-A inventory** (soy, LNG, copper cathodes, potash, sulfur, helium, bromine/iodine) is **a provisional gross reference**, not a physically realizable displacement ceiling. The 8.01× and 2× import-substitution multipliers are not established China-specific net GDP effects; even comparable U.S. sector studies usually report gross output including indirect/induced effects.

Report at least: full delivered import cost; full domestic replacement cost; financial NPV/IRR; gross output; **incremental domestic GDP/value added**; local household income; worker jobs and wage quality; government revenues; imported inputs embedded in domestic projects; land/labor/capital opportunity costs; net welfare; and independently estimated social/environmental costs. Treat tax revenue as a transfer for economy-wide welfare accounting when appropriate. Imported LNG also creates domestic logistics/distribution value added.

**Local value retention ratio (LVRR)** = attributable value added within the declared locality / total eligible project expenditure.

**Net incremental local value added** = local VA with project − local VA in credible counterfactual, after displaced activity.

**Eden reinvestment capacity** = distributable free cash flow after legal obligations, maintenance, reserves and risk provisions; **not** hypothetical output multipliers. Ownership and benefits distribution must be reported.

## 5. Strategic-resilience ledger: uncertainty and tail-risk rigor

Model disruptions as **scenario trees** with duration, probability or probability interval, shock correlations, sector dependencies, severity and recovery. Identify firm supply rather than annual production alone. Compare domestic biomethane and renewable fuels with gas/oil storage, efficiency, import diversification, electrification, demand response and network hardening.

A useful *simplified* annual expected net resilience value is

`V_R = Σ_s p_s × (L_baseline,s − L_with_project,s) − C_incremental,annual`.

For non-independent severe scenarios, account for overlap and conditional probabilities; for multiyear shocks, discount an explicit loss time series. This is only one decision criterion; tail risk and essential-service thresholds should be reported separately.

**Probability-period correction:** 5% chance of ≥1 event in ten years implies annual hazard `p=1−(1−0.05)^(1/10)≈0.005116`, not 5% annually, under a stationary independent-year hazard. A 0.812% national GDP loss at $19.5T equals **$158.34B gross impact**; annualized gross expected exposure under that assumed decadal event probability is about **$0.81B**. A deliberately extreme **5% annual GDP-loss scenario** implies **$975B loss if it occurs** and about **$4.99B annualized expected gross exposure** under the same 5% *decadal* event probability. If, instead, event probability were 5% **each year**, that gross expectation would be **$48.75B/year**. None represents the impact Atlas prevents.

Report scenario loss prevented `L_baseline−L_with_project`, bounded by actual physical coverage and sectors protected. Aviation SAF, renewable diesel, green methanol, biomethane, reserves and material security may mitigate *different* disruption pathways; they cannot each be assigned the whole national GDP shock.

**Resilience measurements:** firm energy/fertilizer/water days of autonomy; protected share of essential demand; time to interruption; time to recovery; severe-shortage probability; winter peak deliverability; alternate-route capacity; supplier concentration; critical inventory buffer days; share of crucial industries staying online; households with protected essential-service access; outage-hours avoided; 90/180-day continuity; correlated-system failure performance. For LNG, use the correct physical target: fossil LNG **imports** vs **total natural gas demand** are different denominators. A 20 bcm/year biomethane program is capacity/target context, not guaranteed import substitution. Dry biomass need for 94.4 bcm LNG-equivalent imports at hypothetical net yields 150–300 m3 methane/t dry biomass is ~629–315 Mt/year **before** ecological availability constraints and basis reconciliation.

## 6. Eden: quality of life and industrial-park beautification as infrastructure

**Design ambition:** a healthy, verdant, flowering Dongjiakou with cool walking corridors, shade, accessible gardens, habitat links, public education and worker rest spaces. These are **proposed designs**, not measured site conditions. Design for Qingdao's climate, coastal winds, salt spray, winter exposure and contamination risks. Use locally vetted native/adapted plants and staggered flowering seasons; ecological function takes precedence over ornamental monocultures.

Potential water supply: compliant treated municipal/industrial reclaimed water, verified stormwater capture and safe condensate recovery. **Do not irrigate with untreated desal brine, unsafe process wastewater or inadequately characterized reuse water.** Test salinity, sodium adsorption ratio, boron, metals, pathogens, soil accumulation and human exposure; provide separate plumbing and fail-safe isolation. Hydrozones, mulching, soil cover, soil testing, drip irrigation, seasonal controls and drought-adapted plantings should minimize recurring labor, electricity, chemical and potable-water requirements. Long-term operating budgets and plant survival must be counted, not hidden.

**No substitution of aesthetics for ecosystem performance.** An attractive planted buffer must not mask hazardous emissions, contaminated soils or ecological discharge damage. The reported ~200,000-tourist Qingdao industrial beautification precedent requires source, year, boundary and uniqueness validation before benchmarking.

**Non-negotiable ecological vetoes:** preserve existing INV-19 for primary permits/monitoring and INV-20 for measured receiving-ecosystem net-positive evidence. Add QOL gates for reclaimed-water quality, soil safety, habitat and food security, water rights, public access safety, winter/summer plant survival and maintenance viability. Regulatory non-deterioration is not evidence of ecological improvement. QOL score weighting, if used, may never offset a veto.

## 7. Global Replication Value (GRV): public knowledge as a measured externality

GRV is the **additional cost/time/risk reduction or QOL improvement external adopters achieve because a documented, independently verified component is reusable**. It is not GitHub stars, publicity, theoretical addressable markets or unilateral claims of country-level influence.

Model cumulative deployments using time, baseline alternatives and counterfactual: `GRV_net = Σ_i [avoided engineering + avoided validation + avoided failure + verified benefits attributable to shared methods − adaptation, training, compliance and integration cost]_i`, with **separate disaggregated components and attribution boundaries**. Do not add a recipient's benefits a second time in a national economic ledger.

**GRV measurements:** independently reproduced source tests; successful public fork checks; independent contributors; portable schema conformity; independent deployment count; paid engineering-hours saved per deployment; time-to-permit/commission reduction; process CAPEX savings at equivalent scope; operation/maintenance savings; translations and usable documentation; standards adoption where documented; beneficial public QOL results in independently deployed sites; replicator-reported negative outcomes; governance portability and local sovereignty retained. Record who validated each claim, when and under which alternative baseline.

**Stage gates:** AVAILABLE → REPRODUCIBLE → INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED → PILOT DEMONSTRATED → OPERATING REPLICATION → MEASURED SOCIAL / ECOLOGICAL BENEFIT. Progress on one stage does not automatically certify a later stage. The 2026-10-08 GitHub release establishes **AVAILABLE** and reports local reproduction; it does not establish independent physical replication or government endorsement.

## Conclusion

Atlas Lattice v0.6 extends the question from **“Can a physical loop close?”** to **“Does a verified loop make life better, remain useful under disruption, and reduce the difficulty of responsible adoption elsewhere?”** Its test of success is not a favorable-looking projected multiplier or an attractive photograph. It is **auditable, additional, durable benefit** to people and ecosystems, produced by physically conservative systems, under accountable human governance.
