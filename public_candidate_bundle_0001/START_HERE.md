Welcome. This folder is the first public-safe scaffold for the **Atlas Lattice / Rainbow Yin Yang Lattice** project.

Atlas Lattice is being developed as a public, provenance-first knowledge graph for making a large human-AI archive inspectable, forkable, reviewable, and useful without confusing maps, dreams, models, or graph edges with authority.

## What this is

- A public front door for the Rainbow Yin Yang Lattice / Atlas Lattice work.
- A forkable knowledge-graph scaffold.
- A place to define source status, release status, claim packets, evidence logs, review findings, and contradiction handling.
- A safe starting point before broad archive ingestion.

## What this is not

- Not canon.
- Not a deployment.
- Not proof of a unified field theory.
- Not an OpenAI, Microsoft, GitHub, Google, or xAI endorsement claim.
- Not a dump of private raw archives.
- Not a replacement for scientific, legal, licensing, or public-safety review.
