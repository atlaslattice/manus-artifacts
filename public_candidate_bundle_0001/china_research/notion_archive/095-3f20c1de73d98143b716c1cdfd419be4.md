## 1. Executive summary
1. **\[MODELED\]** **The SHREDDERNAUT cost-and-energy model is UNSOUND.** Its 1,800 kWh/day budget implies **18 kWh/t at 100 t/day** and **72–120 kWh/t at 25–15 t/day**, which is below a favourable pre-extrusion engineering comparator of **256.9–336.7 kWh/t** for the stated shred/wash/dry sequence and far below cited commercial inventories.
2. **\[MODELED\]** The same 1,800 kWh/day supports only **5.35–7.01 t/day** against the favourable engineering comparator, **3.42 t/day** against the commercial-HDPE-chain normalisation, or **2.24 t/day** against the Spanish operating-facility pre-pellet electricity inventory; it does not substantiate 15–25 t/day with washing and drying, and does not substantiate 50–150 or 100 t/day.
3. **\[UNKNOWN\]** No independent SHREDDERNAUT/Trashium pilot, meter log, mass balance, audited financial record, product certificate, buyer acceptance or comparable Chinese operating installation was found. The real capacity, yield, energy use, uptime, reject route and product specification therefore remain unverified.
4. **\[UNKNOWN\]** Dongjiakou’s own usable plastic supply is not quantified. No audited node material-flow balance, source contract, legal catchment, transport quote or measured polymer/contamination profile was found; the node cannot be shown to support even the lower stated capacity.
5. **\[REPORTED\]** China permits a compliant domestic waste-plastic-recycling project through project-specific environmental, discharge and other approvals, and may offer conditional VAT treatment. **\[REPORTED\]** It does **not** transfer U.S. EPA/§168(k)/RIN/California-LCFS assumptions, and **\[REPORTED\]** imported solid-waste plastic cannot be a feedstock from 1 January 2021.
6. **\[REPORTED\]** Landfill excavation/remediation and selective recovery are real, including Chinese legacy-waste programmes. **\[UNKNOWN\]** They do not establish stable, specification-grade mixed aged-plastic feedstock, profit principally from plastic sales, a universal methane credit, or a construction-material route.
7. **\[MEASURED\]** Clean, source-separated concrete/masonry fractions can be trialled as printable mineral inputs after processing and printer-specific mix qualification. **\[UNKNOWN\]** No independently documented occupied building using disclosed recycled-C&D aggregate, or comparable Chinese RMB/m² evidence, was found.
8. **\[MEASURED\]** Controlled recycled-polymer products can serve defined outdoor and infrastructure uses. **\[UNKNOWN\]** Variable landfill/transfer-station plastic has no demonstrated Chinese approval, safety package, fire-rated wall system, long-term durability record or structural-building qualification; it must not be presented as a structural block feedstock.
9. **\[MODELED\]** The technically better *screening hypothesis* for a steel-and-port node is to characterise mineral C&D and steel co-products for conventional qualified outlets before considering imported mixed plastics or printed housing. **\[UNKNOWN\]** This is not yet an investment case because local tonnes, chemistry, existing commitments, demand and permits are absent.

## 6. Ranked design assumptions that must change
1. **\[MODELED\]** **Remove the current 15–25 / 50–150 t/day capacity claim from the design basis.** Replace it with a phased, independently supervised acceptance/yield/energy trial that records wet and dry input, operating hours, uptime, product, rejects, water and every electrical load.
2. **\[MODELED\]** **Replace “mixed landfill/transfer-station plastic” with a controlled, traceable feedstock specification.** Establish polymer bands; PVC/chlorine, brominated-flame-retardant, e-waste-plastic, metal, organic, moisture and ash limits; and batch acceptance/rejection rules before selecting machinery or a product.
3. **\[MODELED\]** **Delete the 1,800 kWh/day, US\$1.2m–2.8m and US\$200–400/t assumptions as bankable inputs.** Rebuild the model from measured pilot energy/yield/water/wastewater/reject data, China-specific installed quotations, buyer-specific delivered offers and disposal costs.
4. **\[MODELED\]** **Do not design Trashium as structural housing masonry.** If a plastic route is retained, limit the first product hypothesis to a defined, non-load-bearing/limited outdoor application only after material identity, safety, durability and an applicable Chinese approval route are demonstrated.
5. **\[MODELED\]** **Treat landfill mining as a remediation option with selective recovery, not a plastics supply guarantee.** Its no-mining baseline, methane management, disposal destinations, excavation hazards, composition and carbon balance must be site-specific.
6. **\[MODELED\]** **Reframe C&D 3D printing as a materials R&D programme, not a housing-delivery claim.** Start with clean concrete/masonry fines, local particle/absorption/contaminant characterisation and a printer-specific trial matrix; exclude mixed C&D, gypsum, wood, plastics and metals from the mineral feedstock claim.
7. **\[MODELED\]** **Prioritise mineral conventional outlets before 3D-printed housing.** Characterise C&D and steel co-products for qualified recycled aggregate, road-base, cementitious products or non-structural blocks, subject to chemistry, volumetric stability, leachability, existing off-take and local demand.
8. **\[MODELED\]** **Replace U.S. regulatory and incentive assumptions with a China workstream.** Obtain a formal process classification, EIA/discharge/siting/fire/waste-residue analysis, tax opinion and confirmed product pathway before including any incentive or approval in the financial model.
9. **\[MODELED\]** **Treat external municipal/commercial feedstock as a separate collection-and-logistics business.** It requires legal catchment, source contracts, weighbridge data, permitted haulage, delivered cost, reject liability and quality control; a container or port location does not supply those conditions.

## 8. Three most important remaining unknowns
1. **\[UNKNOWN\] Local, controlled feedstock reality:** the twelve-month Dongjiakou/Qingdao material balance; source-separated usable plastic and mineral tonnes; composition/contamination; ownership; current destination; contracts; legal catchment; transport and reject-disposal route.
2. **\[UNKNOWN\] Actual process performance and cost:** an independently metered, full-process pilot mass/energy/water/wastewater balance with sustained throughput, uptime, yield, maintenance, worker requirements, installed capital and China-specific operating costs.
3. **\[UNKNOWN\] Product legitimacy and revenue:** a defined formulation and buyer specification, independently tested mechanical/fire/smoke/toxicology/leachate/durability package, Chinese code/permit route, accepted product, signed offtake and buyer-specific delivered price.
## Final decision statement
**\[MODELED\]** The available evidence supports a **measurement-first, staged resource-characterisation programme**, not a commercial plastic-to-block or C&D-to-printed-housing deployment at Dongjiakou. **\[REPORTED\]** China’s policy setting can accommodate compliant domestic recycling and C&D resource use. **\[UNKNOWN\]** It does not supply the missing feedstock, process, product, market, approval or economic evidence. **\[MODELED\]** Until the three remaining unknowns are closed with independent local evidence, no capacity, capex, revenue, carbon credit, payback or construction claim should enter the node design basis.
