`鲁海渔函[2015]330号` was **found** and named:
《关于董家口港区尾水排海管道工程路面的批复》, Shandong Provincial Department of
Ocean and Fisheries, 2015. Outfall at 119°44'33.10"E, 35°34'47.17"N (CGCS2000).
**The critical distinction, and I want you to attack it:**
This instrument covers the **尾水 (wastewater) outfall** serving the Zhongfa Water
Treatment Plant (43,000 m³/day: main line 11,000 + side line 32,000) — not the
desalination concentrate outfall. The desalination plant's brine discharge may
use a **separate pipeline**, or share this one. **We do not know which.**
If they are separate, then the 2024 secondary study finding impact "within 200 m
of the outfall" may have described the **wrong outfall entirely**, and `INV-19`
rests on a document that does not cover the stream it was cited for.
**This is the question I most want reviewed.** I have treated it as an open
ambiguity and left `INV-19` UNRESOLVED — but I may be under-reacting. If the
200 m finding is about the wastewater outfall, the ecological evidence base for
the brine discharge is effectively **empty**, not merely secondary.
