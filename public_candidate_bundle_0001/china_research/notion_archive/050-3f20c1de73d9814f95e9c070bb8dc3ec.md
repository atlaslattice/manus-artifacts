## 2. Enhanced landfill mining — **PARTIALLY CONFIRMED**
### What actually works
**ecomaine (US, ashfill):** recovered **34,352 Mg** of ferrous and non-ferrous metals, December 2011-March 2015. The peer-reviewed case states ashfills can be **profitably mined without government support.**
> **But this is an ASHFILL metals operation — not mixed municipal legacy waste.** The distinction is everything, and it is the only documented unsubsidised profit case found.
**Mont-Saint-Guibert (Belgium):** excavated **374 Mg.** Fines composition: **41.9-43.9% \<4.5 mm fines, 35.9-39.0% inerts, 7.4-10.0% combustibles, 1.2-1.8% ferrous, 0.2-0.4% non-ferrous.** A field demonstration, not production.
**Frey Farm (US):** cash-flow neutral *before* airspace and cover value; **+US\$13.30/t only after those internal benefits.**
**Yingchun, Jingmen (China):** MODELLED cost US\$12.7/t stored waste; NPV US\$1.92M-16.63M depending on land reuse, energy facilities and avoided post-closure support. **Not realised profit.**
### Aged plastic is a poor feedstock
One polyethylene-rich excavated-plastic study found **PP \<5 wt%, cellulose \<4 wt%, PS \<2 wt%, and 11% inorganic inclusion after washing.** Unmodified material had **elongation at break of 11 ± 2%** — demonstrating contamination and blend variability.
### Climate outcome is scenario-dependent
Published ranges: **−1,550 to +640 kg CO₂e per Mg excavated.** Landfill-gas management under the no-mining baseline, energy system, waste composition and treatment technology dominate. **No universal methane credit is defensible.**
### China has a real programme
The 2012 national plan forecast **1,882 legacy-waste governance projects**, allowing selective excavation under suitable conditions. Named excavation projects exist — **Tangxia (Guangzhou), Yulong (Shenzhen).** The MEE technical guideline reviewed is a **2025 consultation draft**, not binding.
> **No independently audited commercial operation was found that supplies a stable specification-grade recycled-plastic feedstock from mixed aged municipal landfill waste for blocks, 3D-printed buildings or similar construction products.**

## 3. C&D waste as 3D-printing feedstock — **PARTIALLY CONFIRMED**
### Scale is enormous; utilisation is not
**China generated more than 3.0 billion tonnes of construction waste in 2023, with resource utilisation of only 10-30%.** *(MEE 2025 explanatory note — REPORTED, not a Dongjiakou measurement.)*
State Council 2025 policy classifies C&D into five source types and directs construction, demolition and renovation waste preferentially to **recycled aggregate, building materials and road materials.**
### The technical picture
Laboratory evidence supports processed recycled concrete and masonry fines in printable mixes. But **recycled aggregate changes water demand, rheology, open time, strength and interlayer performance**, so no universal usable percentage exists. Published replacement results span **12.5-100%**; partial 25-75% substitutions can match controls in some studies, while **full replacement and high brick content reduce directional and interlayer strength.**
### The null result that matters
> **No independently documented occupied building-scale 3D-printed structure using recycled C&D aggregate was found — anywhere.**
The Wujiazhuang, Hebei house (106 m², 2021, completed and occupied) used **polypropylene-fibre concrete, not recycled C&D aggregate.** China's real occupied printed houses do not disclose this feedstock. And the Wujiazhuang figure of about **¥200,000 was reported to exceed local rural self-build cost.**
No issued national Chinese whole-building 3D-concrete-printing standard was found; the 2024 document located is an **association consultation draft.**

## 6. Node waste integration — **PARTIALLY CONFIRMED, and the pathway is C&D + slag**
**No audited Dongjiakou mass balance exists** for plastic, slag, C&D, food residues, e-waste, brine or chemical residues.
**Steel co-products are the clearest potentially large mineral stream:** global averages of **200 kg/t crude steel (EAF)** and **400 kg/t (BF/BOF)**, with slag \~90% by mass. Not Dongjiakou measurements.
**Existing local evidence points to water symbiosis, not plastics circularity:** SUEZ, the facility operator, states its Dongjiakou wastewater plant has **23,200 m³/day capacity and enables 100% reuse of treated steel wastewater.**
**A Chinese precedent exists:** Caofeidian, where steel slag and scrap recycling plus steel-associated low-temperature sources are linked to desalination.
**Delivered-cost benchmarks:** China's 2019 national MSW collection-and-transport benchmark was **CNY 210/t**; a 2018 Qingdao C&D tariff was **CNY 23.03/t under 10 km plus CNY 0.95/t-km.**
> **The research verdict: "C&D plus steel slag is the better provisional construction-material pathway" than imported mixed plastic for a port-side industrial node.** The core claim that on-node waste can feed a plastic-to-block unit is **NOT CONFIRMED** — essential local mass, quality, logistics, regulatory, yield, offtake and independent pilot evidence is all absent.

Its energy budget supports **2-7 t/day**, not 15-150. Its capex is **3-5× below** comparable conventional facilities. Its feedstock route produces a variable, contaminated material.
