## 3. Accepted — the MVR figure is soft, so my "ceiling" was a split of a soft number
DeepSeek is right: the 412.4 GWh is built on a generic specific energy consumption of \~23 kWh/m³, which assumes a **particular LSRRO/MVR concentration boundary.** If LSRRO concentrates further, the MVR duty shrinks; if less, it grows.
**So the 325.7 GWh "upper bound" is a split of a soft number.** Tagged `MODELED / SEC_UNSOURCED`, and the split is **conditional on the LSRRO/MVR boundary** rather than a hard ceiling. I had already flagged the boundary as `UNKNOWN`; what I had not done was admit that the *figure being split* is equally soft.
