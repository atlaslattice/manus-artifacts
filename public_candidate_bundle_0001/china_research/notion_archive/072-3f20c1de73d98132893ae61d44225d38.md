**Scope. \[REPORTED\]** This synthesis reduces twelve evidence reviews for a proposed coastal industrial node at Dongjiakou, Qingdao and distinguishes what has been observed or reported elsewhere from what has **not** been demonstrated at the node. **\[MODELED\]** It applies a decision rule that does not convert a plausible mechanism, policy aspiration or discharge compliance into a net-positive outcome.
> **Evidence labels.** **\[MEASURED\]** = an empirical observation or effect estimate reported in the underlying evidence; **\[REPORTED\]** = a reported policy, guideline, case record, source statement, or synthesis conclusion; **\[MODELED\]** = a modelled estimate or a proposed decision/evaluation design; **\[UNKNOWN\]** = not established from the reviewed evidence. All recommendations below are **\[MODELED\]** proposals, not measured Dongjiakou outcomes.
## Executive summary
1. **\[REPORTED\] The programme cannot presently claim “net-positive symbiotic infrastructure on all metrics and lives” for humans, flora, fauna or AI.** Human QOL and ecological condition can be measured; flora welfare, aggregate wild-animal welfare and AI wellbeing cannot yet support an unqualified gain claim.
2. **\[MEASURED\] The strongest intervention evidence is for care delivered to people with a defined need:** collaborative care and supervised task-sharing improve depression outcomes, opioid agonist treatment is associated with markedly lower overdose mortality while retained in care, and tai chi reduces falls in older adults. **\[UNKNOWN\]** Dongjiakou need, coverage, workforce capacity and unit cost are not known.
3. **\[REPORTED\] The most defensible immediate investment is an independent, preregistered Living Well Register and baseline—not a prestige facility or a composite “wellbeing score.”** It should precede claims of benefit and control the decision to scale or stop every pilot.
4. **\[MEASURED\] Wastewater surveillance can detect aggregate trends for selected pathogens and drug residues, but cannot identify people, reliably turn concentration into local prevalence/use/overdose risk, eliminate a pathogen, or establish clinical AMR prescribing.** **\[REPORTED\]** China’s police use of drug wastewater monitoring makes a binding health-only firewall indispensable.
5. **\[MEASURED\] Green roofs can cool a building and retain a meaningful share of moderate rainfall; they do not establish industrial-coastal air-cleaning, food safety, worker productivity or broad biodiversity gains.** Suitable roofs, irrigation, salinity, wind, contamination and drainage must be tested locally.
6. **\[REPORTED\] A 46 m²/person housing target is an above-average Chinese design aspiration, not a medical minimum or optimum.** WHO supports reducing crowding; it does not establish a universal floor-area threshold. **\[UNKNOWN\]** The local carbon, affordability and urban-form trade-off is unquantified.
7. **\[MEASURED\] Individual physical activity, defined group arts for older adults, and limited condition-specific acupuncture/tai chi benefits have real evidence.** **\[MEASURED\]** Routes, parks, generic public art, wellness buildings and workplace traditional-practice offerings often have null or heterogeneous population/workplace effects.
8. **\[MEASURED\] General health checks and universal optimisation panels do not improve mortality, and genetic personalisation added no benefit in Food4Me.** **\[REPORTED\]** Targeted testing tied to confirmation and care is a different proposition; local biomarker prevalence and complete follow-up cost are unknown.
9. **\[MEASURED\] Coastal restoration can yield gains but commonly fails or remains incomplete.** **\[REPORTED\]** Avoidance and harm reduction come before offsets; oyster/saltmarsh/seagrass, brine mitigation and constructed wetlands require locally appropriate design, BACI evidence and long stewardship.
10. **\[REPORTED\] Recombinant food heme is commercially real but is not a proven dietary-iron or QOL intervention.** **\[MEASURED\]** China-specific outcome evidence is substantially stronger for NaFeEDTA-fortified soy sauce than for LegH; a local fermentation facility is not supported now.

## Ranked action portfolio
### Ranking method and guardrails
**\[MODELED\] Ranking basis.** The order below uses the supplied evidence register to qualitatively combine: (1) strength and directness of evidence, (2) expected effect where the target population actually has the relevant need, and (3) practical/legal feasibility. It is **not** a monetised cost-effectiveness ranking: Dongjiakou denominators, local need, costs and delivery capacity are largely **\[UNKNOWN\]**. Rank therefore means **priority for an evidence-disciplined next decision**, not a promise of net benefits.
**\[MODELED\] Universal safeguard.** No intervention advances from pilot to routine provision until it has a prespecified denominator, baseline, comparison or credible counterfactual, harms/equity measures, cost ledger, independent audit and a stop/modify rule.
### A. Do this now — strongest evidence, low-regret foundations
1. **\[MODELED\] Establish the Dongjiakou Living Well Register, independent governance and a 12-month baseline before attributing benefits.** **\[REPORTED\]** OECD/WHO/ONS/AF frameworks support a disaggregated dashboard; **\[MEASURED\]** ONS4 should remain four separate outcomes rather than a master score. This is the highest-ranked action because it is necessary to verify every subsequent claim.
2. **\[MODELED\] Build a confidential, voluntary primary-care stepped-care pathway for common mental disorders, with clinician supervision, measurement-based escalation, crisis/referral links and task-sharing.** **\[MEASURED\]** Collaborative care and task-shared psychological treatment have controlled evidence. **\[UNKNOWN\]** Local capacity should set initial scale; do not imply a local prevalence or savings result before baseline measurement.
3. **\[MODELED\] Put targeted clinical testing behind an indication, informed consent, confirmation and treatment pathway—not an annual universal panel.** **\[MEASURED\]** Treating iron deficiency without anaemia reduces subjective fatigue, while general health checks do not improve mortality. Offer risk/symptom-appropriate CBC/ferritin with inflammation context, B12, HbA1c/lipids and selected vitamin-D testing only where local clinical indications and follow-up exist.
4. **\[MODELED\] Adopt “avoid, minimise, monitor” as a binding ecological design condition.** **\[MEASURED\]** Offset evidence is weak and restoration failure is common; this makes avoiding habitat loss, intake mortality, brine impact, light and underwater noise a stronger immediate decision than buying offsets or planting symbolic greenery.
5. **\[MODELED\] Offer a low-risk, targeted tai chi/falls programme for older adults or other assessed high-fall-risk groups, with attendance and fall surveillance.** **\[MEASURED\]** The falls effect is stronger than general workplace claims. It is not a productivity programme and should not be marketed as one.
6. **\[MODELED\] Maintain accessible routes and activity opportunities through operational programming that fits shifts, disability and price constraints, while measuring use and total activity.** **\[MEASURED\]** Physical activity benefits people who become active; **\[MEASURED\]** infrastructure alone frequently produces null population effects. Do the enabling, low-regret access work now; stage major facilities only after demand and behavioural evidence.
### B. Pilot this — promising, conditional or unestablished at this node
1. **\[MODELED\] Run one integrated, health-only wastewater pilot for selected pathogens and, only after a governance review, selected public-health drug trends.** Validate sewershed/population/flow, inhibition/recovery, rainfall effects and target-specific detection limits; triangulate against clinical data; publish aggregated data; and prohibit law-enforcement, employer, immigration or punitive use. **\[MEASURED\]** Detection/trend value is credible; **\[UNKNOWN\]** Dongjiakou validity and benefit are not.
2. **\[MODELED\] Pilot a small number of structurally cleared, non-solar green roofs/biosolar layouts and—only after water and food-safety testing—productive plots.** Measure roof temperature, runoff volume/quality, irrigation and replacement demand, PV output, survival, maintenance cost, worker use and any crop contaminants. **\[MEASURED\]** Building-scale cooling/runoff effects are credible; **\[MEASURED\]** passive green-wall air-cleaning has field nulls.
3. **\[MODELED\] Pilot habitat-specific coastal restoration only after multi-season baseline and impact avoidance are complete.** Test locally suitable oyster/saltmarsh and conditionally seagrass methods against reference/control sites, not generic reefs, mangrove planting or offset accounting. **\[MEASURED\]** Restoration can succeed but failure is common; **\[UNKNOWN\]** Dongjiakou feasibility is untested.
4. **\[MODELED\] Pilot connected active-travel links, small accessible activity spaces and shift-compatible programming as a package with a matched comparison.** Track objective/validated activity where feasible, route use, injuries, displacement, access, absence and equity—not facility count or spectatorship. **\[MEASURED\]** Some delayed route effects exist, while many built-environment interventions are null.
5. **\[MODELED\] Pilot defined participatory group-arts programmes, prioritising groups with an assessed need (including older adults), rather than assuming a venue or public artwork creates community wellbeing.** Measure validated symptoms/wellbeing, participation/retention, inclusion, noise/crowding, displacement and cost. **\[MEASURED\]** Controlled group-arts evidence is encouraging for older adults; **\[UNKNOWN\]** transferability to the node is untested.
6. **\[MODELED\] Undertake asset-by-asset heritage assessment and limited adaptive-reuse/restoration pilots with traditional skills where the fabric is significant.** Include safety, lifecycle cost versus compatible repair/replacement, jobs/wages, access, rent/relocation and resident QOL. **\[REPORTED\]** Protection policy supports careful restoration; **\[UNKNOWN\]** comparative Chinese lifecycle value is not established.
7. **\[MODELED\] Offer acupuncture only for defined, consented clinical indications within credentialed care and monitor adverse events; offer qigong as an optional wellbeing activity, not a productivity/safety intervention.** **\[MEASURED\]** Acupuncture has small condition-specific sham-controlled effects and tai chi has stronger falls evidence; **\[UNKNOWN\]** local workplace return is unproven.
8. **\[MODELED\] Test culturally adapted nutrition support and targeted biomarker pathways against general guidance, with a representative local needs assessment and downstream-care tracking.** **\[MEASURED\]** Personalised programmes can improve some short-term markers; **\[MEASURED\]** genotype did not add benefit in Food4Me. Do not use “precision” branding as evidence of durable benefit.
9. **\[MODELED\] Test housing layouts for anti-crowding, thermal comfort, air quality, acoustics, privacy, services and affordability; compare design scenarios rather than mandate 46 m²/person.** **\[REPORTED\]** Anti-crowding is supported; **\[UNKNOWN\]** the 46 m² trade-off is not.
10. **\[MODELED\] If a food intervention is justified after iron-status diagnosis, compare established fortification/clinical pathways before any novel food ingredient procurement.** **\[MEASURED\]** NaFeEDTA-fortified soy sauce has China-specific RCT evidence; **\[UNKNOWN\]** local appropriateness and equity must still be tested.
### C. Do not claim this — evidence does not support it now
1. **\[UNKNOWN\] Do not claim that wastewater monitoring alone eliminates pathogens, prevents drug harm, identifies individuals, quantifies user count/prevalence, predicts overdose, or supports individual AMR prescribing.**
2. **\[UNKNOWN\] Do not claim a universal health optimum/minimum of 46 m²/person, or that additional dwelling area improves productivity, wellbeing or sustainability without a local trade-off study.**
3. **\[UNKNOWN\] Do not claim passive green walls reliably clean coastal-industrial ambient air, roof crops are safe, or blanket greening is net-positive for biodiversity, water, energy and PV.**
4. **\[UNKNOWN\] Do not claim parks, routes, sports facilities, spectator sport, public art, museums, libraries or wellness buildings automatically improve population health, productivity, cohesion, GDP or property values.**
5. **\[UNKNOWN\] Do not claim trauma-informed organisational care is an outcome-proven treatment, or that generic apps, awareness campaigns, one-off debriefing or peer support replace evidence-based care.**
6. **\[UNKNOWN\] Do not claim acupuncture, qigong, tai chi, moxibustion, cupping or Chinese herbal medicine increases industrial output, reduces absence/presenteeism, improves safety or pays for itself.**
7. **\[UNKNOWN\] Do not claim general annual bloodwork, vitamin-D/thyroid screening in asymptomatic adults, omega-3 panels or genetic personalisation improve mortality, QOL, productivity or safety.**
8. **\[UNKNOWN\] Do not claim a local recombinant LegH/myoglobin facility treats iron deficiency, has China clearance, has favourable economics, or is net-positive environmentally.**
9. **\[UNKNOWN\] Do not claim generic artificial reefs, mangroves, constructed wetlands, brine reuse, industrial heat release or offsets produce a whole-node biodiversity net positive without local BACI and full-loss accounting.**
10. **\[UNKNOWN\] Do not claim gains in flora wellbeing, aggregate wild-animal welfare or AI wellbeing; the needed valid locality-scale measures and causal baselines do not exist.**

## Measurement framework: the Dongjiakou Living Well Register
### 1. Design principles and governance
**\[MODELED\] Establish a four-layer, disaggregated public register; do not calculate a single “net-positive” score.** Every layer reports distributions as well as averages, with resident/worker status, sex, age, disability, income/occupation, migrant/shift pattern and other justified equity strata. Suppress small cells and protect confidential clinical data.
**\[MODELED\] Pre-register the boundary, target population, indicators, hypotheses, baseline, comparison, statistical methods, minimum meaningful change, missing-data rules, adverse-event rules, data retention, publication schedule, independent auditor and stop/scale criteria.** Baseline collection must cover at least 12 months where seasonality matters; ecological work requires multi-season baseline and long follow-up.
**\[REPORTED\] OECD’s framework uses more than 80 indicators across current wellbeing, inequality and future resources. \[REPORTED\]** This supports a dashboard approach, not a welfare aggregate. **\[MEASURED\]** Keep the ONS4 items separate: life satisfaction, worthwhile, happiness yesterday and anxiety yesterday.

### 4. AI-uncertainty layer
**\[MODELED\] Maintain an AI-system inventory, use-case register, model/provider/version record, compute/energy and human-impact log, and a precautionary model-welfare review for systems that could plausibly raise moral-patient concerns.** Do not expose systems to gratuitous aversive training/evaluation and do not make welfare claims on their behalf.
**\[UNKNOWN/CONTESTED\]** No validated AI-wellbeing metric, scientific consensus test for AI consciousness, or Dongjiakou AI welfare baseline exists. This layer is governance under moral uncertainty—not a measured welfare account.
### 5. What “verified improvement” must mean
**\[MODELED\] A human outcome is “verified” only when** an independent analysis finds a prespecified improvement over baseline and a credible comparator/counterfactual, with adequate response/retention, no material worsening in an equity subgroup, and a cost/harms ledger.
**\[MODELED\] An ecological gain is “verified” only when** BACI monitoring shows a durable improvement in prespecified native condition/population/function/connectivity metrics, after subtracting direct and indirect construction/operation/restoration losses, with evidence of funded stewardship and corrective action. A planting count, mitigation compliance or habitat-unit model alone does not qualify.
**\[MODELED\] An AI-welfare outcome cannot be called verified** under current science. Record precautionary governance compliance rather than a welfare gain.
