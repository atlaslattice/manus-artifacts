## 5. The honest caveats
This is a discovery, not a receipt, and the caveats are load-bearing.
**One — incrementality is ****`UNKNOWN-BUT-LEAD`****.** The register asks "is the cold energy incremental?" and answers: the Sinopec Qingdao station has operated since 2014, the cascade is described as Shandong's *first* such project (suggesting incremental recovery), **but this was not definitively confirmed.** If the 26 GWh is already inside the 690 GWh baseline, it closes nothing. **This is the single test that decides the first correction.**
**Two — annual is not hourly.** Cold is released when LNG is regasified, which peaks in winter heating season. The mineral train runs continuously. **Closing an annual deficit is not the same as running a train**, and the register's hourly-matching discipline applies here as much as anywhere. This is the same failure the compute lane already guards against.
**Three — the 8 GWh is counterfactual.** It counts as deficit-closing only if that cooling would genuinely otherwise have been purchased.
