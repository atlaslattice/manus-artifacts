## 5. The Notion and Zapier execution flow
Per the standing workflow:
```plain text
1. Manus produces an artifact in the build tree
        ↓
2. Manus dumps it to Notion in full — ALWAYS, nothing held back
        ↓
3. The artifact is marked UNREVIEWED
        ↓
4. GPT and DeepSeek review it in Notion
        ↓
5. Corrections applied — cheap, because the archive is private
        ↓
6. On approval by BOTH, the register promotes it
        ↓
7. Only then does it reach git and, for Tier 3, House 11
```
**Zapier's role:** bridge the Notion review state to the next actor. A review request should trigger on new UNREVIEWED artifacts, and an approval should trigger the promotion step. The specific Zapier wiring is not yet built and is a task for the user or for a dedicated setup pass.
**What the MCP layer already does:** the Notion MCP connection is live and working. 82 documents were dumped this session with zero failures. The git side is manual because of the 2FA issue, which is why GPT pushing to git after approval remains the current bridge.

## 7. The first thing this map should do
**Module 01 has a blocking question, and the user has now asked it directly.** The request was to promote items *"from UNKNOWN even if UNVERIFIED."*
That is a change to the promotion boundary, and the boundary is a red line. **The honest position:** `UNKNOWN` and `UNVERIFIED` are different states, and collapsing them would destroy the register's only real asset — its ability to distinguish what is known from what is hoped.
**A defensible path exists**, and it preserves the distinction:
> Introduce **`UNVERIFIED_LEAD`** as a fourth state. An item may be promoted as a `LEAD` with zero credit, carrying its source and its confidence level. It appears in the register, it is visible and actionable, and **it still cannot carry credit until a receipt closes.**
That gives the user the visibility they want without trading away the discipline that has already caught four falsified premises, a 125× block-mass error, an 11-45× energy error and a 261 GWh land/sea ambiguity.
**Recommendation: adopt ****`UNVERIFIED_LEAD`****, and do not collapse ****`UNKNOWN`**** into ****`UNVERIFIED`****.** This is the single most consequential decision in this document.
