## 2. AGR01 is not a side module — it is an integration surface
AGR01 should be treated as the food/nutrient ledger that closes loops among the existing Dongjiakou subnodes.
### W01 → R01 → AGR01
**W01** supplies the desalination concentrate state. **R01** is the separation/refinery layer for K, Mg, Ca and sulfate. AGR01 is the qualified local/regional sink for fertilizer products after chemistry, energy, product-quality and regulatory gates.
Candidate outputs:
- K salts / KCl-equivalent → K nutrition where deficient
- Mg → struvite reagent and qualified Mg fertilizers
- CaSO4 / gypsum → direct soil-amendment/S route or regional acid feed after qualification
- sulfate products → plant-available sulfur products where agronomically justified
No inventory becomes fertilizer credit merely because AGR01 exists.

Intended loop:
**LDC01 / AGR01 residues + wastewater → BIO01 digestion / treatment → biogas + nutrient-rich sidestream → struvite / digestate polishing → AGR01 fertigation or qualified nutrient product**
LDC01 is particularly important because it anchors a real food-processing facility inside the Node-001 boundary. Its wastewater/residue composition remains UNKNOWN, so the loop is physically plausible but zero-credit.

## 4. Corrected sulfur aggregation threshold
DeepSeek's proposed arithmetic used **\~25–30 kt/y as gypsum feed per node**. That mixes two different quantities.
The current **generic-seawater stoichiometric sensitivity** is:
- **62.7–76.9 kt/y gypsum-equivalent ceiling per standardized Node-001** — derived from 14.6–17.9 kt/y generic-seawater-modeled Ca at 100% Ca capture; NOT a site-specific Dongjiakou production estimate
- applying the external Chinese comparator ratio of \~0.4 t H2SO4 per t gypsum gives **25.1–30.8 kt/y sulfuric-acid output sensitivity per node-equivalent**
Therefore the aggregation threshold depends on the chosen **gypsum-feed capacity**, not the acid-output number.
