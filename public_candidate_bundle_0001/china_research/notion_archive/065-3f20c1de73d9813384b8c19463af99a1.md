## 4. Product quality and the nutrient-offset test
### 4.1 The applicable product benchmarks
**\[REPORTED\] The provincial agriculture-department summary of NY/T 525-2021 says commercial organic fertiliser requires at least 30% organic matter and at least 4% total nutrient (total N + P₂O₅ + K₂O); it also adds a seed-germination index of at least 70%.** The 4% threshold is a combined nutrient floor, not a specification for individual N, P or K. Source: \[S9\].
**\[REPORTED\] Qingdao’s 2025 straw-compost subsidy specification is less stringent than the national product benchmark: at least 25% organic matter, at least 3% N+P₂O₅+K₂O, and moisture no more than 40%.** This is a programme compost-quality condition, not proof that a saleable, registered NY/T 525 product has been made. Source: \[S8\].
**\[REPORTED\] The NY/T 525-2021 summary divides raw materials into allowed, assessed, and prohibited categories and lists sewage sludge, coal ash, steel slag, unsorted municipal waste, and materials with safety risks among prohibited raw materials.** This makes the mixed industrial wastewater/sludge route materially different from straw/manure or segregated food residues. Source: \[S9\].
**\[REPORTED\] A 2025 digestate review concludes that nutrient composition and fertilising performance vary materially with feedstock and process conditions: manure/slurry digestates are generally richer in N, P and K than crop digestates, while crop digestates tend to be richer in organic carbon.** This is a literature synthesis, not an assay for this node; it supports testing rather than assigning a generic NPK value. Source: \[S16\].

## 6. CO₂ routing: greenhouse enrichment and algal bioreactor
### 6.1 CO₂ available from biogas upgrading
**\[REPORTED\] The CAS centre says raw biogas generally contains about 55% methane and 45% CO₂, and its pressure-water-wash system reports methane recovery above 98.5% and CO₂ recovery above 70%.** This is a supplier/institute performance assertion. It does not provide a Dongjiakou gas-flow rate, CO₂ purity certificate, compression/storage specification, or annual captured-CO₂ mass. Source: \[S3\].
**\[UNKNOWN\] The amount, pressure, purity, H₂S/volatile contaminant content, temporal profile and destination of recoverable Dongjiakou CO₂ are unknown.** A company recovery percentage cannot be applied to a node with no measured biogas flow or upgrading design. Sources: \[S3\] \[S5\] \[S7\].
### 6.2 Greenhouse enrichment evidence
**\[MEASURED\] A 2023 peer-reviewed pilot that captured CO₂ from biomass combustion flue gas by adsorption reported 99.99% removal of harmful gases from supplied gas and an 18% tomato productivity increase versus a control over a November–February test period.** The experiment is a pilot-scale greenhouse outside the Dongjiakou case and used purified biomass flue gas, not direct raw biogas-upgrading off-gas. Source: \[S15\].
**\[REPORTED\] A 2022 peer-reviewed review reports that daytime greenhouse CO₂ can fall to 100–250 µmol mol⁻¹, while an often cited crop-growth target range is 800–1,000 µmol mol⁻¹; it also warns that combustion-derived gas needs cooling and purification for NOₓ, SO₂ and CO.** This is general literature synthesis, not a measured condition at Dongjiakou. Source: \[S17\].
**\[MODELED\] A purified, food-safe CO₂ stream could be evaluated for a nearby controlled-environment greenhouse only after gas-specification testing, crop/area demand modelling, seasonal heat/ventilation matching, and a safety case.** No public evidence shows a greenhouse at the node, a CO₂ distribution system, or an offtake agreement. Sources: \[S3\] \[S15\] \[S17\].
### 6.3 Algal photobioreactor evidence
**\[MEASURED\] In a 2015 experimental study, a methane-rich atmosphere did not affect *Nannochloropsis gaditana* development; CO₂ inhibition appeared when culture was exposed to 9% CO₂. The authors found direct gas–culture contact unsuitable for upgrading because oxygen desorbed into the product gas, while a two-stage photobioreactor plus gas/liquid mass-transfer configuration removed CO₂ efficiently while keeping oxygen low.** This is laboratory/experimental evidence, not commercial-scale deployment and not a Dongjiakou demonstration. Source: \[S20\].
**\[MODELED\] An algae route could couple CO₂ uptake with nutrient recovery from a suitably clean liquid fraction, but it should be treated as a pilot research package rather than a credited fertiliser or carbon offset.** Gas transfer, light, land/footprint, biomass product eligibility, salinity, contaminants, seasonal productivity, harvesting energy and market route are all unknown for the node. Source: \[S20\].
**\[UNKNOWN\] No source found a Zhongke Juneng or Dongjiakou commercial deployment that routes upgraded-biogas CO₂ to a greenhouse or algal bioreactor.** Sources: \[S2\] \[S3\] \[S4\].
## 7. China-scale deployment and policy alignment
### 7.1 National commercial context, including disagreement
**\[REPORTED\] A 2025 industry-conference account attributed to the National Agricultural Technology Extension Service says China had more than 4,500 bio-organic fertiliser enterprises, more than 45 Mt/y output, 15,010 registered products, annual output value above RMB 40 billion and application on more than 500 million mu.** It also reported current use around 40 Mt pure nutrient, or 59.5% of a 67.198 Mt resource base. These are speaker-reported sector figures, not an official statistical series reproduced with methodology. Source: \[S19\].
**\[REPORTED\] A 2023 literature review instead cites 2021 organic-fertiliser output of 16.2 Mt and demand of 15.7 Mt, based on secondary market data.** It also states that 76.2% of producers had capacity below 20,000 t/y and only 3.8% exceeded 100,000 t/y. Source: \[S14\].
**\[REPORTED\] These two sector estimates conflict materially and appear to use different dates and potentially different product scopes (bio-organic category versus organic-fertiliser market series).** They should not be merged into a single national capacity number without their original methodologies. Sources: \[S19\] \[S14\].
**\[REPORTED\] MARA reports national straw generation of 865 Mt/y, livestock-manure generation of 3.05 Bt/y, straw comprehensive utilisation above 88%, and manure comprehensive utilisation of 79.4%.** These national statistics establish scale and policy direction, not feedstock availability at Dongjiakou. Source: \[S11\].
### 7.2 Registration and incentives
**\[REPORTED\] China’s fertiliser-registration rule says unregistered fertiliser may not be imported, produced, sold, used or advertised; applications require chemical, efficacy, safety and label materials plus representative samples, and normally require field trials in China.** Products with a national/industry standard may receive corresponding field-test reductions. Source: \[S10\].
**\[REPORTED\] The rule assigns provincial agricultural authorities responsibility for registration and certificates for refined organic fertiliser (精制有机肥), while MARA governs national fertiliser registration and oversight.** The exact category and route for a proposed digestate product therefore require regulator confirmation before commercial claims. Source: \[S10\].
**\[REPORTED\] Qingdao’s 2025 scheme creates a potential straw-input incentive of up to RMB 50/t for energy use and up to RMB 220/t for off-field composting, with local-origin and inspection conditions.** It is an incentive for qualifying straw activity, not a blanket subsidy for digestion, sludge handling, finished product, carbon capture, or imported feedstock. Source: \[S8\].
