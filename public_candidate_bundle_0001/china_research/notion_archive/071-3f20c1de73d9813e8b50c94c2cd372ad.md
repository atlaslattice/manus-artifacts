- **REPORTED — Chinese and Shandong HIL demand exists at laboratory/procurement level, especially for renewable/grid and powertrain controls.** **UNKNOWN —** a Dongjiakou serviceable market, capacity shortage, price, utilisation, anchor customers, and a material 40 MW/15 °C HIL heat load were not evidenced.

## Decisive question — continuous operation or seasonal operation?
> **Availability verdict: UNKNOWN quantitatively; REPORTED as winter-peaked/dispatchable.** The public record does **not** establish that Qingdao regasification and recoverable external cold are continuous, and it also does **not** establish that they occur only during a defined 4–6-month season.
- **REPORTED —** Sinopec describes Qingdao as serving winter peak shaving and reports a 56 million m³/day winter-peak full-export capability; a heating-season snapshot reported more than 20 million m³/day average send-out to date.
- **REPORTED —** 2024 aggregates of 5.53 Mt received and more than 6.5 bcm gasified/exported demonstrate material annual activity, not a load-duration curve.
- **MODELED —** converting those aggregates to approximately 4,400 receipt and more than 3,450 gas-supply full-load-equivalent hours does **not** identify the calendar hours, number of start/stops, load profile, or alignment with downstream demand.
- **UNKNOWN —** no Qingdao monthly send-out curve, hourly vaporizer flow, mode log, annual vaporizer run-hour total, or cold-exergy balance was located. A reported 2021 inventory incident with a potential send-out cessation also shows why nominal terminal capacity is not an availability guarantee; it is not an annual outage statistic.
### Effect on the cascade
- **UNKNOWN —** the integrated cascade cannot be credited as a continuously running source of cold until the upstream interface has a demonstrated minimum firm duty, temperature, ramp envelope, and availability profile.
- **MODELED —** if upstream cold is intermittent or falls below downstream requirement, the cascade ceases to be the sole cooling/refrigeration source; the ASU, freeze train, and computing facility would need a defined turndown, thermal-storage, bypass, or mechanically backed-up operating mode.
- **UNKNOWN —** no evidence was found that such storage, bypass, mechanical fallback, automatic switchover, or contractual cold-delivery SLA has been specified or demonstrated at Dongjiakou.
- **REPORTED —** winter peaking is a reason to test seasonal operation and inventory/cold-storage scenarios; it is not evidence that a 40 MW facility can operate continuously from LNG cold.
## Ranked prerequisites for the cascade to work
The following are **conditions that would need to be demonstrated**, ranked by their ability to invalidate the integrated concept. A condition marked **FALSIFIED** is limited to the stated premise; it does not rule out a redesigned alternative.
1. **UNKNOWN — Firm, time-resolved, externally recoverable cold must exist at the terminal.** At least two full years of 15-minute or hourly LNG flow, vaporizer mode/duty, gas send-out, tank inventory, truck loading, planned/unplanned outage, and external-recovery data must show a minimum available duty at required temperatures. Annual LNG volumes and nameplate capacity cannot substitute.
2. **UNKNOWN — The ASU must have a commissioned, defined, and contractually allocable downstream cold stream after its own needs.** A PFD/H&MB, actual heat-exchanger topology, temperature/pressure schedule, LNG flow, duty, product mix, ASU electric load, and performance test must prove that cold remains available to the next stage. An announced capacity is not a cold-balance result.
3. **FALSIFIED IF THIS IS THE DESIGN PREMISE — An actual approximately −20 °C utility alone cannot take a binary NaCl–water stream to its reported −21.1 °C eutectic.** **MODELED —** a finite heat-transfer driving force requires a colder source. If eutectic operation is required, the design must provide a colder utility, mechanical refrigeration, or a different stopping concentration. **UNKNOWN —** the Dongjiakou ASU outlet and real multicomponent-brine phase path are not known, so this does not falsify an alternative design.
4. **UNKNOWN — The freeze block must demonstrate site-specific production, not merely low-TDS laboratory ice.** A pilot on the actual RO brine must close the mass balance for raw ice, wash/recycle/purge, product water, crystals/mother liquor, and auxiliaries while testing the complete target water-quality suite, fouling/control stability, and residual-disposal route. **FALSIFIED AS A QUALITY LOGIC —** TDS alone cannot establish potable compliance under GB 5749-2022.
5. **FALSIFIED AS A DIRECT-CONNECTION DESIGN BASIS — A −5 to +5 °C industrial stream should not be sent directly to server cold plates.** **REPORTED —** OCP/ASHRAE guidance supports isolation of facility and technology loops and temperature control above dew point. The viable prerequisite is a liquid-to-liquid HX/CDU, treated secondary technology loop, dew-point controls, residual-air cooling, and independently available backup heat rejection; the sizes and performance remain **UNKNOWN**.
6. **UNKNOWN — The 40 MW load must be a real, compatible, reliable offtaker rather than a nominal heat sink.** Selected racks must specify the approved supply/return conditions and liquid heat-capture fraction; the data centre must quantify residual air/non-IT cooling and demonstrate failover when LNG cold is unavailable. HIL/PHIL must separately establish equipment rating, operating hours, heat load, and cooling-temperature needs; public evidence does not support HIL as a material 40 MW load.
7. **UNKNOWN — A market must exist for the HIL component and for the ASU/freeze products at the required scale and duty cycle.** Named customers, signed demand, service pricing, utilisation, data/IP governance, and evidence that existing Jinan/Hefei/grid/OEM capacity does not satisfy the need are required. Public procurements and academic laboratories establish use cases, not a Dongjiakou revenue model.
8. **UNKNOWN — The electrical and final brine/heat-rejection balances must close under project-specific inputs.** The planned 214 MW wind cannot yet be classified onshore/offshore and planned wind/PV generation is undisclosed. LSRRO and MVR/MVC figures are literature/model ranges, not local metered performance. Final residual salt/mother-liquor and ambient-rejection designs need chemistry, permits, equipment duty, and energy meters.

## Three most important remaining unknowns — ordered
1. **UNKNOWN — Firm cold-source availability and recoverable duty.** The missing hourly terminal/vaporizer and exergy data determine whether there is any continuously usable upstream stream at all, and whether the nominal cold resource is already consumed, rejected, or unavailable at the desired temperature.
2. **UNKNOWN — An integrated, measured thermodynamic and mass balance from LNG through ASU and freeze concentration.** The project needs actual interface temperatures, flows, duties, pinch points, ASU allocation, brine chemistry/phase behavior, wash/recycle/purge, auxiliary electricity, and residual disposition. Without this, neither the 14 million m³/year claim nor a downstream +15 °C load can be sized.
3. **UNKNOWN — A resilient and bankable 40 MW downstream offtake.** The data-centre/HIL stage needs selected hardware, heat-capture and thermal specifications, pipe/HX/CDU/backup design, cold-source loss response, metering boundary, anchor customers, HIL service demand, pricing, and availability requirements. Generic DLC feasibility and discrete HIL demand do not establish this node-scale load.
## Bottom-line decision statement
**UNKNOWN — The public evidence does not show a continuously operating Dongjiakou five-stage cold cascade.** **REPORTED —** it shows an active, winter-peaked LNG terminal; an announced LNG-cold ASU; laboratory/pilot evidence for freeze desalination; technically credible DLC interface practices; and discrete HIL demand signals. **MEASURED —** selected experiments and field facilities establish component-level possibilities, not an integrated plant outcome. **MODELED —** several useful scenario inputs exist, but they cannot replace the missing source-duty, thermodynamic, operational, market, and resilience data. The justified next step is evidence-gated measurement and piloting, not presentation of the proposal as an operating finding.
