> **Evidence-label key.** **MEASURED** = an instrument measured a signal in a study. **REPORTED** = an agency, programme, or observational study reported an association or operational result. **MODELED** = a calculation, mechanistic inference, or proposed design, not an observed outcome. **UNKNOWN** = no adequate evidence or site-specific number was found. “Detectable” means a laboratory detected a target or biomarker; it does **not** mean a person, prevalence, harm, viability, or causal outcome was detected.
## Executive summary
- **MEASURED / REPORTED — strongest finding:** Wastewater-based epidemiology (WBE) is a credible **population-level trend and early-warning instrument**, but it is not a diagnostic test and it cannot by itself identify people, count users, or reliably convert a concentration into disease prevalence or drug consumption at a new locality. Its most defensible use is to complement clinical, laboratory, pharmacy, and community data—not replace them. \[1\] \[2\]
- **MEASURED — pathogen sensitivity is target- and method-specific:** In Hong Kong, China, daily sampling at 12 plants covering about 80% of the population measured SARS-CoV-2 with wastewater limits of detection of **828–3,125 copies/L**, and observed two epidemic peaks three days before reported cases. That is strong trend evidence, not a transferable Dongjiakou case threshold. \[3\]
- **MODELED / MEASURED — there is no universal minimum population:** WHO’s polio guidance gives an *idealised* 100-fold-concentration calculation of one excretor among 10,000 uninfected people, while warning that the assumptions do not hold perfectly in practice. A SARS-CoV-2 study reported detection thresholds of 4–97 new reported cases/100,000 depending on method. These figures cannot be converted into a Dongjiakou population requirement without sewer, flow, shedding, and recovery data. \[4\] \[5\]
- **MEASURED / REPORTED — drug surveillance is mature for methamphetamine and several stimulants:** A 2015 Chinese study measured methamphetamine and ketamine at 36 treatment plants in 18 major cities; the international SCORE network has coordinated annual one-week campaigns since 2011. WBE describes aggregate load trends, not the number, identity, frequency, or health of users. \[6\] \[7\] \[2\]
- **MEASURED / UNKNOWN — fentanyl can be measured but is not reliably interpretable as local prevalence:** China’s 2016–2019 study across 30 cities detected fentanyl in only a few samples; China’s 2024 national drug report said its urban-sewage and hair monitoring found no *large-scale* fentanyl-class misuse. No transferable fentanyl/analogue concentration, detection probability, or prevalence-conversion factor for Dongjiakou was found. \[8\] \[9\]
- **REPORTED / MIXED EVIDENCE — a signal can support action, but outcome proof is scarce:** Israel detected wild poliovirus in sewage before paralytic cases and launched a supplementary vaccination campaign; a North Carolina opioid pilot reported more than 30 community conversations and a twofold increase in returned medicines. Neither is controlled evidence that WBE itself eliminated a pathogen, reduced drug use, overdose, or drug harm. \[10\] \[11\]
- **REPORTED — China already uses WBE, including for enforcement:** China’s Ministry of Public Security reports national urban wastewater drug-content monitoring; *Nature* documented dozens of Chinese cities using sewage analysis to police illicit drugs. A health programme without a published purpose limitation and law-enforcement firewall has a real mission-creep and stigma risk. \[9\] \[12\]
- **UNKNOWN — no Dongjiakou annual budget can be evidenced:** EUDA gives **EUR100–200 per drug sample** as a general analysis price. SARS-CoV-2 pilot operating costs were **US\$6,175–8,272/month** in Blantyre and **US\$16,756–30,050/month** in Kathmandu, with materially different designs. Neither is a price quote for Qingdao or a multi-analyte site-year budget. \[2\] \[13\]
## Direct answer
**MEASURED / REPORTED / UNKNOWN — PARTIALLY SUPPORTED.** A locality-scale programme can credibly reveal **changes in aggregate exposure or circulation** for selected, analytically validated targets, and it can sometimes lead public-health teams to investigate, communicate, vaccinate, or target services. The evidence is strongest for SARS-CoV-2 trend surveillance, established poliovirus environmental surveillance, and stimulant-load surveillance. It is substantially weaker for converting a single measurement into prevalence, drug-user numbers, individual risk, antimicrobial-resistant organism (AMRO) disease burden, fentanyl prevalence, or a demonstrated health/harm-reduction outcome.
**UNKNOWN:** The available evidence does not establish that monitoring the Dongjiakou node would be net-positive across health, dignity, ecology, and quality-of-life metrics. That depends on sewer connectivity, industrial inputs, governance, action pathways, community consent/legitimacy, and measured outcomes that have not been supplied or found.
## What WBE measures—and the critical gap to an outcome
**MEASURED:** At a wastewater-treatment-plant inlet, a programme usually measures chemical residues or pathogen-specific nucleic-acid fragments in a pooled sewage sample. For infection targets, the target often is RNA or DNA rather than a viable pathogen. \[1\]
**MODELED:** Drug load is commonly calculated as measured concentration × wastewater flow, then adjusted by an excretion factor and divided by the contributing population. Each term creates uncertainty: composite representativeness, chemical stability, sewer travel, flow, analytical recovery, human excretion, direct disposal, and the *de facto* rather than census population. \[2\]
**REPORTED:** EUDA states that drug WBE cannot provide prevalence, frequency of use, number/type of users, or drug purity. It identifies the method as complementary to established monitoring, not a substitute. \[2\]
**REPORTED:** The National Academies similarly concludes that pathogen concentrations can be confounded by variability in shedding and timing, pathogen stability, and other factors. A concentration is therefore best interpreted as a within-sewershed trend with uncertainty, then checked against clinical and other surveillance streams. \[1\]
**MODELED:** The causal chain is: **sample → validated signal → interpreted trend → pre-agreed public-health response → uptake/coverage → clinical or social outcome**. Evidence at the first two steps is frequently stronger than at the last three. A detected signal should trigger confirmatory and context-sensitive public-health work; it should not trigger a claim that the programme has improved health.
## Sensitivity, quantification, and population scale
### Analytical limit versus epidemiological sensitivity
**MEASURED:** The Hong Kong SARS-CoV-2 programme used 24-hour composite samples and RT-qPCR. It reported wastewater limits of detection of **3,125 copies/L** for its N1 assay and **828 copies/L** for its HKU-05 assay. These are laboratory-method limits for that workflow, not a universal virus or population threshold. \[3\]
**REPORTED:** A study of SARS-CoV-2 wastewater sensitivity reported that the number of daily *reported* new cases required for detection varied by analytical method: **4–17 (median 8), 9–43 (median 18), and 17–97 (median 38) cases per 100,000 population**. Reported cases are themselves incomplete and policy-dependent, so these are observational detection thresholds rather than infection-prevalence thresholds. \[5\]
**MODELED:** WHO’s poliovirus guidance states that, if its ideal assumptions hold and the sample is concentrated 100-fold, a system *might* detect one infected individual among 10,000 uninfected people. The same guidance says perfect performance will not occur in real life and that one or a few excretors are unlikely to make a large-catchment sample positive; repeated positives are more informative for circulation. \[4\]
**REPORTED:** In 2022–2023 United States poliovirus surveillance, CDC stated that PCR sensitivity and limits of detection in wastewater had not yet been defined, and that catchment choice, timing of shedding, infected-person count, sample concentration, and method variation affect detection. \[14\]
**UNKNOWN:** No peer-reviewed evidence was found that specifies the population size, flow rate, dilution factor, composite-sampling schedule, or concentrations detectable at Dongjiakou. No defensible minimum population can therefore be asserted.

## Drug surveillance
### Methamphetamine and other established targets
**MEASURED:** China has conducted city-scale drug WBE for at least a decade. The 2015 nationwide reconnaissance sampled influent and effluent at **36 sewage treatment plants in 18 major cities** and measured methamphetamine and ketamine. Mean city methamphetamine loads ranged from **12.5 ± 14.9 to 181.2 ± 6.5 mg/1,000 inhabitants/day**; mean ketamine loads ranged from **\<0.2 to 89.6 ± 27.4 mg/1,000 inhabitants/day**. \[6\]
**MEASURED:** The same study found apparent methamphetamine removal greater than 80% at most plants and ketamine removal below 50% at most plants, with negative apparent ketamine removal at some plants. This demonstrates why influent—not treated effluent—should be the default surveillance matrix and why chemical fate needs local validation. \[6\]
**REPORTED:** SCORE has coordinated an annual, one-week campaign since 2011. Its current monitoring page states that 143 wastewater plants in 120 cities and 37 countries participated, analysing cocaine, amphetamine, methamphetamine, and MDMA using validated laboratory methods and inter-laboratory quality checks. \[7\]
**REPORTED:** EUDA’s 2025 European study covered 115 cities and reports geographical and temporal load patterns; methamphetamine was historically concentrated in Czechia and Slovakia but signals were also observed in Germany, Lithuania, Norway, Türkiye, Spain, Cyprus, and the Netherlands. It is a real-world programme for trend comparison, not a census of use. \[17\]
### Opioids, fentanyl, and fentanyl analogues
**MEASURED:** A North Carolina pilot deployed 24-hour, hourly composite sampling at 10 residential manholes covering 44.5% of one municipality. Targeted mass spectrometry detected all 11 targeted opioid/treatment/reversal metabolites; the study reported mean total exposure of **49.1 ± 31.9 morphine-milligram-equivalents/day/1,000 people**. This demonstrates local multi-opioid signal detection, not an overdose prediction or harm-reduction outcome. \[18\]
**MEASURED:** China’s 2016–2019 study sampled wastewater in 30 cities across all seven geographic regions. Its abstract reports fentanyl in only a few samples, which the authors interpreted as low prevalence in that sampling frame; it reports tramadol, not fentanyl, at concentrations up to **186 ng/L**. \[8\]
**REPORTED:** China’s Ministry of Public Security states that national-drug-laboratory urban-wastewater and hair monitoring found no **large-scale** fentanyl-class misuse in 2024. This is an official surveillance conclusion, not proof of zero fentanyl use and not a measured Dongjiakou concentration. \[9\]
**MEASURED / UNKNOWN:** Fentanyl and norfentanyl can be targeted by sensitive LC–MS/MS workflows, and published wastewater studies exist. However, no robust universal concentration range, limit of quantification, probability of detection at a specified number of users, or conversion from norfentanyl/fentanyl load to fentanyl prevalence was found that can be transferred to Dongjiakou. The available Chinese nationwide result instead shows sparse detection. \[8\] \[2\]
**UNKNOWN:** No evidence was found that fentanyl **analogues as a class** can be monitored reliably by a fixed panel over time. New analogues may not be included in a targeted assay; changing drug markets, reference-standard availability, metabolite knowledge, recovery, and degradation can produce false negatives or incomparable time series. A panel should therefore be reviewed through an accredited analytical laboratory and reported as “targets covered,” never as “all fentanyl analogues.”
**REPORTED:** Heroin surveillance has a specific weakness: EUDA notes that 6-monoacetylmorphine is unstable in wastewater, while morphine is non-specific because therapeutic morphine also contributes. This is a concrete example of why an opioid signal cannot automatically be labelled illicit use. \[2\] 
### Direct disposal and industrial confounding
**REPORTED:** EUDA describes multiple ways to investigate direct disposal rather than consumption, including parent-to-metabolite ratios, sudden ratio changes, anomalous weekly patterns, enantiomeric composition, and synthesis-waste profiles. It also states that WBE cannot identify the location of a dump or manufacturing site. \[2\]
**MODELED:** At a port–industrial node, industrial discharge, variable shifts, temporary populations, rainfall/infiltration, process water, and potentially separate sewer networks could bias concentration and population normalisation. A network map and a pre-specified industrial-input exclusion/interpretation plan are necessary before interpreting a “community” drug trend.
**UNKNOWN:** The sewer map, domestic/industrial mixing, hydraulic residence time, stormwater configuration, treatment-plant influent access, and actual contributing population for the Dongjiakou residential and industrial areas were not provided and were not found in this research. No local drug-use conclusion is possible.
## Pathogen surveillance
### SARS-CoV-2
**MEASURED:** From February to November 2022, Hong Kong, China sampled 12 treatment plants daily for nine months, covering approximately 80% of the population. Virus concentrations correlated with reported cases and captured two peaks three days earlier. \[3\]
**REPORTED:** The Hong Kong Government reports that its programme supported source tracking and sentinel surveillance in different pandemic phases, and that its statistical models predicted new cases two to four days ahead during the fifth wave. This is programme evidence of operational use, not a controlled comparison showing fewer infections, hospitalisations, or deaths because of WBE. \[19\]
**REPORTED:** CDC operates a U.S. national network with site-, state/territory-, regional-, and national-level wastewater data for influenza A, SARS-CoV-2, and RSV. Its existence is strong evidence of operational adoption; it does not establish a single conversion from wastewater concentration to prevalence. \[20\]
### Influenza
**MEASURED / REPORTED:** A systematic review describes influenza A detection in Ottawa, Canada wastewater and reports clinical-case correlation in campus studies. It concludes that the literature and public-health-implication evidence remain limited, that standard methods are lacking, and that wastewater presence does not necessarily equal active infection or transmission. \[21\]
**REPORTED:** Hong Kong’s Centre for Health Protection ran a seasonal-influenza pilot using collected wastewater samples and reported close alignment between wastewater viral load and respiratory-sample positivity trends, with earlier detection of trend changes than healthcare-based indicators. It made influenza monitoring routine from 2025 as a complement to conventional surveillance. \[22\]
**UNKNOWN:** No directly applicable influenza wastewater concentration threshold, case-prevalence conversion factor, or Dongjiakou baseline was found.
### Polio
**REPORTED / MEASURED:** Poliovirus environmental surveillance is the most established pathogen application. In Israel in 2013, routine sewage testing found wild poliovirus type 1 in Rahat before paralytic cases; by mid-August, virus had been found in 67 sewage samples from 24 sites, and asymptomatic transmission was confirmed by fecal survey. \[10\]
**REPORTED:** In response to sewage and clinical detection, Israel began an oral-polio-vaccine supplementary campaign for children in the south on 4 August 2013 and expanded it nationwide. That is an important documented action pathway from sewage signal to intervention. \[10\]
**REPORTED / UNKNOWN:** The evidence does **not** establish that wastewater monitoring itself eliminated the virus or caused the absence of paralysis. Israel had high pre-existing IPV coverage, multiple surveillance actions, and a vaccination campaign; no controlled counterfactual isolates the monitoring effect. \[10\] \[23\]
**MEASURED / REPORTED:** In New York, wastewater surveillance retrospectively showed vaccine-derived poliovirus months before a 2022 paralytic case and identified ongoing transmission in surrounding counties. Later negative detections suggested outbreak end, but CDC explicitly says routine-sampling value outside outbreaks remains unclear and interprets negatives cautiously. \[14\]
### Antimicrobial-resistant organisms and genes
**REPORTED:** The 2022 systematic review identified 48 parallel WBE/clinical-evidence papers. It concluded that AMRO/antimicrobial-resistance-gene (ARG) wastewater surveillance is emerging, but clinical utility, sensitivity, and uniformity are poorly understood. \[15\]
**REPORTED:** Detection of ARGs often measures resistance determinants rather than a viable human pathogen. The review warns that animal sources, asymptomatic carriage, environmental bacterial multiplication, sewer fate, rain, and industrial discharge make human disease-prevalence inference difficult. \[15\]
**UNKNOWN:** No validated wastewater ARG/AMRO threshold was found at which a locality should declare an outbreak, change treatment guidance, or claim a reduction in antimicrobial resistance. Clinical microbiology and infection-prevention surveillance remain necessary.
### Enteric pathogens
**MEASURED:** Oklahoma’s state-level surveillance from July 2021 to December 2022 measured *Salmonella*, *Campylobacter*, and norovirus GII. Reported case counts and wastewater concentrations for the two bacteria had significant seasonality, and wastewater peaks associated with cases one to two weeks later. \[16\]
**MEASURED:** The study’s average weekly wastewater concentrations were 180,000 *Campylobacter*, 127,000 *Salmonella*, and 390 million norovirus particles/L. These are study- and assay-specific measurements, not target thresholds for a Chinese industrial locality. \[16\]
**UNKNOWN:** No evidence was found that wastewater enteric-pathogen monitoring alone prevented a foodborne outbreak, or that a positive sample establishes a waterborne exposure route, at the locality scale.

## Ethics, rights, legality, and the risk of policing
**REPORTED:** In typical large community sewersheds, wastewater data are aggregated and anonymous, and the National Academies considers present individual/household re-identification risk ordinarily low. The risk rises as the sample represents fewer people, such as a building, workplace, school, prison, very small residential area, or a tightly bounded industrial workforce. \[24\]
**REPORTED:** The National Academies recommends ongoing—not one-time—assessment of re-identification risk, public communication, an ethics advisory process, data-use agreements, and a strong firewall precluding law-enforcement use. Its assessment is United States-focused and cannot be treated as a legal opinion for China. \[24\]
**REPORTED:** EUDA identifies ethical risks of WBE in small communities, including workplaces, schools, music festivals, prisons, and specific neighbourhoods, because a group may be identifiable or stigmatised. \[2\]
**REPORTED:** The China context makes the policing concern concrete. The Ministry of Public Security’s 2024 national drug report says the National Drug Laboratory monitors drug content in urban wastewater, and *Nature* reported that dozens of Chinese cities were using sewage analysis to police illicit drug use. \[9\] \[12\]
**UNKNOWN:** This review found no authoritative, site-specific legal analysis of the permissions, data-controller responsibilities, retention limits, disclosure obligations, cross-agency sharing rules, procurement requirements, or remedies that would govern a Dongjiakou WBE programme. A conclusion on PRC, Shandong, Qingdao, or Dongjiakou legality would therefore be unsupported.
**MODELED:** A rights-preserving design should publish a purpose limitation: public-health surveillance and voluntary harm-reduction service planning only; prohibit individual, group, employer, landlord, immigration, and law-enforcement identification/targeting; set a minimum aggregation rule; avoid routine upstream sampling of small catchments; suppress small-cell public data; set short retention and access logs; use an independent health/ethics governance body; and publish an appeal/complaint channel. These are governance safeguards, not a claim about present law.
**MODELED:** The programme should never publicise a “drug hotspot” map at a scale likely to stigmatise a residential locality or workforce. Where a signal is elevated, the first response should be confidential confirmation and offers of non-punitive health information, naloxone access where lawful and clinically appropriate, treatment navigation, or outbreak investigation—not enforcement.
## Costs: evidence, not a Dongjiakou budget
**REPORTED:** EUDA gives a general analytical cost of approximately **EUR100–200 per drug sample**. It says total cost depends on study size/design; staffing for collection and the effort to arrange plant/laboratory participation are important costs. \[2\]
**REPORTED:** A 2021-price SARS-CoV-2 pilot reported monthly operating costs of **US\$6,175–8,272** for Blantyre, Malawi (84–336 samples/month) and **US\$16,756–30,050** for Kathmandu, Nepal (96–250 samples/month). Reported average cost per sample was **US\$25–74** and **US\$120–175**, respectively. \[13\]
**UNKNOWN:** No sampling-and-analytical cost per site per year was reported for a Dongjiakou or Qingdao multi-target programme. Do not convert the figures above into a local annual budget: their currencies, date, labour allocation, shared equipment, assay panels, sample counts, and supply chains differ.
**MODELED:** A transparent tender should separate: sampler/installation and maintenance; 24-hour composite collection; cold chain and courier; flow data; chemical and molecular consumables; LC–MS/MS or high-resolution-MS drug panel; RT-qPCR/dPCR and sequencing; culture where required for polio; AMRO culture/metagenomics; method validation; quality controls; data engineering; independent interpretation; ethics/community governance; reporting; and response capacity. The response pathway is a real programme cost, not an optional add-on.
## What a locality-scale programme would require
### A gated, health-first design
1. **UNKNOWN → required baseline:** Obtain a validated sewer-network map, all domestic/industrial connections, flow records, combined/separate sewer status, rainfall/infiltration, treatment-plant influent access, hydraulic travel times, and actual contributing population. Without these, a concentration per litre is not a defensible locality exposure trend.
2. **MODELED:** Begin with a limited public-health pilot at the largest feasible aggregated municipal influent(s), using 24-hour flow-proportional composites rather than grab samples. Do not begin with dormitory, employer, residential-block, school, or individual-facility upstream sampling.
3. **MODELED:** Build separate analytical modules: (a) stimulants/opioids by accredited LC–MS/MS or high-resolution MS, including confirmed metabolites and a reviewed fentanyl/analogue target list; (b) respiratory/enteric viruses by validated RT-qPCR or RT-dPCR, with sequencing only where a surveillance question warrants it; (c) polio only within the relevant public-health/WHO laboratory and response framework; and (d) AMRO/ARG as research or One Health trend surveillance, not a disease-prevalence service.
4. **MODELED:** Pre-register target-specific performance specifications: method detection/quantification limits, extraction recovery, matrix inhibition, surrogate/process controls, duplicate/blank criteria, storage/transport limits, flow and population normalisation, rain-event rules, and the policy for non-detects. Publish the results with uncertainty and analytic coverage.
5. **MODELED:** Create a response table **before sampling**. Examples: sustained SARS-CoV-2/influenza rise → confirm with health surveillance and risk communication; poliovirus positive → immediate public-health confirmation and immunisation response; drug trend rise → confidential service capacity and public-health communications. No response table should include police referral.
6. **MODELED:** Link the data only to aggregated clinical, pharmacy, hospital, outbreak, vaccination, sanitation, and service-uptake indicators under a governance protocol. This is how the programme can test whether it has improved outcomes rather than merely produced a dashboard.
7. **MODELED:** Audit benefits and harms at fixed intervals: lead time versus clinical indicators, false-positive/false-negative investigations, time to action, service uptake, vaccine coverage, overdose/outbreak outcomes where valid data exist, public trust, reports of stigma, access requests, data sharing, and any attempted non-health use.
### Minimum evidence standard for a claim of net-positive value
**MODELED:** Promote the programme from **UNKNOWN** only when it can show all of the following for a named target: (1) an externally checked analytical signal; (2) a documented and proportionate response; (3) a comparison against a pre-specified baseline or suitable comparator; (4) a measured public-health or service outcome; and (5) no unresolved rights, stigma, or data-misuse harm. A signal alone meets none of steps 2–5.

## NULL RESULTS
The following searches did **not** locate evidence adequate to make the claimed inference:
- **UNKNOWN — documented null result:** No controlled trial or robust quasi-experimental study was found showing that locality wastewater drug monitoring **caused** a reduction in drug use, overdose, mortality, or other drug harm. The North Carolina take-back result is an operational report without a controlled health outcome. \[11\]
- **UNKNOWN — documented null result:** No study was found showing that wastewater surveillance **alone** eliminated a pathogen. Israel and New York demonstrate detection linked to vaccination/outbreak responses, not an isolated monitoring effect. \[10\] \[14\]
- **UNKNOWN — documented null result:** No universal minimum catchment population, minimum number of infections/users, or concentration threshold was found that can validly be imported to Dongjiakou for all pathogens or drugs. \[1\] \[4\] \[5\]
