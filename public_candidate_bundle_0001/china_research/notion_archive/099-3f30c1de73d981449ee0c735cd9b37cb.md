## 3. Sulfur — potentially enormous, but zero credited today
The existing Dongjiakou published-range brine model carries approximately **109,373 t/y sulfate (SO₄)** per node.
Stoichiometrically that contains about **36.5 kt/y sulfur-equivalent atoms per node**, or about **4.38 Mt/y S-equivalent at 120 identical nodes** — roughly **45.6% of China’s 2025 elemental-sulfur imports by sulfur mass**.
**Do not count this as import displacement.** Sulfate/gypsum is already oxidized and is not automatically interchangeable with elemental sulfur used to make sulfuric acid.
The number is a **RESOURCE-INVENTORY RESEARCH CEILING** showing why these pathways deserve aggressive testing:
- direct gypsum / sulfate fertilizer;
- soil-amendment use where agronomically appropriate;
- potassium-magnesium sulfate products;
- sulfuric-acid regeneration / gypsum-to-acid routes where technically and energetically justified;
- industrial sulfate recovery from wastewater.
**Credited sulfur-import offset in v0.1: 0.**
## 4. Phosphorus / struvite — strong process evidence, missing local mass
China pilot evidence:
- \~**95% phosphate-P recovery**;
- \~**90% struvite purity** at a 25 m³/day Tianjin pilot.
Dongjiakou advantage:
- BIO01 creates a nutrient-recovery interface;
- seawater / purified brine Mg can potentially replace purchased Mg reagent;
- controlled experiments using seawater Mg have achieved **80–90% P recovery**.
Missing receipt:
- Dongjiakou time-series soluble P and NH₄ load.
Therefore:
- recovery fraction has strong comparator support;
- **annual recoverable phosphorus remains UNKNOWN**;
- conservative national import-offset credit remains **0** until local nutrient mass is bounded.
Struvite should still be pursued because it can reduce virgin phosphate fertilizer demand and may indirectly reduce sulfuric-acid/sulfur demand even where China is not strongly phosphate-import dependent.
## 5. Nitrogen — reduce demand and energy, not imports
China is not meaningfully dependent on imported urea in the current trade data.
AGR01 should therefore optimize nitrogen through:
- BIO01 nutrient recycling;
- legume rotations / intercropping;
- precision soil testing and variable-rate application;
- piezo/perovskite + conventional sensor fusion for continuous water/nutrient control;
- research into piezo-/photo-catalytic nitrogen chemistry;
- reduced N losses / runoff / N₂O.
No imported-urea offset should be used to inflate the strategic result.
## 6. AGR01 self-sufficiency stack
The target is not one miracle fertilizer. It is simultaneous numerator and denominator pressure:
**SUPPLY**
- recover K from desalination concentrate;
- recover P as struvite / other qualified products;
- recover sulfate / gypsum;
- recycle wastewater and digestate nutrients;
- recycle crop-residue K and S;
- retain Mg for struvite, K-Mg-S products and soil needs.
**DEMAND REDUCTION**
- soil-test / formula fertilization;
- AI nutrient dosing;
- diversified grain rotations;
- legumes for biological N;
- residue return;
- gypsum where saline-sodic soil response supports it;
- organic matter / BIO01 products after contaminant and registration gates;
- nutrient-loss prevention.
Every tonne can receive at most one primary strategic credit.
## 7. Conservative national target
For v0.1, define a target that does not need speculative P, S or N credit:
> **120-node conservative floor: demonstrate ≥6% displacement of China’s current KCl imports from a 25%-of-inventory recovery scenario, while holding sulfur, phosphorus and nitrogen strategic offsets at zero until their local mass balances close.**
Then promote additional offset only as receipts land.
The next meaningful milestones are:
1. **K pilot:** actual Dongjiakou K assay + ≥25% measured recovery with product-grade K salt.
2. **P pilot:** measure BIO01 soluble P/N mass and run seawater/brine-Mg struvite pilot.
3. **S pilot:** identify which sulfate/gypsum tonnes can directly displace agricultural sulfur inputs versus which require conversion.
4. **Demand pilot:** conventional grain baseline versus diversified/AI-managed nutrient programme, measuring fertilizer input per tonne crop and total yield.
> **Keeper:** self-sufficiency comes from recovering strategic atoms *and* needing fewer imported atoms per tonne of food. Do both, and the denominator shrinks while domestic supply rises.
