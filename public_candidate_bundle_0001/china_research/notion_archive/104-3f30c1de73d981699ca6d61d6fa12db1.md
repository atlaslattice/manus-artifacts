Therefore the calcium basis is:
**MODELED · GENERIC_SEAWATER_COMPOSITION · NOT SITE-SPECIFIC**
A standard salinity-35 seawater composition reference carries about **0.413 g/kg Ca** and **2.712 g/kg sulfate**, consistent with that generic basis.[\[1\]](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/12066/noaa_12066_DS1.pdf)
The gypsum conversion is purely stoichiometric:
`CaSO4·2H2O / Ca = 172.169 / 40.078 = 4.29585`
- 14.6 kt Ca × 4.29585 = **62.72 kt gypsum-equivalent**
- 17.9 kt Ca × 4.29585 = **76.90 kt gypsum-equivalent**
The sulfate needed for that gypsum is only about **35.0–42.9 kt SO4/y**, below the generic modeled sulfate inventory. Within this sensitivity, **Ca is the limiting modeled reagent**.
> **Correct label: 62.7–76.9 kt/y = MODELED / GENERIC_SEAWATER_COMPOSITION / 100%-Ca-CAPTURE STOICHIOMETRIC CEILING / ZERO CREDIT.**

### 10.3 Permaculture-style grain — diversified cropping rather than monocropping
The user's intent is specifically **permaculture-style crop diversification at commercial grain scale**, not merely generic “regenerative agriculture” and not conversion to small garden plots.
The design objective is to replace large-area monocropping, where agronomically and operationally feasible, with a **mechanized diversified crop mosaic** that preserves industrial-scale harvesting and logistics.
Candidate system:
- wheat / maize remain major staple crops where locally appropriate, but **not as permanent single-crop monocultures**;
- soybean-maize strip intercropping and other mechanizable intercrops where machinery/market fit supports them;
- multi-year rotations across cereals, legumes, oilseeds and suitable cover crops;
- relay cropping / seasonal double-cropping where climate and water allow;
- nitrogen-fixing legumes integrated as productive crops or cover phases;
- deep-rooted / nutrient-scavenging species selected to recycle nutrients from different soil depths;
- flowering / habitat strips, windbreaks or perennial margins where they improve biodiversity, erosion control or beneficial-insect habitat without materially impairing machinery;
- crop-residue return with measured K/S/C balance rather than automatic residue removal;
- qualified BIO01 organic matter returned where contaminant and agronomic gates pass;
- gypsum or sulfate amendments only on soil-tested fields where salinity/sodicity/S need justifies them;
- C01 sensing and AI optimization to choose crop mix, rotation, planting geometry, nutrient rates, irrigation and harvest timing.
The useful permaculture principle is **functional diversity**: different crops and landscape elements perform different jobs — food production, biological N fixation, nutrient scavenging, residue generation, erosion control, soil carbon, habitat and risk diversification — while the system remains compatible with large-scale mechanization.
Therefore AGR01 should compare the diversified system against the incumbent monocrop/short-rotation baseline on total food/protein output, fertilizer use, soil chemistry, water demand, pest/disease pressure, resilience, labor/machinery burden and economics — not assume diversification wins by definition.
#### Evidence envelope — decades of diversification data
The user's intuition that diversified systems can produce **30–40%+ gains** is plausible in some configurations, but it should not be treated as a universal mean yield uplift.
Current synthesis literature supports a stronger, more nuanced claim:
- a 2025 global crop-rotation meta-analysis using **3,663 paired field observations from 1980–2024** found subsequent-crop yield increases averaging **+23% after legume pre-crops** and **+16% after non-legume pre-crops**; across the full crop sequence, rotations increased total yield, dietary energy, nutrients and revenue by roughly **14–27%** versus continuous monoculture.[\[1\]](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-64567-9)
- a global intercropping meta-analysis found mean **yield land-equivalent ratio (LER) = 1.49**, meaning diversified intercrops required about **49% less land to produce the same combined yield** as component monocultures. This is a land-productivity metric, not a claim that each individual crop yields 49% more.[\[2\]](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0167880922003887)
- a 2026 cover-crop meta-analysis across **1,788 observations / 191 studies** found average main-crop yield gains of about **+25% in rotations** and **+7% in intercrops**, with very large context-specific responses in low-yield environments.[\[3\]](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0378429025004666)
- a 2025 second-order meta-analysis spanning **184 meta-analyses, 6,741 effect sizes and up to 120 years of evidence** found diversification strongly increased long-term profitability, biodiversity, soil quality and carbon sequestration while producing **no significant overall yield penalty**; long-horizon gains in ecosystem/service metrics rose roughly **37–189%** over 20 years depending on the metric.[\[4\]](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-67757-7)
- permaculture-specific field evidence is smaller but directionally strong: a 2024 paired-farm study found **27% higher soil-carbon stocks**, **20% lower bulk density**, **201% greater earthworm abundance**, and sharply higher plant/earthworm/bird richness on permaculture sites than neighboring conventional controls.[\[5\]](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43247-024-01405-8)
- broader crop-diversification meta-analysis found **+7.5% soil aggregate mean-weight diameter**, **+3.3% bulk-soil carbon**, and **+12.5% macroaggregate-associated carbon** relative to monoculture.[\[6\]](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0301479723024490)
**AGR01 interpretation:** use **\~15–25% as a defensible literature-scale central yield/productivity improvement band for many rotation/diversification systems**, while allowing **30–40%+ as an evidence-supported upside range for favorable crop combinations, degraded/low-yield baselines, land-equivalent productivity, or combined interventions**. Keep Dongjiakou's own yield gain at **UNKNOWN** until a local randomized/paired pilot measures it.
