## 8. Verdict
### What is established
- The plant runs at **49.1%** of design capacity — the real process energy is **39.4 GWh/yr**, not the design-basis 80.3.
- The legacy model discharges **17.93-21.91 Mm³/yr** of reject containing **1.24-1.52 Mt/yr** of dissolved minerals, **cross-checked by two independent routes**, and recovers **none** of it.
- Rooftop solar over the measured 17,474 m² workshop area can deliver **2.7-2.9 GWh/yr** — **6.9-7.4%** of the process train.
### What is not established
- **Any** economic conclusion — the tariff and whole-site SEC are `UNKNOWN`.
- **Any** node-level energy conclusion — the land/sea classification gates ±261 GWh/yr.
- **Any** resource-recovery credit — all seven lanes are at zero for want of a receipt.
- Whether the cold cascade runs continuously — the regasification schedule is `UNKNOWN`.
### The verdict
> **The node model is not yet demonstrated to beat the legacy baseline. What is demonstrated is that the legacy baseline wastes a very large, arithmetically verified quantity — 1.24-1.52 Mt/yr of dissolved minerals and 100% of its available cold exergy — and that the node model's only verified gain is a 6.9% process-energy improvement from rooftop solar. The opportunity is large and the capture is unproven. The programme's own discipline is what keeps that distinction visible, and it should not be traded away for a favourable headline.**
**No net-positive claim is made. No credit is realised. Register v0.4.1. 6 of 6 vetoes UNRESOLVED.**
