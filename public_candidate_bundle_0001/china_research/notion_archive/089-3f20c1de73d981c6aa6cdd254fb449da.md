## (e) AI optical sorting: accuracy, purity, recovery and contamination are different measures
**\[REPORTED\]** The 2022 commercial-technology review found MRF-reported sorting efficiencies of **91–99%**, below manufacturer claims as high as 99.99%. It explicitly says input composition, sourcing and operating conditions affect achieved results; it also reports about **10 wt% contamination** in many outgoing film bales. These are not equivalent to a 95% polymer-recognition accuracy or a 90% contamination-detection rate. \[S9\]
**\[MEASURED\]** In the multi-plant EU study, annual-operation MRF target-output purity ranged from **65% to 98%** where reported. Examples were PP 92–98%, films 82–98%, and HDPE 98% in two plants. The same study warns that controlled sampling can exceed annual average performance because annual figures include failures and operation below capacity. \[S10\]
**\[REPORTED\]** A review describes a field MRF case in Spain where an AI robot achieved approximately **100% sorted-HDPE purity** and **60–80% recovery**. This supports the proposition that high purity is possible on a selected target stream; it simultaneously demonstrates that purity cannot be substituted for capture/recovery. \[S11\]
**\[NOT CONFIRMED\] \[UNKNOWN\]** “95%+ polymer and 90%+ contamination detection” is not a testable performance specification as stated. **\[UNKNOWN\]** The source documents do not specify the feed composition, belt loading, object size, wet/dirty/black-plastic share, target polymers, confusion matrix, threshold, definition of contamination, false positives, false negatives, sample size, uptime, downstream purity, or recovery. No independent test of the claimed system was found. \[S0\] \[S9\] \[S10\] \[S11\]
**\[PARTIALLY CONFIRMED\]** A 95% *output purity* target can be technically feasible for a defined, conditioned stream with quality control. **\[NOT CONFIRMED\]** It cannot be extrapolated to 95% recognition across mixed landfill waste, or to 90% contamination detection, from the evidence supplied. \[S9\] \[S10\] \[S11\]
