## 2. Scaling method
The cold comparator has a combined electrical-system effect of approximately **5.207 MW** (1.342 MW generation + 3.865 MW avoided electrical cooling). Dongjiakou's modeled **34 GWh/y** equals approximately **3.88 MW annual-average electrical-system impact**, or about **74.5%** of the comparator magnitude.
A simple first-pass scale therefore gives:
- central cold CAPEX: approximately **CNY 37.0M**;
- central annual cold net cash flow: approximately **CNY 15.24M/y**.
This is a **TRANSFERRED_REFERENCE_SCENARIO**, not an engineering estimate.
PV sizing from the existing Node-001 yield/output pair gives approximately **2.41–2.60 MWp**. At recent Chinese C&I rooftop EPC comparators, first-pass PV CAPEX is approximately **CNY 6.2–8.7M**.

### Interpretation
- **Year 1:** not cash-positive after build cost in any comparator case.
- **Year 3:** central and favorable cases cross cumulative break-even; conservative case does not.
- **Year 5:** all three first-pass comparator cases are cumulatively positive.
- **Simple central break-even:** approximately **2.6 years**.
This is encouraging, but it is **not a Dongjiakou investment case**. It is a ballpark result showing that externally observed economics do not immediately falsify the architecture.
## 4. What is intentionally worth zero in this v0.1 run
The following remain **CNY 0** in the base scenario despite possible upside:
- Mg / salt / bromine / lithium / strontium product revenue;
- recovered-water / freeze-product revenue;
- compute or HIL service revenue;
- HVAC condensate value;
- reclaimed-water / nutrient / organics revenue;
- waste-to-feedstock profit;
- tourism, hotels, resorts, stadium, events or real estate;
- carbon / biodiversity / ecological-service monetization;
- QOL/productivity value;
- regenerative-dividend value.
This prevents speculative upside from being required to make the core case work.
