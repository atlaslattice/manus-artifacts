## 3. The Fossilbranch Move

When the work gets fast, weird, or overloaded:

```text
1. Preserve the branch.
2. Name the uncertainty.
3. Separate wire from overlay.
4. Separate candidate from canon.
5. Separate review from ratification.
6. Separate storage from authority.
7. Create the smallest useful artifact.
8. Link it to the issue/PR trail.
9. Keep the failed path as evidence.
10. Move one clean step forward.
```

That is the Fossilbranch move.

No panic. No deletion. No role-squatting. No false finality.

## 5. Artifact Minimum Standard

Every important artifact should answer:

```yaml

  what_is_this: required
  status: required
  canon: required
  deployment: required
  authority_effect: required
  source_or_lineage: required
  strongest_safe_claim: required
  overclaims_to_avoid: required
  next_review_step: required
  deletion_policy: preserve
```

If the artifact cannot answer those fields, it is not ready to be called stable.

## 6. The Fun Rule

Fun is not a distraction.

Fun is the signal that the system is still alive enough to create.

But fun must not smuggle authority.

```text
Play can inspire.
Play can preserve morale.
Play can compress culture.
Play can reveal structure.
Play cannot ratify canon.
Play cannot deploy runtime.
Play cannot override receipts.
```

Madden booth, goblin verdicts, Krakoa welcomes, and mythic names are allowed in the archive when clearly labeled.

They are not allowed to pretend they are tests, approvals, or execution evidence.
