# Graph Is Not Canon FAQ

Status: candidate staging material  
Canon: no  
Deployment: no  
Authority: none  

## What is the graph?

The graph is a map of sources, claims, receipts, contradictions, review states, and next actions.

It helps humans and agents inspect lineage. It does not decide truth.

## Does a graph edge promote a claim?

No.

A graph edge can mean:

- derived_from
- cites
- contradicts
- requires_review
- missing_receipt
- blocked_by
- related_to
- may_supersede_candidate

None of these mean canon, deployment, ownership, endorsement, or authority.

## Does a central node become authoritative?

No.

Centrality means the node is connected. It may mean it needs more review. It does not mean it is true or ratified.

## Does public GitHub make something proven?

No.

Public GitHub makes a candidate artifact inspectable. It does not make it canon.

## What makes something canon?

Only an explicit human-root or defined governance decision within a stated scope.

## What is the safe public claim?

Atlas Lattice is being developed as a public, open-source, provenance-first knowledge graph and archive substrate that maps sources, claims, evidence, review states, contradictions, deltas, and next actions across a large body of original human-AI creative and technical work.
