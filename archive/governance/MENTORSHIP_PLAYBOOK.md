# Mentorship Playbook

> Defines the mentorship program structure for Atlas Lattice contributors.

status: candidate

---

## Purpose

Mentorship accelerates contributor development and builds a stronger community. This playbook defines how mentors and mentees are paired, what they work on together, and how success is measured.

---

## Mentor Eligibility

Mentors must:
- Have ≥ 3 merged PRs in the repository
- Have familiarity with at least one domain (governance, KG, CI, testing, etc.)
- Be willing to commit ~1 hour per week for 90 days

---

## Mentee Eligibility

Any contributor who:
- Has made their first contribution
- Wants to go deeper but isn't sure where to start

---

## Pairing Process

1. Mentee opens a GitHub Discussion in the `Mentorship` category expressing interest
2. An available mentor responds and proposes a pairing
3. The council confirms the pairing and logs it in this document (Mentorship Registry)
4. Pairing is active for 90 days

---

## What Happens During a Mentorship

| Week | Focus |
|------|-------|
| 1 | Orientation: review the mentee's goals; pick a first mentored task |
| 2–8 | Collaborative work: pair on PRs, answer questions, do code reviews |
| 9–12 | Transition: mentee leads; mentor advises |
| Post-pairing | Mentee is invited to mentor the next generation |

## Mentorship Feedback

At the end of each pairing:
1. Mentee completes a brief async feedback survey (GitHub Discussion)
2. Mentor reflects on what worked and what could improve
3. Playbook is updated if process improvements are identified
