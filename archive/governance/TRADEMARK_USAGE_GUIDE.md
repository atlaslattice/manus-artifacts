# Trademark Usage Guide

*Atlas Lattice Foundation · Aetherforge Mission #15 · 2026-05-28*

status: candidate

> Defines how the Atlas Lattice Foundation name, marks, and project names may be used by contributors, forks, and third parties.

---

## Protected Marks

The following names and identifiers are trademarks or service marks of the Atlas Lattice Foundation:

- **Atlas Lattice** (name)
- **Atlas Lattice Foundation** (organization name)
- **Aetherforge** (project/game name)
- **GPTDream++** (protocol name)
- **TIDELOCKBrain** (agent/artifact name)
- **ORCS** (framework abbreviation, in context of Atlas/ORCS)

---

## Permitted Uses (No Prior Approval Needed)

You may freely use these marks to:

1. **Refer accurately to this project** — e.g., "This tool integrates with Atlas Lattice."
2. **Fork and build upon the open-source code** under the MIT License, provided you make clear your fork is not the official project.
3. **Discuss, write about, or teach** about Atlas Lattice in educational or research contexts.
4. **Attribute work** — e.g., "Based on GPTDream++ by Atlas Lattice Foundation."

---

## Restricted Uses (Prior Approval Required)

The following require written approval from @atlaslattice before use:

1. Using any protected mark in the **name or logo of a derived product, service, or organization**.
2. Implying **official endorsement or affiliation** with Atlas Lattice Foundation.
3. Using the marks in **commercial advertising or marketing** materials.
4. Creating **merchandise** featuring the marks.

---

## Fork Naming Convention

If you fork this repository, you **must not** name your fork "Atlas Lattice" or "Aetherforge" without written permission. Acceptable patterns:

- `MyProject (based on Atlas Lattice)`
- `MyForge (forked from Aetherforge)`

---

## Logo and Visual Identity

No official logo has been released at this time. When visual identity assets are published, they will be licensed under CC BY 4.0 for permitted uses listed above.

---

## Reporting Misuse

If you encounter unauthorized use of these marks, report it via GitHub Discussions or email `security@atlaslattice.org`.
