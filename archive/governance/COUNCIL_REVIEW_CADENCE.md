# Council Review Cadence

*Atlas Lattice Foundation · Aetherforge Mission #7 · 2026-05-28*

status: candidate

> Defines the rhythm and format of Atlas Lattice Foundation council review sessions, ensuring the governance engine remains alive, consistent, and publicly transparent.

---

## Review Cadence Overview

| Cadence | Name | Scope |
|---------|------|-------|
| Weekly | **Triage Sync** | Open PRs, new issues, urgent escalations |
| Monthly | **Council Wave Review** | Wave progress, KPI pulse, artifact quality |
| Quarterly | **Full Council Session** | Ratification batch, policy reviews, risk register |
| Annual | **Summit** | Strategy, roadmap v-next, retrospective |

---

## Weekly — Triage Sync

**Format:** Asynchronous (GitHub Discussions or comment threads)  
**Cadence:** Every Monday (UTC)  
**Artifacts:**
- Review open PRs flagged for council attention
- Acknowledge new RFC proposals
- Triage security/urgent escalations (Tier 1 SLAs)

**Output:** Updated labels/assignments on open items; no formal minutes required.

---

## Monthly — Council Wave Review

**Format:** Written report posted to `archive/governance/` or GitHub Discussions  
**Cadence:** Last Friday of each month  
**Artifacts:**
- Wave progress checkpoint (% complete on current 12-task face)
- KPI pulse: test pass rate, CI health, orphan link count
- Artifact quality spot-check (3 randomly selected docs)
- Outstanding SLA breaches review

**Output:** Monthly Wave Review document filed as `WAVE_REVIEW_YYYY-MM.md`.

---

## Quarterly — Full Council Session

**Format:** Written ratification batch document  
**Cadence:** End of Q1/Q2/Q3/Q4 (last working day of March, June, September, December)  
**Artifacts:**
- Ratification queue: all candidate artifacts eligible for canon review
- Policy document updates
- Legal/trust audit (Mission #24)
- Risk register update (Mission #23)
- Next-quarter objectives

**Output:** `COUNCIL_SESSION_YYYY-Qn.md` filed in `archive/governance/`.

---

## Annual — Summit

**Format:** Extended written retrospective + strategy session  
**Cadence:** First two weeks of January  
**Artifacts:**
- Full 144-category scorecard review
- Roadmap v-next definition
- Community health assessment
- Benchmark report vs top OSS repositories (Mission #138)
- Launch retrospective (Mission #144 delivery)

**Output:** `ANNUAL_SUMMIT_YYYY.md` filed in `archive/governance/` and linked from README.

---

## Participation

- All council sessions are **open record** — artifacts published to this repository.
- Any contributor may submit agenda items via GitHub Discussions.
- Final ratification authority always rests with **@atlaslattice**.
