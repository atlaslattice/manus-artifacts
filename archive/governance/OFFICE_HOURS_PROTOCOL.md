# Office Hours Protocol

> Defines the format and schedule for Atlas Lattice office hours.

status: candidate

---

## What Are Office Hours?

Office hours are scheduled async or live sessions where contributors can get help, ask questions, propose ideas, or discuss ongoing work with council members and mentors.

---

## Format Options

### Option A: Async Office Hours (Default)

A designated GitHub Discussions thread is opened weekly:

```markdown
# Office Hours — Week of YYYY-MM-DD

Drop your questions, blockers, or topics below.
A council member or mentor will respond within 48 hours.

@atlaslattice and mentors are available to respond.
```

**Advantages:** Timezone-friendly; asynchronous; permanent record.

---

### Option B: Live Office Hours (Optional, Milestone Weeks)

When significant milestones are reached (e.g., Wave 12 launch), a live session is scheduled:
- Platform: GitHub Live / Discord / TBD
- Duration: 60 minutes
- Always scheduled in UTC; provide timezone converter link
- Recorded and transcribed if possible for async access

---

## Schedule

| Format | Frequency | Time |
|--------|-----------|------|
| Async thread | Weekly | Monday 09:00 UTC |
| Live session | At major milestones | Announced ≥ 1 week in advance |

---

## Office Hours Scope

Topics welcome in office hours:
- Contribution questions ("how do I do X?")
- Architecture discussions
- Feedback on draft PRs
- Aetherforge game design
- Knowledge graph expansion ideas

Topics not appropriate for office hours:
- Bug reports (open an issue)
- Security vulnerabilities (follow vulnerability disclosure process)
- Canon ratification decisions (those go through the formal RFC process)

---

## Response Commitment

Council members commit to responding to async office hours questions within **48 hours** during active development periods. During off-periods, response time may extend to 7 days.
