# Quarterly Objective Review

> Defines the quarterly review loop for objectives, score movement, and launch readiness.

status: candidate

---

## Purpose

Quarterly objective reviews convert roadmap promises into an operating rhythm. Each review checks whether Atlas Lattice is moving toward the v1.0 milestones, whether world-class scores are improving, and which gaps need focused action in the next quarter.

---

## Review Inputs

Every quarterly review uses:

- `WORLDCLASS_SCORECARD_BASELINE.md`
- `TOP_OSS_BENCHMARK_REPORT.md`
- `KPI_DASHBOARD_DESIGN.md`
- `V1_MILESTONE_PLAN.md`
- `CHANGELOG.md`
- Latest governance, quality, and community reports

---

## Review Questions

1. Which milestone criteria moved materially this quarter?
2. Which Faces improved, stalled, or regressed?
3. What evidence now exists that did not exist last quarter?
4. Which gaps threaten the v1.0 timeline?
5. What are the next quarter's top three objective commitments?

---

## Standard Agenda

| Step | Timebox | Outcome |
|------|---------|---------|
| Scorecard review | 20 min | Updated view of 12 Faces |
| Milestone review | 20 min | Status for M1–M7 |
| KPI review | 15 min | Metrics trend summary |
| Benchmark review | 15 min | Relative position vs comparators |
| Priority reset | 20 min | Top objectives for next quarter |
| Decision log | 10 min | Recorded owners and deadlines |

---

## Required Outputs

Each quarterly review must publish:

1. An updated objective review note at `archive/governance/OBJECTIVE_REVIEW_YYYY_QN.md`
2. Any score updates needed in the scorecard baseline
3. A short public summary for the changelog digest
4. Any milestone date adjustments approved by council

---

## Success Criteria

A quarterly objective review is complete only when:

- Owners are assigned for every new priority
- At least one metric trend is compared to the previous quarter
- Changes to v1.0 risk are made explicit
- Follow-up artifacts are linked from the review note

---

## Quarterly Cadence

| Quarter | Review window | Primary focus |
|---------|---------------|---------------|
| Q1 | Last week of March | Foundation and execution health |
| Q2 | Last week of June | Mid-year benchmark and release readiness |
| Q3 | Last week of September | v1 milestone progression |
| Q4 | Last week of December | Annual retrospective and next-year reset |
