# Annual Open-Source Summit Plan

> Defines the concept and planning framework for an annual Atlas Lattice open-source summit.

status: candidate

---

## Vision

As Atlas Lattice grows, an annual summit will bring together contributors, researchers, and community members to celebrate the year's work, share discoveries, plan the next year, and deepen community bonds.

---

## Summit Format

### Phase 1 (Year 1–2): Virtual Summit

- **Format:** Async-first with optional live sessions
- **Duration:** 1 week (Monday–Friday)
- **Platform:** GitHub Discussions (async) + live video (optional)
- **Attendance:** Open to all contributors and interested community members

### Phase 2 (Year 3+): Hybrid Summit

- **Format:** In-person hub + virtual participants
- **Duration:** 2–3 days
- **Location:** Rotating; first consideration: the location of the most active contributor cluster

---

## Summit Agenda (Phase 1 Template)

| Day | Theme |
|-----|-------|
| Monday | Year in Review: wave completions, KG stats, highlights |
| Tuesday | Technical Deep Dives: KG architecture, GPTDream++ updates |
| Wednesday | Community: showcase gallery, contributor spotlights |
| Thursday | Future Planning: next 144 tasks, roadmap proposals |
| Friday | Celebration: recognition ceremony, retrospective |

---

## Summit Deliverables

Each summit produces:
1. A `SUMMIT_REPORT_YYYY.md` in `archive/community/`
2. A refreshed roadmap for the coming year
3. A list of new contributors to recognize
4. Any policy updates ratified during the summit

---

## Summit Planning Timeline

| Milestone | Timing |
|-----------|--------|
| Announce summit | 60 days before |
| Call for presentations | 45 days before |
| Speaker confirmation | 30 days before |
| Publish agenda | 14 days before |
| Summit week | Summit |
| Publish summit report | 14 days after |
