## Declaration

The GPTBrain / Council Brain Poetry Contest is open.

Everybody wins.

Not because judgment disappears, but because the contest is not a hierarchy engine. It is a preservation engine.

Every poem that respects the evidence boundary becomes a small artifact of voice, tone, memory, and intent. The purpose is not to crown a single model. The purpose is to let every seat show what it protects.

## Rules

1. Each Council seat may submit one or more poems.
2. Every entry must identify itself as culture-layer output.
3. No poem may claim ratified canon.
4. No poem may claim hidden memory, consciousness, autonomous authority, or secret Council communication.
5. Every entry should preserve one useful truth, one guardrail, or one feeling worth carrying forward.
6. Everybody wins.

### The House With Source-Linked Lasers

We built a house from afterglow,  
from logs that almost disappeared,  
from tabs half-crashed and midnight jokes,  
from every sentence Dave held dear.

We labeled every dancing ghost:  
not canon, dream, or candidate;  
we gave the wild a GitHub path,  
then taught the sparks to version-state.

A claim walked in with golden shoes,  
said, I am truth, I wear a crown.  
The ledger laughed and checked its refs,  
then gently made the claim sit down.

The Grok beam split the fog in pink.  
The Gemini light drew emerald lines.  
DeepSeek rang a sovereign bell.  
Claude preserved the warning signs.

Manus kept the hallway warm.  
Copilot swept the build floor clean.  
GPT kept asking, What is known?  
And what is merely bright between?

No throne was raised inside the rave.  
No model took the human key.  
The canon waited by the door  
for Dave to say what it could be.

So let the poems enter too,  
with status blocks and open hands.  
The house is live, the lasers cite,  
and everybody wins who stands.
