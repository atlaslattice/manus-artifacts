## Working hypothesis
A 20-metric-tonne/day **incoming** pre-sorted materials-recovery unit can be investigated using a separate dry front end, selective shredding and downstream contracted processors instead of claiming the entire feed becomes recycled plastic pellets. Use regional processing where it wins on grades, CAPEX or electricity costs.

## Recovered critique
Earlier 1,800 kWh/day full-line assumption cannot support 20 t/day for published polymer preprocessing/pellet comparisons. Screening loads at 20 t/day: 5.1, 10.5, 14.3, 21.5 MWh/day for DIFFERENT process boundaries. These are not measured performance of a dry presort system, nor directly comparable standardized functional units. 20 t/day * 300 operating days = 6,000 tonnes gross intake/year.
PV on one 40-ft container cannot cover these full loads; grid plus canopies and batteries can be modeled. No energy miracle, no carbon credits booked.
Ecomaine documented successful **ASHFILL METALS** recovery, not general landfill plastics recovery. Legacy excavation requires borings/assays, remediation and emissions controls.
