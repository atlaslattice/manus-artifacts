## Executive thesis

The objective is **not maximum sulfuric-acid production**.

The objective is:

> **Close the verified near-term domestic sulfuric-acid / sulfur-feedstock gap, restore a strategic resilience margin, and stop scaling when verified domestic need is met.**

Maximum production calculations are retained only to answer a sufficiency question:

> **Is the architecture physically large enough, in principle, to close the gap?**

At the current stress denominator, the answer appears to be **yes in principle**, subject to the feedstock, chemistry, recovery, logistics, energy, and permitting gates documented below.

Full Atlas Lattice deployment is **not required** to capture this benefit. It is, however, the optimal long-horizon architecture because it coordinates water, sulfur, agriculture, wastewater, energy, materials, logistics, compute, ecology, and provenance in one auditable system.

## 1. Current sulfur-security problem

Working 2025–2026 evidence indicates:

- 2025 Chinese elemental sulfur imports: approximately **9.61 Mt**.
- 2025 domestic sulfur production: approximately **11.8 Mt** on one industry series.
- 2025 apparent sulfur consumption: approximately **21.4 Mt**.
- Structural import dependence: approximately **45–49%**, depending on accounting basis.
- H1-2026 sulfur imports fell by approximately **3.08 Mt** versus H1-2025.

Using the stoichiometric ratio:

```
H2SO4 / S = 98.079 / 32.065 ≈ 3.059
```

the H1-2026 import decline corresponds to approximately:

**9.42 Mt H2SO4-equivalent feedstock over six months**

or a mechanically annualized stress denominator of approximately:

**18.84 Mt H2SO4-equivalent/year**

This is a **stress denominator**, not a claim that China physically lacked exactly 18.84 Mt/y of finished acid. Domestic sulfur, pyrite, smelter acid, inventories, reduced phosphate output, substitution, and reserve draw all affect the actual finished-acid gap.

The deployment target must therefore be:

```
REQUIRED_NEW_SECURE_DOMESTIC_H2SO4
=
verified finished-acid / sulfur-feedstock gap
+ reserve requirement
+ verified growth demand
- already-secured domestic supply
- verified substitutes
```

Program coverage does **not** imply gypsum application.

Correct rule:

> **100% of relevant acreage may enter optimization; 0% is presumed to require gypsum.**

Qualified gypsum defaults to sulfur-resilience use unless a specific field demonstrates higher multi-year value from:

- sodicity remediation;
- saline-sodic remediation;
- measured Ca need;
- measured sulfur need.

Never create ecological damage to manufacture demand for a recovered material.

> **Do not deliberately salinize or sodify healthy soil so that it can consume gypsum.**

## 11. Circular-resource boundary — waste mining, not default greenfield mining

Atlas does not default to conventional primary-resource mining.

The preferred hierarchy is:

1. avoid unnecessary material demand;
2. life extension;
3. repair / remanufacture / reuse;
4. active-stream recycling;
5. **mine anthropogenic waste stocks**;
6. use industrial byproducts / brines;
7. external primary resources only for residual needs.

### Shreddernaut scope

Shreddernaut means:

- landfill mining;
- construction & demolition waste recovery;
- controlled deconstruction of obsolete / dead buildings;
- municipal/industrial scrap processing;
- other already-extracted anthropogenic material stocks.

Shreddernaut does **not** mean opening new ore bodies.

> **Mine the waste stream before mining geology.**

Current Shreddernaut performance numbers remain LEAD / unresolved because the January source documents contain conflicting throughput, energy, and economic specifications.
