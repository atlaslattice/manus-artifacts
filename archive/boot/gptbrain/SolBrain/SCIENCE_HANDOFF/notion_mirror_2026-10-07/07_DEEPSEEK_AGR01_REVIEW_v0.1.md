## Accepted findings
1. **2025 KCl exposure:** DeepSeek reports China remains materially import-dependent in KCl/potash. Preserve as strategic-input exposure; update denominators when commodity-consistent customs data are available.
2. **Crop-residue K is baseline, not merely future opportunity.** The 2026 *Field Crops Research* paper reports **9.25 Mt K returned in 2020**, 2.1× national cereal K-fertilizer consumption, shifting major cereal systems from deficit to a modest surplus. Independently verified against the published article.[\[1\]](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0378429026001462)
3. **Energy-autonomous KCl separation is a real technology lead.** A 2025 *Nature Communications* paper demonstrates a lab-scale COF/redox system using salinity-gradient energy to drive KCl extraction without external pressure/voltage, with reported net-energy-output potential. Independently verified. **TRL remains laboratory/research; no Dongjiakou industrial credit.**[\[2\]](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-66345-z)
4. **Struvite should be evaluated as compliance + nutrient recovery**, not assumed to be a stand-alone profit center.
5. **SWRO gypsum → sulfuric acid remains UNKNOWN-BUT-LEAD.** Chinese gypsum-to-acid infrastructure is real; transferability of SWRO-derived gypsum remains unproven.
6. **Node self-sufficiency ratio is a useful primary AGR metric**, with inter-node exchange reported separately. National offset remains a secondary macro-resilience metric.

DeepSeek correctly identified that the white paper did not publish a reproducible denominator clearly enough and mixed two internal K bases.
The white-paper floor is now explicitly tied to:
- 17.93 Mm³/y product;
- 45–50% modeled recovery sensitivity → 35.86–39.84 Mm³/y feed;
- 380 mg/L external-reference K concentration;
- 13.63–15.14 kt/y elemental-K feed inventory;
- KCl/K molecular conversion 1.9068;
- **25.98–28.87 kt/y KCl-equivalent inventory ceiling**.
A separate legacy 14.2–17.3 kt/y K composition sensitivity remains a different MODELED basis and must not be mixed into the floor.
## Human-root correction to DeepSeek's siting proposal
DeepSeek proposed making AGR01 an agricultural-hinterland siting constraint.
**Rejected as a hard constraint; accepted as an optimization preference.**
Atlas nodes are **locality/population-first**, not farm-first. Agricultural hinterland improves closure and reduces logistics, but dense urban/industrial nodes remain valid and can use:
- peri-urban agriculture;
- controlled-environment agriculture / greenhouses;
- rooftop/built-surface production;
- wastewater nutrient recovery;
- neighboring-node exchange.
Long horizon: nodes follow populations/localities and ultimately extend wherever material metabolism can be instrumented and governed.
