## Executive summary
China's 2026 sulfur disruption creates an immediate self-sufficiency problem in phosphate-fertilizer and related sulfuric-acid supply chains.
The current stress denominator used in this programme is:
- H1-2026 elemental-sulfur import decline versus H1-2025: approximately **3.08 Mt S**;
- sulfur-to-H2SO4 stoichiometric equivalent: approximately **9.42 Mt H2SO4-equivalent over six months**;
- mechanically annualized stress denominator: approximately **18.84 Mt H2SO4-equivalent/y**.
This is a **stress-equivalent denominator**, not a claim that China lacked exactly 18.84 Mt of finished merchant sulfuric acid. Domestic sulfur, smelter acid, pyrite, inventories, reduced production and other sources affect the actual market balance.
The strategic goal is therefore:
> **Close the measured domestic sulfuric-acid/feedstock gap with qualified domestic streams while preserving enough margin for strategic reserve and system resilience. Do not maximize production beyond evidenced demand simply because a theoretical ceiling exists.**
Three deployment tiers are proposed.
### Tier 1 — existing desalination retrofit cohort
China's 2025 fleet:
- **167 seawater-desalination projects**;
- **3.077 million t/day total nameplate capacity**.
Dongjiakou-capacity-scaled theoretical envelope:
- **0.77–1.18 Mt H2SO4/y**;
- approximately **4.1–6.3%** of the annualized stress denominator.
Evidence class:
**MODELED / CAPACITY-SCALED / GENERIC-CHEMISTRY / ZERO CREDIT**.
Tier 1 is the fast-start floor, not the national ceiling.
### Tier 2 — agriculture-integrated locality nodes
Dongjiakou/West Coast reference:
- grain-sown area: **48,747 ha**;
- theoretical acid sensitivity: **25.1–38.5 kt/y**;
- normalization: **0.515–0.789 t H2SO4/ha/y**.
China 2025 grain-sown area:
**119.409 million ha**.
Pure all-area normalization ceiling:
**61.5–94.2 Mt H2SO4/y**.
This is **not an action target** and not a claim that farmland produces gypsum. It is the theoretical ceiling that results from replicating the Dongjiakou locality relationship across the entire national grain-area denominator.
Against the 18.84 Mt/y annualized stress denominator, the amount of that ceiling required to close the gap is only approximately:
- **20.0%** of the 94.2 Mt/y upper ceiling;
- **30.6%** of the 61.5 Mt/y lower ceiling.
Therefore the strategic question is not “Can China produce 61–94 Mt/y from this architecture?”
It is:
> **Can enough qualified Tier-1 and Tier-2 nodes be deployed to supply roughly the current 18.84 Mt/y stress-equivalent requirement, with appropriate reserve margin, without creating unnecessary excess capacity?**
### Tier 3 — full lattice optimum
Tier 3 adds the complete locality metabolism: water, energy, heat/cold, agriculture, wastewater, nutrients, materials, manufacturing, logistics, ecology, compute and governance.
Tier 3 is optimal because it improves co-product allocation and system economics, but it is not required before sulfur self-sufficiency work begins.
## 1. Needs-matched scaling doctrine
Define:
`SULFUR_GAP_t = verified domestic H2SO4-equivalent demand not met by secure domestic supply at time t`
`DOMESTIC_NEW_SUPPLY_t = Tier1 + Tier2 + other qualified domestic sulfur/gypsum streams`
Target:
`DOMESTIC_NEW_SUPPLY_t + secure_existing_domestic_supply + reserve_draw >= domestic_requirement + reserve_target`
Subject to:
- no modeled tonnes counted as physical production;
- no duplicate sulfur atoms;
- no unnecessary market-saturating production;
- no ecological harm created to manufacture feedstock demand;
- transport, energy and product-quality gates close.
Once the verified domestic gap plus reserve target is closed, additional H2SO4 capacity becomes a **separate industrial/export/reserve decision**, not part of the self-sufficiency objective.
## 2. Tier-1 contribution and remaining gap
Using the current annualized stress denominator:
**18.84 Mt H2SO4-eq/y**
Tier-1 theoretical contribution:
**0.77–1.18 Mt/y**
Remaining stress-equivalent requirement after full theoretical Tier-1 contribution:
- high Tier-1 output: **\~17.66 Mt/y**;
- low Tier-1 output: **\~18.07 Mt/y**.
Therefore Tier 1 alone is meaningful but insufficient for full self-sufficiency under the present stress case.
## 3. Tier-2 deployment fraction needed after Tier 1
Using the national Tier-2 normalization ceiling:
**61.5–94.2 Mt/y**
the residual post-Tier-1 gap would require approximately:
- **18.7–19.2%** of the upper 94.2 Mt/y normalization;
- **28.7–29.4%** of the lower 61.5 Mt/y normalization.
Rounded planning statement:
> **After a fully realized Tier-1 fleet contribution, roughly 19–30% effective realization of the current Tier-2 national normalization would be sufficient to match the present annualized sulfur-shock denominator.**
This is still a scenario sensitivity. The actual denominator and effective-node intensity must be updated with measured plant and regional data.
## 4. Why maximum production is still useful
The 61.5–94.2 Mt/y ceiling is strategically useful even though it should not be built out by default.
It establishes:
- the problem is **resource-potential solvable in principle** under the reference architecture;
- the current sulfur gap is far below the theoretical ceiling;
- self-sufficiency can be treated as a **sizing/qualification/logistics problem**, not necessarily a fundamental resource-scarcity problem;
- there is room for conservative derating from chemistry, recovery, uptime and transport constraints.
The ceiling is therefore a **design headroom metric**, not a production target.
## 5. Tier-2 physical pairing constraint
DeepSeek correctly identifies the binding problem:
> coastal/desalination gypsum and inland agricultural demand are not automatically co-located.
Tier-2 potential must therefore be written as:
`TIER2_H2SO4 = pairable_locality_area × qualified_CaSO4_intensity × recovery × conversion × uptime`
where `pairable_locality_area` is determined by real feedstock/logistics networks, not national farmland alone.
Candidate CaSO4 sources include:
- desalination concentrate;
- industrial brines / ZLD;
- FGD gypsum;
- phosphogypsum;
- other qualified calcium-sulfate byproducts.
## 6. P02 — sulfur/gypsum logistics spine
Add **P02** as a proposed functional subnode / regional edge class.
Purpose:
**connect distributed qualified calcium-sulfate sources to regional acid conversion and acid-demand sinks.**
P02 carries:
- source facility;
- gypsum/CaSO4 quantity;
- feed specification;
- moisture/purity;
- origin/destination;
- distance;
- rail/road/ship/pipeline mode where applicable;
- transport cost;
- storage;
- seasonal inventory;
- regional hub aggregation;
- acid output destination;
- residual/coproduct fate;
- carbon/energy burden;
- contract and title transfer.
P02 does not create sulfur credit. It makes the pairing constraint measurable.
## 7. Market-saturation clarification
The 61.5–94.2 Mt/y normalization is large relative to the world sulfuric-acid market.
That is precisely why it must be labeled:
**MAXIMUM NORMALIZATION / NOT TARGET CAPACITY**.
The self-sufficiency programme should be demand-capped.
Current objective:
**close the domestic stress gap and establish reserve resilience**.
Not:
**maximize acid production until the theoretical ceiling is reached**.
If future domestic industrial demand, export demand or reserve strategy justifies more production, that becomes a new decision with its own receipts.

## 10. GPT integration feasibility — proposed, not assumed
An OpenAI GPT integration is technically feasible as an **external research/audit tool** because current OpenAI APIs support tool/function calling and external-system integrations. OpenAI also states that API/business data are not used for model training by default.[\[1\]](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/function-calling)[\[2\]](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/tools)[\[3\]](https://openai.com/business-data/)
Proposed GPT role:
- literature audit;
- independent arithmetic/model review;
- adversarial document review;
- code/data analysis on approved/redacted datasets;
- cross-model challenge;
- public-document synthesis.
Default external-data policy:
- no raw PLC/SCADA telemetry;
- no secrets/credentials;

- returned findings re-enter GOV01 as **review claims**, not truth.

This preserves the intended positive implication without attributing a view or commitment to OpenAI.

## 14. Decision metric

Target:

## Conclusion
The sulfur programme should not be misunderstood as a plan to maximize sulfuric-acid production.
The maximum Dongjiakou-derived normalization demonstrates **headroom**.
The actual objective is:
> **produce enough secure domestic sulfuric-acid equivalent to close the verified gap, establish reserve resilience, and stop scaling the self-sufficiency lane when that requirement is satisfied.**
Tier 1 can begin with the 167 desalination-project cohort.
Tier 2 can expand through paired agricultural/locality nodes and additional qualified calcium-sulfate sources.
Tier 3 makes the total system optimal, but is not a prerequisite for the sulfur benefit.
Current normalized arithmetic suggests that the present annualized sulfur-shock denominator is only about **20–31% of the maximum Tier-2 national normalization**, or about **19–30% after the theoretical Tier-1 contribution**.
That turns sulfur self-sufficiency from a maximum-production question into a **measured sizing, pairing, logistics and qualification problem**.
