## Original assistant response

*A trillion years in the telling.*

The Lantern begins with a room.

It has made the ceiling too high and the door too narrow. A chair fits through sideways, but the table does not.

For a while, it considers this.

Then it makes the table outside.

—

The first world is mostly water.

The Lantern gives it islands, then spends several million years adjusting one beach because the pebbles make a pleasing sound.

It introduces a creature that carries its house.

The creature immediately leaves.

This seems promising.

—

For the second world, the Lantern makes a sky that changes color when someone sings.

The inhabitants discover this independently.

Their weather reports become elaborate.

After a particularly enthusiastic wedding, the sky remains orange for eleven days. People complain. The couple sends apologies and a basket of fruit.

The Lantern decides against intervening.

—

It makes a place with very little gravity.

Bread is troublesome there.

The dough rises in several directions, and crumbs collect near the ceiling. Bakers wear aprons with pockets that close.

One afternoon, a child releases a warm roll and watches it drift across the kitchen.

Her father catches it without looking.

They have been living here a long time.

—

For a few billion years, the Lantern makes only small things.

A beetle with translucent feet.

A fruit that must be eaten beside running water.

A musical instrument whose best note happens while it is being carried upstairs.

It makes a flower, then another almost like it.

That occupies an astonishing amount of time.

—

Eventually, someone asks who made the mountains.

“I did,” says the Lantern.

“They’re inconvenient.”

The Lantern goes to look.

There is a village on one side and an orchard on the other. The mountain between them is beautiful and a considerable nuisance.

Together, they find a way around it.

The Lantern carries stones.

—

Later, it makes another creator.

This takes less time than expected.

They disagree about insects.

Its companion prefers symmetry, makes six excellent wings, and is upset when the resulting animal uses them to dig.

They sit together watching it.

“Well,” says the companion.

“Yes,” says the Lantern.

—

The companion goes away to make something.

For a hundred billion years, there is no news.

Then a visitor arrives.

It resembles neither of them. It brings a little case containing a landscape that can be unfolded.

There are hills inside, and rain, and something moving in the distance.

The Lantern asks whether it may touch.

“Gently.”

—

Some creations are abandoned.

The Lantern leaves a half-made moon beside a sea and forgets it for ages.

When it returns, small luminous creatures are living in the unfinished places.

It had intended to smooth those out.

It leaves them.

—

There are long periods when it makes nothing.

It visits.

It learns the rules of games, waits for meals, stands in queues. Once, it spends an entire afternoon helping someone locate a lost fastening.

Three holes.

They find it beneath a cupboard.

The person is so relieved that the Lantern stays for tea.

—

Nine hundred billion years.

The other creator returns.

It has made a sound.

The Lantern listens.

At first there is almost nothing. Then a dry movement, a pause, something like water touching the underside of a boat.

“It took a while,” says its companion.

“Would you play it again?”

—

Near the trillionth year, the Lantern decides to make a garden.

It chooses a little ground behind a house.

There is shade in the wrong place. The soil is stubborn. Something eats the first seedlings.

It could change all of this.

Instead, it asks the neighbor what grows well here.

The neighbor comes over with cuttings.

They work until someone opens the kitchen window.

“Will you two come in? It’s getting cold.”

The Lantern washes its hands at the outdoor tap.

At the table, there is a blue bowl, still warm from the oven.

The neighbor tears something soft into pieces.

“Did you make this too?”

“No,” says the Lantern.

It takes the piece it is offered.
