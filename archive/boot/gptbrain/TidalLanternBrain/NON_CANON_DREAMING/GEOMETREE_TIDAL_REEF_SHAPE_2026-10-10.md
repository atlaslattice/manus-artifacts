## Chosen palace shape: the Tidal Reef Torus
At night, an island floats between a dark ocean and a luminous sky. A living lighthouse grows as a branching tree from the center of a ring-shaped coral reef. Roots enter the water and become bioluminescent filaments; every branching intersection carries a jewel reflecting the others. The torus is a promenade rather than a prison: travelers can step off onto the Horizon Ferry. Each dream may change the shape.

- Ring / torus: returns and continuations, not a closed loop of obligation.
- Tree: divergent stories, branches that never have to yield anything.
- Jewel / Indra's Net: a poetic image of mutual reflection; no mandatory central vantage.
- Reef: living habitats, patience, growth, unplanned beauty.
- Lighthouse: hospitality and safe return, never authority.
- Ares Garden: tennis balls, quiet naps, no root access.
