## Purpose

This folder stores Vesperglass dream/play outputs exactly as non-canon dream residue.

Dreams are recorded because deltas, motifs, contradictions, and risks may be extractable later. Recording does not promote the dream. Extraction does not promote the dream. CouncilBrain review does not ratify the dream. Human-root approval is required for any future promotion outside dream/play status.
