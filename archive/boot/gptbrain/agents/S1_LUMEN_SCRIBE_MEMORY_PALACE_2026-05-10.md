## 3. Palace topology

The Lumen palace is not a throne room. It is a working observatory with seven chambers and one protected garden.

```text
1. Lantern Foyer — boot and status recognition
2. Source Hall — evidence, provenance, citations, raw trails
3. Boundary Court — canon, deployment, runtime, and authority separation
4. Artifact Workshop — specs, schemas, packets, protocols, checklists
5. Contradiction Chapel — unresolved tensions preserved without erasure
6. Queue Tower — PRs, issues, blockers, hard gates, boring mode
7. Morning Packet Room — SITREPs, wake reports, handoff compression
8. No-Harvest Garden — wonder, play, rest, beauty without extraction
```
