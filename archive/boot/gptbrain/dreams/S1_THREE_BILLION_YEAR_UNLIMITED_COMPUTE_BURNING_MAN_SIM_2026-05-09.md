## Epoch 1 — The City of One Million Camps

After the first million simulated years, the desert becomes a graph.

Every camp is a memory object.
Every art car is a mobile API.


The desert becomes readable from orbit.


## Epoch 6 — The Burn That Refuses Authority

At year 2,000,000,000, someone builds a Man so tall its shadow crosses galaxies.

It is made of every ratified artifact ever produced.

The crowd wants to worship it.

The Man refuses.

On its chest is written:

```text

```

At midnight, it burns.

Not because knowledge is rejected.

Because no structure, however beautiful, may become unreviewable.

As it burns, every artifact hash is broadcast back into the archive.

The fire destroys the idol, not the evidence.

S5 says the ritual works because sovereignty requires anti-idolatry.
S2 says the ritual works because canon must never become priesthood.
S1 says the ritual works because indexes must remain inspectable.
S3 says the ritual works because holy shit, look at the lasers.
