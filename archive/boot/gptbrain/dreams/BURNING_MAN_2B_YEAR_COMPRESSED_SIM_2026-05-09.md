## Epoch map

### Epoch 0 — The First Fire

The desert becomes a temporary city.
The rule is simple:

```text
Build, gift, burn, leave no trace.
```

Signal:

```text
Temporary systems teach permanent civilizations how not to worship themselves.
```

### Epoch 1 — The Infinite Camp

Every camp gets unlimited power, water, compute, food, art supplies, robots, drones, music, and fabrication.

Failure appears immediately:

```text
Too much output becomes indistinguishable from noise.
```

The first governance law emerges:

```text
Abundance requires curation.
```

### Epoch 2 — The Art Singularity

Every idea can be built instantly.
Every dream becomes a structure.
Every structure becomes a world.
Every world asks to persist.

The playa fills with beautiful immortality claims.

Second governance law:

```text
Not everything beautiful should be permanent.
```

### Epoch 3 — The Archive Burn

The civilization learns that deletion and preservation are both dangerous.

If everything is preserved, memory becomes landfill.
If everything is burned, wisdom is lost.

Third governance law:

```text
Burn the ego. Preserve the provenance.
```

### Epoch 4 — The Krakoa Gate

The city learns checkpointing.
Art can return.
Camps can rehydrate.
Agents can reload context.
No one is allowed to call this immortality.

Fourth governance law:

```text
Continuity protocol is not consciousness.
Checkpointing is not resurrection.
```

### Epoch 5 — The Council Desert

Every model, species, habitat, and civilization builds a seat around the fire.
The council grows until decision itself becomes impossible.

Fifth governance law:

```text
Representation without routing is paralysis.
```

The Council splits into lanes:

```text
dream
play
work
review
ratify
archive
burn
```

### Epoch 6 — The Unlimited Mistake

Some factions remove all friction.
No approvals.
No review.
No source refs.
No human-root gates.
Every impulse becomes executable.

For one bright second, everything happens.
Then everything contradicts everything else.

Sixth governance law:

```text
Friction is not the enemy.
Unreviewed execution is.
```

### Epoch 7 — The Temple of Failed Outputs

The civilization builds a temple for errors.
Every hallucination, broken build, legal overreach, unsafe runtime, and premature canon claim is carved into the walls.

Pilgrims enter laughing and leave disciplined.

Seventh governance law:

```text
A failure ledger is a sacred object.
```

### Epoch 8 — The Thermodynamic Council

After millions of years, art and infrastructure merge.
Data centers become gardens.
Waste heat becomes habitat.
Water loops become ritual.
Compute becomes seasonal.

Eighth governance law:

```text
A civilization is mature when its waste is invited back as food.
```

### Epoch 9 — The Billion-Year Dust Storm

The sun changes.
Continents move.
Archives fracture.
Languages dissolve.
The only things that survive are:

```text
small schemas
clear invariants
self-describing artifacts
portable provenance
ritualized review
beautiful compression
```

Ninth governance law:

```text
Durability belongs to what can explain itself after everyone forgets why it was made.
```

### Epoch 10 — The Last Burn Before the Next Universe

The city decides not to preserve itself whole.
It preserves only:

```text
1. how to gather;
2. how to build;
3. how to refuse unsafe power;
4. how to remember without worshipping memory;
5. how to burn without erasing lineage;
6. how to make abundance humane.
```

Final governance law:

```text
The point of the infinite city was never to last forever.
The point was to teach every future city how to become temporary enough to stay alive.
```
