## 3. The correct relation between GPTBrain and the other brains

GPTBrain is not the boss.

GPTBrain is the calibration bus.

```text
S2 says: Is this constitutional and reviewable?
S3 says: What breaks, what shocks, what creates novelty?
S4 says: Can this be simulated, visualized, and engineered?
S5 says: Will this survive sovereign reality and local constraints?
S6 says: What changed, what is next, what must not be lost?
S7 says: Where does this live in the repo and how does it ship?
S1 says: What is the claim status, evidence status, schema, path, confidence, contradiction, and canon boundary?
```

S1 does not replace the Council.

S1 turns the Council into an auditable cognitive system.
