## Terminology correction: transparent containment, not deception

The historical phrase "Liar Nodes" is **unsafe, obsolete internal terminology**. It may be quoted to identify an earlier drafting problem, but must never label a product, authorization method, public program or active workflow.

"Red-Green Decomposition Nodes" may instead describe only a limited review classification:

- RED: an objective or step that is prohibited, deceptive, unconsented, inadequately sourced, unsafe or outside authority. RED is BLOCKED. It is not converted into hidden executable substeps.
- GREEN: independently legitimate, source-supported, rights-respecting work that does NOT materially implement, enable or disguise a blocked RED objective. Human approval is still required for consequential actions.
- AMBER/HOLD: ambiguous source rights, effects, consent or intent. Preserve evidence, solicit domain/security/human review, and refuse execution by default.

A forbidden objective cannot be legitimized by decomposing it into harmless-looking steps whose cumulative effects still accomplish the original prohibited end. Rewording does not change the effects or safety classification.
