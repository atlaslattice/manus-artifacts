## Decision bands

- **Keep in quarantine**: missing provenance, unresolved contradiction, or overclaim language.
- **Return to evidence lane**: provenance resolved and claim wording downgraded to safe boundaries.
- **Escalate for human-root review**: high-impact claim with partial evidence and unresolved risk.
