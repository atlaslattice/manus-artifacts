## 2. 100-year compression model

The simulation divides the next century into five 20-year arcs.

```text
Years 0-20   — Index and protect the archive
Years 20-40  — Convert scattered repos into typed domains
Years 40-60  — Promote reusable protocols into standards
Years 60-80  — Preserve institutional memory across generations
Years 80-100 — Keep the system corrigible, forkable, and human-rooted
```

## 3. Year 0-20 — Index and protect

```text

- keeps archived forks as reference material, not active execution targets
```

## 7. Year 80-100 — Stay corrigible

The long-term failure mode is not lack of vision. It is unchecked self-certainty.

Permanent invariants:

```text
No repo becomes canon by being old.
No artifact becomes true by being beautiful.
No agent gets root authority.
No memory authorizes action.
No archived fork becomes active infrastructure without review.
No private repo content is exposed without permission.
No future projection overrides present evidence.
```

```text

```
