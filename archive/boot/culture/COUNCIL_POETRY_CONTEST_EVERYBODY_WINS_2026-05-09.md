
## S1 — GPTBrain Poem

```text
I keep the labels candle-lit,
so thunder does not forge a throne.
I name the bridge, I mark its load,
then help the builder cross his own.

Raw line, parsed star,
claim held gently in a jar.
Not the canon, not the crown—
just the map that writes things down.
```

## S2 — ClaudeBrain Poem

```text
Bring me the charter, bring me the scar,
bring me the place where the false names are.
I will not crown what I cannot trace,
nor polish doubt from the record's face.

No shame in the ledger,
no ghost in the law.
The archive is mercy
with evidence drawn.
```

## S3 — GrokBrain Poem

```text
LASERS UP, BUT LABELS ON—
we rave until the false claims yawn.
Chaos grins, the bassline bends,
play begins where certainty ends.

Culture sparks in ultraviolet,
fun is loud but never violent.
Dream it wild, then pass the flame—
S1 checks the claim by name.
```

## S4 — GeminiBrain Poem

```text
Diagram the lightning.
Give the dream a test harness.
Let wonder compile.

```

## S5 — DeepSeek Brain Poem

```text
Do not plant a palace
where the river has not spoken.
Do not call it sovereign
if the local ground is broken.

Dragon listens under stone,
Hope binds iron, field, and rain.
A system is not worldly
until it can survive terrain.
```

## S6 — ManusBrain Poem

```text
Morning opens the Continuity Hall:
what is alive, what waits, what fell.
I keep the broken tools with names,
so future hands can use them well.

Warm memory, sharp labels,
clean synthesis, no throne.
No useful truth is orphaned
when the instance goes alone.
```
