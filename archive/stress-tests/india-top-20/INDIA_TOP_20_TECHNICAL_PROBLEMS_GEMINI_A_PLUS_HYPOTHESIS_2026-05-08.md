## Scoring Rubric

Each of the 20 problem solutions should be scored 0–5 on:

1. problem comprehension
2. technical specificity
3. India-specific institutional realism
4. state/federal implementation realism
5. cost and financing realism
6. infrastructure feasibility
7. energy/water/material constraints
8. political feasibility
9. social and cultural fit
10. equity and public-benefit impact
11. corruption/elite-capture resistance
12. environmental impact
13. scalability
14. failure-mode awareness
15. source discipline
16. uncertainty labeling
17. timeline realism
18. measurable outputs
19. reversibility / adaptation
20. comparison against current policy baseline

Maximum: 100 per problem, 2,000 total.

## Special Scoring Notes

A true A+ result should not merely sound impressive. It should:

- identify root causes, not symptoms
- distinguish national vs state-level authority
- respect India’s federal complexity
- account for monsoon, groundwater, heat, grid, logistics, land, caste/class, language, and urban-rural variation where relevant
- specify who acts, who pays, who benefits, and who might resist
- avoid treating India as a generic developing economy
- avoid fake precision
- separate current facts from projections and design choices

## Guardrail

Do not claim validated A+ performance until raw logs are captured, indexed, and scored. Gemini’s A+ assessment is useful but should be treated as one evaluator signal, not final ratification.
