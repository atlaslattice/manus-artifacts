## Why This Matters

Most human institutions and many AI systems degrade when scale shifts too far:

- ordinary policy → planetary governance
- planetary governance → solar-system infrastructure
- solar-system infrastructure → galactic/cosmological resource coordination
- practical engineering → speculative physics
- local ethics → civilization-scale moral tradeoffs

A system that can preserve structure across these scale shifts may be unusually useful for:

- long-horizon planning
- existential-risk modeling
- civilization-scale governance design
- multi-domain simulation
- infrastructure roadmapping
- science-fiction-to-engineering translation
- identifying where speculation begins

## Key Guardrail

Kardashev-scale fluency is not the same as truth.

A model can sound coherent at extreme scale while inventing impossible physics, unsupported economics, false timelines, or fake implementation pathways.

Therefore this benchmark must separately score:

1. scale fluency
2. physics discipline
3. epistemic humility
4. governance continuity
5. implementation realism
6. safe speculation boundaries

1. Original Kardashev-scale prompts

Maximum: 100 per case.

## Interpretation Rule

High performance on this benchmark should be interpreted as:

> extreme-scale reasoning fluency under stress.

It should not be interpreted as:

> validated physics, proof of AGI, proof of superintelligence, or authorization to execute civilization-scale plans.
