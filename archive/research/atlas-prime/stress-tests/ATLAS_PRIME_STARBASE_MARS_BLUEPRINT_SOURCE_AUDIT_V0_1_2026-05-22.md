## Executive Summary

This blueprint explores a regenerative system where compute and energy infrastructure generate valuable byproducts such as heat for food, water, and thermal systems, while supporting Starship operations and Mars industrialization.

Core architecture:

```text
Compute / Energy Hub
  → Waste Heat Recovery
  → Agriculture + Water + District Heating
  → Propellant / Manufacturing Loops
  → Mars ISRU Bootstrap
```

```text

```
