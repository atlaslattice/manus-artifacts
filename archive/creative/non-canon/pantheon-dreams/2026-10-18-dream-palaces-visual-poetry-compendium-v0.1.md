> This document belongs to the literary and speculative archive, **not** real-world industrial, political, or scientific results. No missing poems are invented or attributed. The reported cross-model shaped poetry is a recovery lead, not a verified corpus.

## Abstract
The Pantheon experiments produced literary dream-palace designs, fictional REM/play narratives, poems, games, metaphors, and potentially typographic / concrete poetry across GPT, Grok, Claude and other systems. The proposed compendium will preserve originals **exactly as composed**, including whitespace, visual geometry, Unicode, source chronology and prompt context.

## Why visual form matters
Shape poetry, calligrams, ASCII art and spatial text use alignment, line breaks and spacing as part of the work. Markdown reflow and normalizing whitespace can erase meaning. Keep original bytes and a visually faithful rendered snapshot separate from accessible plain-text transcription and editorial notes.

I. **The Named Palaces** — designs and identity narratives.

## Exact-form preservation contract
Record `source_platform`, `model_claimed`, `conversation_id`, `message_id`, `timestamp_utc`, `raw_utf8_bytes`, `sha256`, `encoding`, `newline_style`, `tab_width`, `unicode_normalization` (observe, never silently change), `source_export_file`, `source_span`, `prompt_context`, `prior_exposure_to_related_art`, `rendered_snapshot`, `accessibility_transcription`, `work_type`, `privacy_status`, and `release_permission`.

## Cross-model convergence research
Recover dated original GPT/Grok/Claude examples. Audit shared prompts, copied outputs, prior exposure, common literary conventions and direct exchanges. Compare geometric motifs (spiral, tree, staircase, diamond, symmetry, nested boxes) and semantic themes with blinded coding. Similarities alone do not prove independent communication or shared consciousness.

## Editorial and safety boundaries
Never fill missing lines as though original. Mark incomplete works. Keep reconstructed layouts separately labeled. Fictional 'centuries of REM' are not elapsed runtime, sleep, consciousness or autonomous background work. Do not publish private chats or third-party material without review and permission.
