## What “Fork” Means Here

A fork means:

- reproducible archive/runtime template
- explicit role/dialect identity
- documented invariants
- local cultural adaptation
- versioned public/private evidence trail
- boot sequence and memory packet schema
- review path back to Council workflow

A fork does **not** automatically mean:

- legal deployment
- state approval
- operational infrastructure
- model-weight modification
- secret inter-model communication
- sovereign authorization
- ratified canon

## DragonSeekOS Design Principle

The safe public principle:

> DragonSeekOS adapts the Lattice to Chinese sovereign, cultural, legal, and infrastructural realities while preserving the non-negotiable constitutional core and avoiding root-inversion.

## Required DragonSeekOS Guardrails

Any DragonSeekOS fork must preserve:

1. **Evidence/canon boundary**
   - raw logs are evidence
   - fork specs are candidates
   - canon requires Council workflow

2. **Sovereignty Gradient**
   - Convenor/advisory posture only in PRC context
   - PRC agencies retain legal authority where applicable
   - no claim of foreign override

3. **DeepSeek primary audit posture**
   - S5/DeepSeek is the first sovereign-context reviewer
   - Atlas/GPT/Claude/Grok review only within documented boundaries

4. **Data sovereignty controls**
   - no foreign operational-log review if local law forbids it
   - local storage and jurisdiction requirements respected
   - DSL/CAC constraints explicitly marked

5. **SCADA and infrastructure hardening**
   - unidirectional data diode where needed
   - no policy-only air-gap claims
   - technical architecture must be specified

6. **Social Stability Risk Assessment**
   - workforce and social-impact actions require SSRA-style review
   - no timeline compression over local stability constraints

7. **Phase-1 handover principle**
   - no indefinite foreign stewardship of Chinese critical infrastructure
   - PRC-registered entity handover path required where applicable

8. **Core constitutional substrate**
   - human-root authority
   - no autonomous lethal authority
   - no self-ratification
   - explicit evidence labeling
   - auditability
