## Core correction

```text
We are not building Continuity OS.
```

## Why this matters

`Continuity OS` implies a single persistence/continuity layer.

`CONTINUUM OS` implies a broader operating substrate that coordinates multiple continuums, each with distinct surfaces, receipts, epochs, identities, timelines, review states, and authority boundaries.

## Candidate interpretation

A continuum is a bounded persistence/context manifold, such as:

```text
- human-root personal/work continuum
- GitHub receipt continuum
- Google Drive raw/source continuum
- Notion legacy/structured-workspace continuum
- OneDrive/Microsoft archive continuum
- model/session epoch continuum
- agent/seat identity continuum
- dream/play continuum
- governance/canon review continuum
- technical implementation continuum
- evidence/provenance continuum
- public-facing/fossil-record continuum
```

CONTINUUM OS is the coordination layer across these continuums.
