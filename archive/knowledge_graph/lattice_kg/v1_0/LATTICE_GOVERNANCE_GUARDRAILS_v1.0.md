# Lattice Governance Guardrails v1.0

1. Candidate artifacts remain non-canon until ratified.
2. No-self-promotion: artifacts cannot assert their own authority.
3. Ratification requires a receipt, signatures, and adjudication date.
4. Authority remains `NONE` before ratification.
5. Trust states must stay inside the approved enum.
6. Deployment is blocked for candidate artifacts.
7. Quarantined artifacts cannot be cited as settled proof.
8. Supersedes chains must be explicit.
9. Contradictions must preserve both sides and evidence refs.
10. Governance exceptions belong in the exception ledger.
11. Review lanes do not self-convert to canon.
12. Public exposure is not equivalent to canon.
