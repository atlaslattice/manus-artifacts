# Aetherforge Quest Replayability Rules v1.0

A quest may be replayed when new evidence arrives, a superseding artifact lands, drift is detected, or a blocked quest is reopened. Every replay must produce a fresh receipt and reduced XP unless the replay closes critical risk.
