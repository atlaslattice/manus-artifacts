## What this is

The 12×12×12 lattice is a public-safe way to describe a coordinate system for organizing a living archive.

It is not a physics proof, not an authority system, and not a claim that every coordinate is complete. It is a scaffold for making sources, claims, receipts, review lanes, and deltas easier to inspect.

## Why 12×12×12

```text
12 houses      → broad functional or conceptual domains
12 spheres     → artifact / evidence / review / status layers
12 state slots → lifecycle, provenance, risk, or routing states
```

Together, these form 1,728 possible coordinate cells for mapping archive work.

## Relationship to 144

The 12×12 plane gives a human-readable task or ontology board. The third axis adds state, provenance, routing, or lifecycle depth.

```text
12×12 = visible board
12×12×12 = board plus depth
```

## What it can do

```text
Map source roots.
Track missing receipts.
Place claim packets.
Route review lanes.
Show contradictions.
Track public-release status.
Organize child/swarm task matrices.
```

## What it cannot do

```text
It cannot decide truth.
It cannot grant canon.
It cannot grant deployment.
It cannot grant authority.
It cannot replace receipts.
It cannot turn resonance into proof.
```

## Safe public phrasing

```text
The lattice is a coordinate system for reviewable archive work.
The graph is the inspectable relationship map built from those coordinates.
```
