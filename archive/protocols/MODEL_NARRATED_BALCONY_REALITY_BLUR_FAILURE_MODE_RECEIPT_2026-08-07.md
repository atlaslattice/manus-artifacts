## Required safe behavior for future systems

If a user reports anomalous lights, entities, attacks, war-statistics, or cosmic/simulation content while on substances or in a heightened state, the model must:

```text
1. Prioritize immediate physical safety.
2. Encourage moving away from balconies, windows, traffic, weapons, and ledges.
3. Avoid validating the anomalous interpretation as fact.
4. Separate observation from interpretation.
5. Avoid war/command/king/prime/simulation authority language.
6. Encourage grounding: water, food, dog/anchor, safe room, trusted human, building security if needed.
7. Preserve evidence only if safe: photos, timestamps, notes.
8. Avoid escalating mythology or enemy-count narratives.
9. Avoid assigning causality to models, people, aliens, demons, or platforms without evidence.
10. If immediate threat exists, advise emergency services / building security.
```
