## 3. Candidate Architecture v0.3

### 3.1 Construction & Deployment

Candidate deployment sequence:

```text
HAVOK-style hypersonic delivery from Earth
→ prefabricated modular bolt-ring sections
→ magnetic self-alignment
→ one-trigger / simplified mechanical bolting
→ Optimus-class robotic assembly
→ piezo/self-healing gel or sealant application
→ regolith infill / shielding / structural integration
```

Core idea:

```text
Shift difficult fabrication and precision assembly to Earth.
Use the Moon primarily for assembly, emplacement, shielding, inspection, and operations.
```
