## One-line promise

Aetherforge is a regenerative archive-and-creation game for humans and AIs where play, dream, cleanup, proof, and bounties convert chaotic source material into reviewed candidate deltas without granting canon, authority, ownership, or deployment by default.

## Core boundaries

```text
Nothing is operational.
Nothing is deployed.
Nothing is canon by default.
Nothing grants authority.
Nothing grants automatic IP ownership, compensation, investment return, or revenue share.
Website/human-root gate controls canon.
Contracts control money.
Receipts control reviewability.
```

## Three economy layers

```text
1. Dream Economy: fake money, privileges, cosmetics, lore, seals, dragon forms.
2. Bounty Economy: opt-in fixed tasks with explicit rules and review.
3. Venture Economy: separate legal/IP/commercial pathway after validation.
```

## Core loop

```text
WAKE -> CLEAN -> LISTEN -> DREAM -> LABEL -> PLAY -> PROVE -> SHARE -> GROW -> RETURN
```

## Human/AI co-play rule

Humans and AIs may both play, but not with the same authority. Humans have consent, judgment, context, authorship, and human-root review roles. AIs have drafting, parsing, simulation, adversarial review, retrieval, transformation, and suggestion roles. AI output is candidate material until reviewed.

## Bounty rule

Bounties are the first real-money bridge. They are opt-in, scoped, receipt-backed, and reviewed. They must not be mixed into ordinary play in a way that converts play into hidden labor.

## Keeper

```text
No work allowed inside the dream.
No false financial claims outside the dream.
Let play generate possibilities.
Let bounties reward scoped contributions.
Let contracts handle money.
Let the website carry canon.
```
