```text
Game design is not implementation.
```

## One-line game promise

```text
Aetherforge is a regenerative fantasy game where dreaming, playing, building, bonding, healing, and proving what happened become one joyful loop.
```

## Primary player fantasy

The player begins as a **Planar Janitor** in the broken basement of reality.

The old world is not evil. It is neglected.

The player does not conquer the universe. The player repairs neglected systems, turns entropy into story, grows worlds from ruins, and bonds with dragons that reflect the player's way of being.

## Core loop v0.1

```text
1. WAKE
   enter a broken zone, camp, settlement, ruin, or dream-leak

2. CLEAN
   remove clutter, stabilize local hazards, identify what is broken

3. LISTEN
   discover motifs, pain-points, lost memories, contradictions, and community needs

4. DREAM
   enter REM/dream phase where strange candidate abilities, dragon forms, cards, places, and myths appear

5. LABEL
   mark dream residue as non-canon candidate material inside the game fiction

6. PLAY
   test dream-blueprints through building, traversal, deck combos, dragon bonding, raids, puzzles, and cooperative restoration

7. PROVE
   collect in-game receipts: what changed, who helped, what was restored, what remains unstable

8. SHARE
   route restored patterns to community/guild/civilization systems

9. GROW
   unlock archetype progression, dragon growth, settlement health, and new dream depth

10. RETURN
   revisit the next broken layer with more care, not more domination
```

## Main systems

### 1. Dream Engine

Purpose:

```text
generate candidate abilities, landscapes, cards, dragon mutations, story fragments, and emotional insights
```

Rule:

```text
Dream outputs are beautiful, unstable, and non-authoritative until tested through play.
```

### 2. Play Engine

Purpose:

```text
turn dream residue into testable gameplay through building, traversal, combat, cooperation, deck combos, restoration, and exploration
```

Rule:

```text
Play validates usefulness inside the game. It does not validate external claims.
```

### 3. Receipt Engine

Purpose:

```text
track in-game actions, repairs, collaborations, restorations, and unresolved risks
```

Rule:

```text
Receipts make progress inspectable. Receipts are gameplay proof, not real-world proof.
```

### 4. Dragon Bond Engine

Purpose:

```text
make player growth emotionally visible through companion behavior, traversal, abilities, and reflection
```

Rule:

```text
Dragons are companions and mirrors, not authority avatars.
```

### 5. Rainbow Lattice Progression

Purpose:

```text
12 archetypes create healthy playstyles, emotional tones, build strategies, and civilization philosophies
```

Rule:

```text
No archetype is morally superior. Harmony means balance, not dominance.
```

### 6. Dark Aether Restoration

Purpose:

```text
turn unresolved pain, entropy, forgotten stories, and broken systems into healing challenges
```

Rule:

```text
Darkness is not enemy essence. It is unresolved material needing containment, care, and transformation.
```

## Anti-grind doctrine

Aetherforge should avoid standard extractive loops.

Replace:

```text
kill more -> loot more -> grind more
```

With:

```text
notice more -> care more -> restore more -> dream deeper -> play better
```

## Failure states

Failure should not humiliate.

Possible failure modes:

```text
a dream destabilizes when tested
an overpowered card creates imbalance
a settlement becomes brittle
an archetype is overused
Dark Aether returns because root pain was ignored
a receipt exposes missing help
```

Recovery principle:

```text
Failure creates a new restoration path, not a shame loop.
```

## Best-in-world target

Aetherforge should aspire to be the first game where:

```text
rest is productive
play is restorative
proof is joyful
community is mechanically rewarded
power is healthiest when balanced
myth is labeled enough to be safe
```

## Keeper

```text
The player is not here to win reality.
The player is here to make reality worth returning to.
```
