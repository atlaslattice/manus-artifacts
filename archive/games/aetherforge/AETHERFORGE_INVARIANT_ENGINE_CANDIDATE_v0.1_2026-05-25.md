Aetherforge is described as a playable constitutional operating system: a game that fuses Atlas Lattice, Microsoft ecosystem mappings, dream/play cycles, dragons, regenerative civilization building, and invariant-based progression into a creative universe.

The source text explicitly frames the rewrite as:

## Core fantasy

The player begins as a **Planar Janitor** in the broken basement of reality. The old simulation is collapsing. Dreams leak. Systems glitch. Dark Aether pools in the cracks.

The fantasy is regenerative repair:

```text
fix what is broken
grow what is barren
ride dragons born from dreams
build civilizations that heal the world
```
