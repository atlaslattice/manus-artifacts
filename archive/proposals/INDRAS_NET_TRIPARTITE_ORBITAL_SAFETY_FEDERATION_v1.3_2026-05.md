## Proposal One-Line Frame

A framework where the United States, India, China, and leading orbital operators each preserve sovereign control while gaining a shared safety layer for Earth’s orbital environment.

## Executive Summary

Indra’s Net is a **tripartite orbital safety federation** that gives every major player a clear, defensible win while protecting humanity’s shared orbital environment.

It is not world government in space.  
It is not one nation dominating the commons.  
It is three sovereign jewels keeping their own power while federating the safety layer that protects everyone.

## Core Architecture

### I. Shared Civil Commons

A narrow but critical layer operates as a shared regenerative commons:

- Collision avoidance and space traffic management
- Debris tracking
- Space weather monitoring and early warning
- Emergency routing and humanitarian bandwidth

This commons is governed by **plural civilizational principles**, with dharmic traditions contributing a non-domination, stewardship, and duty-of-care frame.

### II. Sovereign Carve-Outs

Every jewel retains permanent, non-negotiable control over:

- All military and classified systems
- National security data and operations
- Sovereign decision-making authority

### III. Constitutional Unanimity Lock

Any change to the core architecture requires **unanimous agreement** among the three jewels.

### IV. Contribution-Credit Economic Layer

Value flows through a transparent contribution-credit system. Each jewel receives benefit in proportion to what it contributes:

- bandwidth
- launch capacity
- manufacturing
- compute
- ground stations
- disaster response
- debris / space-weather data
- emergency access

### V. Gray-Zone Dual-Use Protocol

When civil commons data becomes materially dual-use — change in specificity, latency, targeting relevance, or military operational utility — it is routed through a restricted review channel with minimum necessary visibility to all three jewels and full audit logs.

### VI. Verification Without Exposure

The mesh uses cryptographic attestations, zero-knowledge proofs, third-party audit enclaves, and tamper-evident logs to verify contribution, uptime, emergency compliance, and safety behavior — without exposing classified payloads, military routing, proprietary models, or sovereign command systems.

### VII. Abuse / Non-Compliance Clause

If any jewel materially abuses civil commons access, withholds required safety telemetry during a verified emergency, falsifies contribution claims, or attempts to use the commons to compromise another jewel’s sovereign systems, emergency access may be restricted through pre-agreed procedures.

All restrictions require:

- tamper-evident logging
- minimum-necessary scope
- post-incident review by the Three-Jewel Mediation Board

### VIII. Planetary Emergency / Carrington Clause

In any verified planetary-scale orbital or solar emergency, the three jewels automatically activate pre-agreed emergency protocols and act as one for collision avoidance, space-weather rerouting, emergency bandwidth, and critical infrastructure continuity — without transferring military command or sovereign ownership.

### IX. Voluntary Federation of Additional Jewels

No sovereign constellation is subject to involuntary absorption. Additional nations or operators may apply for voluntary federation under transparent Indra’s Net rules. Additional participants may join as federated jewels without altering the three-core constitutional structure unless unanimously elevated by the original core jewels.

## Final One-Liner

> Indra’s Net is a tripartite orbital safety federation in which sovereign space powers retain full national control while contributing narrowly scoped civil telemetry, emergency coordination, and orbital continuity services to a shared commons for the benefit of Earth.
