## Proposed project-spec paragraph

"The Planetarium Revival Series is an independently developed, exploratory program investigating original science-informed fulldome experiences, educational accessibility and responsible cultural collaboration. We are preparing synthetic technical demonstrations and voluntary customer-discovery questions. Potential venues, scientific reviewers, cultural collaborators, sponsors and distribution partners are **prospective contacts only** unless separately documented in signed, current evidence. No partnership, endorsement, licensing, paid booking, community authorization, governmental approval, funding or production installation is represented by this draft. Scientific, educational, legal, accessibility and cultural claims will be independently reviewed before external release."

## Required human-controlled gates

No automatic venue outreach, solicitation of personal information, real customer testing involving minors, vendor charges, use of third-party trademarks as sponsorship, Indigenous/cultural content extraction, or external licensing statements. Treat all outreach as a separately authorized operation. Preserve negative feedback, accessibility constraints and community refusal without converting a refusal into an optimization target.
