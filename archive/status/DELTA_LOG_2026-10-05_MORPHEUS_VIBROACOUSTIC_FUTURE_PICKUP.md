## 2. Vibroacoustics separates into distinct physical regimes

Do not collapse all acoustic effects into one mechanism.

### A. Sonochemistry / sonoelectrochemistry

Candidate mechanisms:
- cavitation
- boundary-layer thinning
- altered mass transport
- electrode-surface cleaning/activation
- local pressure/temperature transients
- reaction-rate changes
- isotope-dependent kinetic/vibrational effects

Primary relevance:
- hydrogen/deuterium electrochemistry
- isotope-separation process optimization
- water/electrolysis systems

Status:
```text
REAL FIELD / ESTABLISHED GENERAL MECHANISMS
H/D-SPECIFIC PROCESS ADVANTAGE: TESTABLE, NOT ASSUMED
```

### B. Low-frequency combustion / fire-flow coupling

Candidate mechanisms:
- flame deformation
- combustion-zone airflow disturbance
- acoustic particle velocity
- mist/flame interaction
- ember/spot-ignition perturbation
- phased-field steering

Primary relevance:
- incipient-fire suppression
- acoustic + mist augmentation
- established-fire scaling tests

Status:
```text
SUPPORTED / EARLY-STAGE at small controlled scales
LARGE-SCALE DEPLOYMENT: HYPOTHESIS
```

### C. Bioacoustic / substrate-borne vibration

Candidate mechanisms:
- insect communication disruption
- mechanosensory interference
- substrate-borne signal detection
- localized plant/trellis/soil actuation
- closed-loop pest response

Primary relevance:
- precision agriculture
- pest detection/deterrence
- ecological sensing

Status:
```text
TARGET-SPECIFIC / EXPERIMENTAL
NO UNIVERSAL PEST FREQUENCY
```

## 5. Convergence worth preserving

A useful cross-domain pattern is emerging:

```text
controlled fields can alter transport, interfaces, signaling, and local state
```

Different domains may use that pattern through different mechanisms:

```text
H/D electrochemistry
  -> transport / interfacial kinetics / vibrational isotope effects

fire suppression
  -> flow field / particle velocity / mist-flame coupling

pest control
  -> sensory / mechanical / substrate-borne signaling

materials / structures
  -> resonance / stress / wave propagation
```

This is a convergence in control vocabulary, not proof of one universal frequency law.

## 6. Research question worth keeping

A disciplined PT2.0 question:

```text
What remains invariant as the same physical identity moves through
different states, environments, and scales — and what changes?
```

For isotope separation:

```text
Can a controlled acoustic environment reshape the pathway an isotope
must travel through strongly enough to change measurable selectivity?
```

For fire:

```text
Can a controlled acoustic field reshape the local flow/mist environment
strongly enough to improve suppression versus matched controls?
```

For pests:

```text
Can target-specific mechanical/acoustic pathways be perturbed selectively
without broad ecological harm?
```
