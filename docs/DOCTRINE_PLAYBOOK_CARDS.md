# Doctrine Playbook Cards
Status: Candidate
Date: 2026-05-28

Five one-page doctrine cards for operations and onboarding.

## Card 1 — Candidate-First Governance
- Goal: Preserve trust boundaries before publication.
- Trigger: Artifact promotion discussion.
- Required evidence: ratification_event_id, canon_status, trust_state.

## Card 2 — Cross-Domain Linking
- Goal: Keep the lattice navigable and connected.
- Trigger: New artifact creation.
- Required evidence: inbound and outbound links.

## Card 3 — Validation Before Promotion
- Goal: Ensure reproducibility and quality.
- Trigger: Release or public claim update.
- Required evidence: script/test receipts.

## Card 4 — Provenance Tiering
- Goal: Match claim strength to evidence strength.
- Trigger: Sensitive or strategic claims.
- Required evidence: tier classification and source chain.

## Card 5 — Mission-Control Cadence
- Goal: Sustain weekly, monthly, quarterly governance rhythm.
- Trigger: recurring operating cycle.
- Required evidence: packet, digest, and risk updates.
