1. **Choose the audience** — newcomer, researcher, builder, governor, or contributor.
2. **Find the friction point** — unclear title, no summary, weak navigation, or missing context.
3. **Tighten the artifact** — improve headings, summaries, and related-doc links.
4. **Add traversal links** — connect the polished page to explainers, reading lists, or implementation surfaces.
5. **Check public legibility** — confirm a first-time reader can understand what the artifact is and why it matters.
6. **Validate if needed** — run existing checks when the change affects trust- or graph-relevant surfaces.
7. **Emit receipt** — log the polish quest, before/after effect, and remaining follow-up work.
