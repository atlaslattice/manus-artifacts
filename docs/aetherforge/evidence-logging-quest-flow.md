1. **Identify the AI-built artifact** — choose the document, code surface, or schema needing explicit evidence.
2. **Gather the claim** — state what was built, by whom/what system, and what part is being evidenced.
3. **Write the evidence entry** — include source paths, validation context, and trust-relevant notes.
4. **Link it in the registry or packet** — connect the evidence to the target artifact or ratification packet.
5. **Validate the schema** — confirm the entry matches the evidence schema/field expectations and log the receipt.
