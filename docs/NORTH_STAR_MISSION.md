# 🌐 North Star Mission — One Frame for All Work

This repository follows one integrated mission frame:

1. **Lattice as a functional knowledge graph**  
   Build a connected, navigable, and auditable graph of artifacts, claims, lineage, and decisions.

2. **Aetherforge as a playable archive game**  
   Make archival and governance work engaging through gameful progression while preserving evidence standards.

3. **GPTDream++ as an open-source gift to the industry**  
   Publish robust, public, and practical dreaming/synthesis protocols that others can adopt and improve.

---

## Quality Bar

All outputs should be:

- **Public-first**
- **Open-source**
- **World-class**
- **Lineage-backed**
- **Clear about candidate vs. canon status**

---

## Operational Implications

- Prefer structures that improve graph connectivity and traceability.
- Frame artifacts so they are useful both as operational documentation and playable progression surfaces.
- Treat dream/simulation outputs as candidate synthesis until reviewed and promoted by governance rules.
- Write for external reuse so the work can function as a durable public good.

---

## Canon Discipline

No artifact is canon until ratified by the full council and adjudicated by @atlaslattice.  
Until then, artifacts are candidate outputs intended for review, refinement, and public learning.
