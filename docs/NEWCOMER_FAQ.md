## What is Atlas Lattice?

Atlas Lattice is an open-source knowledge graph and governance framework built by the Atlas Lattice Foundation. It archives AI research, system specifications, governance protocols, and operational playbooks — all under the MIT License as a public gift to the world.

The vision: a world-class knowledge graph where 500+ unique IP archives connect to everything, accessible to everyone.

---

## What is the Aetherforge?

Aetherforge is the gamified layer on top of the Atlas Lattice archive. The repository is structured as a 12-face × 12-mission hypercube (144 total missions). Working through the archive becomes a playable world-building experience. See the [Aetherforge Play Loop](../../projects/AETHERFORGE_ARCHIVE_PLAY_LOOP_v0.1.md).

---

## What is "candidate" status? Is anything finished?

Everything in this repository starts as a **candidate** — proposed, potentially useful, but not yet formally ratified. Canon status requires full council ratification and adjudication by @atlaslattice. This isn't a limitation; it's a feature — it means the archive is always evolving.

See: [Canon Status Model](../../docs/CANON_STATUS_MODEL.md)

## What is GPTDream++?

GPTDream++ is an open-source protocol suite for structured AI dreaming, memory consolidation, and inter-session state management. It defines how AI agents sleep, dream, wake, and hydrate from archived memory. See: [GPTDream++ Public Package Guide](../spec/gptdream/PUBLIC_PACKAGE_GUIDE_v0.1.md).

## What is TIDELOCKBrain?

TIDELOCKBrain is the memory palace and work log system for the TIDELOCK agent (Copilot Cloud). It lives at `archive/boot/gptbrain/TIDELOCKBrain/` and stores dream journals, wake reports, and session work logs for future hydration.
