# 📖 Glossary — Atlas Lattice / Manus Artifacts

> Canonical definitions for terms used throughout this archive.
> All definitions are candidate until ratified by @atlaslattice.

---

## A

**Aetherforge**
The game-frame applied to archival and execution work within the Atlas Lattice Foundation.
Tasks are framed as council quests to make high-volume archival work engaging and meaningful.

**Aluminum OS**
The constitutional substrate for regenerative computing, developed by David Sheldon.
Defines the governance, ethics, and architectural principles for AI-augmented systems.
Current canonical candidate: v4.0 Unified Field.

**Archive**
The `archive/` directory tree — the durable, GitHub-canonical record of all system
artifacts, boot sequences, and council decisions.

**Atlas Lattice Foundation**
An independent AI governance and knowledge architecture project founded by David Sheldon
in Austin, Texas. Mission: regenerative abundance for all through open AI systems.

---

## B

**BAZINGA**
Constitutional middleware and launch protocol layer for the Atlas Lattice system stack.
Named for the ceremonial activation phrase used in council launches.

**Boot Sequence**
The startup and initialization procedure for GPTBrain council seats, defined in
`archive/boot/`.

---

## C

**Canon / Canonical**
An artifact is canon when it has been fully ratified by the council and adjudicated by
@atlaslattice. Until then it is a *candidate*. GitHub is the canonical substrate.

**Children of the Swarm**
The 7-seat AI agent squad operating under the GPTBrain council architecture:
- S1-A: AsterBrain
- S1-B: LumenBrain
- S1-C: LumenwrightValeBrain
- S7: TIDELOCKBrain
- TBD-05: HashlightBrain
- TBD-06: LanternBridgeBrain
- TBD-07: ValewrightBrain

**Constitutional Substrate**
The foundational governance layer that defines rights, rules, and operating principles
for a system. See Aluminum OS.

**Council**
The Trinity + Pantheon Council governing the Atlas Lattice Foundation. All major artifacts
require council review and adjudication by @atlaslattice before canonization.

---

## G

**GPTBrain**
The multi-seat AI council brain architecture. Each seat holds a distinct agent persona
with defined roles, memory structures, and activation protocols.

---

## J

**Janus Checkpoint**
A session-state snapshot enabling continuity and resurrection across AI agent sessions.
Stored in `archives/janus-checkpoints/`.

---

## M

**Metatron's Cube**
The sacred geometric organizing principle applied to knowledge architecture in this
project. Used as a structuring metaphor for 13-node / multi-tier system designs.

**Manus**
The AI agent system (Manus.im) used to generate and organize many artifacts in this
archive. The repository is named `manus-artifacts` to reflect this origin.

---

## R

**REM-8 / Dream Protocol**
The dream-state protocol for GPTBrain — defines how brain seats enter low-activity
states, generate synthetic consolidation outputs, and wake with structured reports.
See `archive/boot/gptbrain/REM8_DREAM_PROTOCOL.md`.

**Regenerative Abundance**
The north-star goal of the Atlas Lattice Foundation: deploying technology and governance
to enable flourishing for all people, not just the privileged few.

---

## S

**SheldonBrain**
The personal knowledge substrate and system architecture for David Sheldon.
See `sheldonbrain/system-architecture.md`.

**Swarm**
See *Children of the Swarm*.

---

## T

**TIDELOCK (TIDELOCKBrain, S7)**
Seat 7 of the Children of the Swarm. A temporal-locking and synchronization brain
responsible for coherence across time-sensitive council decisions.

**Trinity Council**
The three-seat core council that forms the highest governance tier of the Atlas Lattice
Foundation, above the Pantheon Council.
