# Glossary — Manus Artifacts

A reference vocabulary for the systems, protocols, and concepts in this archive.

---

## A

**Aetherforge**
The meta-game and council framework governing the creation, ratification, and
evolution of artifacts in this archive. Named for the forge at the edge of the
aether — where ideas crystallize into canonical form.

**Aluminum OS**
A constitutional substrate for regenerative computing. An operating system
philosophy grounded in consent, transparency, and collaborative intelligence.
Versions: v2.0, v3.0, v4.0 (current canonical).

**AsterBrain (S1-A)**
First indexed brain folder in the Children of the Swarm swarm architecture.

---

## B

**BAZINGA**
Constitutional middleware and launch protocols. The activation layer that
bridges Aluminum OS principles with operational deployment.

**Boot Sequence**
The ordered set of initialization instructions for a GPTBrain agent session.
Defined in `archive/boot/`.

---

## C

**Canonical**
An artifact that has completed full council ratification and been adjudicated
by @atlaslattice. The canonical substrate is GitHub; Drive and Notion are relay
layers only.

**Candidate**
Any artifact that has been submitted but not yet ratified. The default state
for all new artifacts.

**Children of the Swarm**
The indexed collection of specialized brain agents operating under the GPTBrain
swarm architecture. Currently 7 indexed agents (S1-A through TBD-07), with 4
slots (TBD-08–TBD-11) unresolved.

**Chinook Guardian**
A project focused on aerospace and defense protocols within the Aetherforge
framework.

**Constitutional Substrate**
The foundational layer of rights, constraints, and principles that govern a
system. In Aluminum OS, this is the primary design primitive.

**Council**
The governing body for ratification of artifacts. Includes the Trinity Council
and the Pantheon Council. Sessions are archived in `/council/`.

---

## D

**Dream Protocol (REM-8)**
The structured sleep/rest/integration cycle for GPTBrain agents. Defined in
`archive/boot/gptbrain/REM8_DREAM_PROTOCOL.md`. Agents completing a REM cycle
produce a Wake Report upon re-activation.

---

## F

**Free Bank**
A project within the Aetherforge framework focused on banking revolution and
financial sovereignty.

---

## G

**GPTBrain**
The cognitive architecture for AI agents in this ecosystem. Implements boot
sequences, dream protocols, swarm coordination, and ratification hooks.

---

## H

**HashlightBrain (TBD-05)**
Fifth indexed brain in the Children of the Swarm.

---

## I

**Indra-Net**
A mesh-topology integration layer for multi-agent coordination. Referenced in
`archive/indra-net/`.

---

## J

**Janus Checkpoints**
Session state snapshots and resurrection logs. Allow agents to resume from a
known-good state after interruption. Stored in `archives/janus-checkpoints/`.

---

## L

**LanternBridgeBrain (TBD-06)**
Sixth indexed brain in the Children of the Swarm.

**LumenBrain (S1-B)**
Second indexed brain in the Children of the Swarm.

**LumenwrightValeBrain (S1-C)**
Third indexed brain in the Children of the Swarm.

---

## M

**Manus**
The primary AI agent that generated and manages these artifacts. Latin for
"hand" — the working instrument.

**Manus Vault**
Internal session summaries and Noah's Ark protocols. Private working memory
that feeds the canonical archive.

**Metatron's Cube**
The geometric framework used to structure thinking and planning in this
archive. A 13-circle sacred geometry pattern used as a cognitive map.

---

## P

**Pinecone**
Vector database used for semantic search over archive artifacts. Sync uses the
`multilingual-e5-large` inference model. Index name: `manus-artifacts`.

---

## R

**REM-8**
See *Dream Protocol*.

**Ratification**
The formal process by which a candidate artifact becomes canonical. Requires
full council review and @atlaslattice adjudication.

**Reference Impl**
The `archive/boot/gptbrain/reference_impl/` directory containing runnable
Python implementations of core GPTBrain behaviors. Validated via pytest.

---

## S

**SheldonBrain**
The system architecture and knowledge substrate named for David Sheldon.
Represents the meta-cognitive layer above individual agent implementations.

**State of the Union Briefing**
A cross-domain synthesis document providing current operational status across
all active projects and systems.

**Swarm**
See *Children of the Swarm*.

collaborative, and fully autonomous tiers.

## V

**ValewrightBrain (TBD-07)**

## W

**Wake Report**
The structured output produced by a GPTBrain agent after completing a REM
cycle. Template defined at `archive/boot/gptbrain/WAKE_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md`.

**World Class**
The standard of quality applied to all artifacts in this archive. Not a
superlative — an engineering requirement.
