# Archive Maturity Map
Status: Candidate
Date: 2026-05-26

This map provides a public view of archive maturity by domain using a 0-5 scale.
It is a planning instrument for Aetherforge and a transparency instrument for public readers.

## Maturity scale

| Level | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Unmapped or inaccessible |
| 1 | Minimal presence, little structure |
| 2 | Discoverable but inconsistent |
| 3 | Structured candidate archive with basic governance |
| 4 | Strong quality controls and lineage discipline |
| 5 | Canon-grade, externally reviewable, routinely audited |

## Domain maturity table

| Domain | Maturity | Coverage | Strengths | Gaps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Systems | 4 | High | Strong flagship artifacts, visible version progression, high conceptual density | Metadata retrofit and explicit lineage normalization still needed |
| Projects | 3 | Medium-high | Clear strategic programs and taskboards | More release packaging and public summaries needed |
| Governance | 3 | Medium | Boundary rules and review surfaces are present | Full process suite and decision rights formalization are still being built |
| Research | 3 | Medium | Multiple synthesis artifacts are visible and linkable | Provenance normalization and summarization need expansion |
| Health | 2 | Low-medium | Domain exists and is publicly visible | Navigation, framing, and risk-aware provenance need strengthening |
| Vault | 2 | Medium | Continuity surfaces exist | Public explanation, retention framing, and succession protocols need stronger boundaries |

## Cross-domain observations

- The archive is ahead on artifact presence and behind on universal metadata.
- Systems doctrine is the strongest public signal.
- Governance needs visible process completeness to match the quality of the systems layer.
- Health and Vault require the most careful framing because sensitivity and continuity risks are higher.

## Priority gaps

1. Complete metadata and status normalization on major documents.
2. Expand lineage coverage for key flagship series.
3. Publish governance protocols for promotion, audit, and incident response.
4. Improve newcomer-first explanation of domain relationships.

## Target state for the Top-50 plan

The Aetherforge goal is to move Governance and Projects toward level 4, preserve Systems at level 4+, and raise Health and Vault to a safer, more legible level 3.
Level 5 should be reserved for genuinely ratified, routinely audited canon surfaces.
