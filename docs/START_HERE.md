Welcome. This archive is the public, open-source record of the **Atlas Lattice Foundation** — a constitutional AI governance and knowledge architecture project built from Austin, Texas.

---

## What Is This?

The Atlas Lattice Foundation is building the governance layer missing from current AI stacks: a constitutional substrate for coordinating intelligence across models, sessions, and civilizational scale.

This repository contains:

- **System architectures** — Aluminum OS, SheldonBrain, BAZINGA
- **Governance protocols** — Trinity Council, Pantheon Council, Constitutional Charters
- **Research** — intelligence sweeps, convergence reports, cross-disciplinary analysis
- **Projects** — Free Bank, Chinook Guardian, Three-Tier Autonomy
- **Health** — patient rights, wellness facility research
- **Session archives** — Janus Checkpoints, council sessions, wake reports

All work is MIT licensed. All artifacts are candidates until ratified by the full council and adjudicated by @atlaslattice.
