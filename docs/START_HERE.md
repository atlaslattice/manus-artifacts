**David Sheldon** is the founder of the Atlas Lattice Foundation — an independent AI governance and knowledge architecture project built from Austin, Texas. Starting from a single question to Claude on January 12, 2025, he deployed a fully operational multi-AI coordination stack in under a year, working entirely through conversational AI while living at subsistence level and surviving two wildfire disasters.
