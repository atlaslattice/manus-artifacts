## What is this repository?

This is the public knowledge archive of the **Atlas Lattice Foundation** — a research,
governance, and AI-systems project founded by David Sheldon in Austin, Texas.

The mission: *use AI, regenerative systems design, and open knowledge to answer the
question — "Why is 90% of the planet in abject poverty when we have the technology for
regenerative abundance?"*

GitHub is the **canonical substrate** for everything here. Notion and Drive are
working/relay layers. Nothing is canon until ratified by the full council and adjudicated
by [@atlaslattice](https://github.com/atlaslattice).
