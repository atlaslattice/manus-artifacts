# Benchmark Scorecard — 2026-05-28

> *Status: CANDIDATE — not canon until ratified by @atlaslattice*

## What This Document Is

This scorecard evaluates Atlas Lattice as a public repository using the eight benchmark dimensions defined in the world-class criteria rubric. Scores are intentionally conservative: the goal is to surface the highest-leverage quality gaps before a broader public launch.

## Scorecard

| Dimension | Score | Justification | Primary gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| README quality | **4/5** | Strong mission framing and good top-level navigation already exist. | Public trust badges and landing-page polish still need work. |
| Documentation depth | **3/5** | Glossary, archive index, and decision-oriented documents are present. | Architecture diagrams and deeper system maps are still thin. |
| Governance rigor | **4/5** | Canon boundary, contribution rules, and ratification framing are clear. | The ratification queue and review pipeline are not yet public enough. |
| Knowledge graph | **3/5** | Registry foundations are in place and cross-linking is improving. | Coverage is still shallow at roughly 33 artifacts, around 6% of total files. |
| Validation automation | **3/5** | CI workflows exist and validation hardening is underway. | Validation remains new and needs deeper, broader enforcement. |
| Contributor experience | **3/5** | CONTRIBUTING exists and basic issue/PR scaffolding is present. | A clearer contributor decision tree and stronger ownership signals are still emerging. |
| Evidence & provenance | **2/5** | The evidence schema and receipts pattern now exist. | Only 3 evidence entries are currently populated, limiting audit depth. |
| Discoverability | **3/5** | Reading paths are improving and top-level docs are much stronger than before. | Topic pages and persona-specific guides remain sparse below the repo root. |

## Overall

- **Total:** **25 / 40**
- **Public quality score:** **62.5%**
- **Interpretation:** strong candidate archive with unusual governance maturity, but not yet world-class by public repository standards.

## Top 5 Gaps by Impact

1. **Expand the knowledge graph** — register and cross-link a much larger share of high-value artifacts.
2. **Increase evidence density** — move from a few receipts to a real audit trail for major AI-built claims.
3. **Publish clearer architecture maps** — make the system legible without requiring deep prior context.
4. **Harden contributor routing** — add ownership cues, decision trees, and clearer review pathways.
5. **Deepen discoverability below the root** — ensure every major domain has a strong landing page and persona-aware entry point.
