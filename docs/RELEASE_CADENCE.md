# Release Cadence
Status: Candidate
Date: 2026-05-26

This policy defines the expected rhythm for archive releases, hygiene work, and candidate-to-canon review windows.
Cadence is how Aetherforge converts aspiration into repeatable motion.

## Standard rhythm

- **Weekly** — mission control standup, stale artifact review, blocker triage
- **Monthly** — quality dashboard refresh, stewardship review, candidate batch assessment
- **Quarterly** — public state-of-archive report, roadmap review, fire drill, and canon promotion window
- **Annually** — full canon audit and trust posture review

## Candidate-to-canon rhythm

Candidate artifacts may be published continuously.
Canon promotions should be batched into explicit review windows unless urgency requires out-of-band adjudication.

## Hygiene sprint rhythm

Run archive hygiene sprints at least once per month with focus areas such as metadata retrofit, lineage cleanup, stale content review, and broken-link repair.

## Public reporting rhythm

Publish a state-of-archive update at least once per quarter summarizing maturity, risks, and major newly added candidate or canon artifacts.

## Exceptions

High-impact incidents, canon conflicts, or urgent trust issues may override the normal cadence and trigger immediate governance response.
