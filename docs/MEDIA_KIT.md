## Short description

Manus Artifacts is a public MIT-licensed archive of systems, projects, governance, research, health, and continuity materials organized for transparency, traceability, and world-class public usability.

## Key messages

- The repository is an archive program, not merely a codebase.
- All artifacts are candidates until ratified by the full council and adjudicated by @atlaslattice.
- The archive uses governance-first publication rules to protect canon integrity.
- Aetherforge is the active public improvement program making the archive easier to navigate and audit.

## FAQ

### Is this a software product repository?

No. It is primarily an archival, documentation, and governance repository with some workflow automation.

### What does “candidate” mean?

Candidate means reviewable and publicly visible, but not yet canon.

### Where should a journalist start?

Start with [BEST_OF_ARCHIVE.md](./BEST_OF_ARCHIVE.md), [EXECUTIVE_SUMMARIES.md](./EXECUTIVE_SUMMARIES.md), [TRUST_CHARTER.md](./TRUST_CHARTER.md), and [ROADMAP.md](./ROADMAP.md).

### How should claims be interpreted?

High-impact claims should be interpreted in light of provenance requirements, governance status, and published decision pathways.
