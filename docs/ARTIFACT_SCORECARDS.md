# Artifact Scorecards
Status: Candidate
Date: 2026-05-26

These scorecards provide a compact quality-health view of the major artifact families.
Scores are directional and intended to focus stewardship effort, not simulate false precision.

## Scoring dimensions

Use a 1-5 scale for each dimension.

- **Completeness** — structural and narrative finish
- **Link integrity** — navigability and internal reference health
- **Metadata coverage** — status, dates, frontmatter, lineage fields
- **Freshness** — evidence of active maintenance
- **Canon readiness** — governance maturity for authority elevation

## Score interpretation

- `5` = exemplary and externally durable
- `4` = strong and dependable with limited cleanup left
- `3` = operational but uneven
- `2` = visible yet under-governed
- `1` = present but not ready for trust-heavy use

## How to use the scorecards

- Review after major documentation batches
- Use score movement, not just static scores, as the main signal
- Pair low canon-readiness with governance action, not cosmetic polishing alone
- Treat low metadata coverage as a scaling risk across all five rings of Aetherforge

## Highest-priority improvements

1. Raise metadata coverage in Systems, Research, Health, and Vault.
2. Keep Governance at or above current readiness through decision logging and cadence discipline.
3. Improve research and health provenance to support safer public use.
