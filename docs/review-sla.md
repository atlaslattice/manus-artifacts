## Target timelines by artifact type

| Artifact type | Target first response | Target review completion |
|---|---|---|
| Documentation / guide | 3 business days | 7 business days |
| Schema / validation / workflow | 2 business days | 5 business days |
| Governance / ADR / policy | 5 business days | 10 business days |
| Evidence entry / receipt | 2 business days | 5 business days |
| Deprecation / supersession | 3 business days | 7 business days |

## Escalation path

1. Request review through the normal pull request flow.
2. If the target first-response window is missed, add a polite follow-up comment summarizing urgency.
3. If the review completion window is missed for governance-critical work, escalate to `@atlaslattice` with links to the artifact, PR, and supporting evidence.

## What happens if there is no response

If no response arrives inside the stated window, the artifact remains in candidate state. No silence-based ratification occurs. Contributors may continue to refine the artifact, but trust-state promotion still requires explicit review and adjudication.
