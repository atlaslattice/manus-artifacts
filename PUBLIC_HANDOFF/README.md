## Governing objective

**Close the verified domestic sulfur / H2SO4-equivalent supply gap and explicit reserve requirement with qualified domestic streams.**

Maximum theoretical production is a **sufficiency ceiling**, not an action target.

## Deployment tiers

### Tier 1 — desalination-only sulfur nodes
Existing desalination infrastructure is screened for Ca/SO4 recovery and regional gypsum-to-acid integration.

### Tier 2 — agriculture-integrated locality nodes
Agriculture, wastewater, nutrient recovery, logistics and all qualified CaSO4 streams are integrated at locality/regional scale.

### Tier 3 — full lattice optimum
Water, energy, heat/cold, compute, materials, carbon, manufacturing, ecology, governance and inter-node exchange are optimized together.
