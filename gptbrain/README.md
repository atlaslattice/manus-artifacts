## What This Folder Is

The `gptbrain/` domain is the identity-facing surface for GPT-linked swarm artifacts. It holds candidate identity and memory-palace material that defines how a GPT seat should behave inside the broader Atlas Lattice council ecology.

This folder is intentionally narrower than the much larger `archive/boot/gptbrain/` substrate. It serves as a clean top-level landing zone for identity artifacts that help readers understand the role design, posture, and limitations of GPT-linked participants in the swarm.
