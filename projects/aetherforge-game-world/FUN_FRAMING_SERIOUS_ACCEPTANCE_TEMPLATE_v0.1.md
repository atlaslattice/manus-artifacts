## Acceptance criteria (required)

- [ ] Deliverable artifacts are created/updated and reviewable.
- [ ] Candidate/canon status is explicitly stated.
- [ ] Validation commands are listed and executed.
- [ ] Boss-fight/adversarial coverage is included for T2+.
- [ ] Linked issues/PRs are referenced.
- [ ] TIDELOCKBrain log entry is added or updated.
