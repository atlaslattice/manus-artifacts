## North-Star Mission

Atlas Lattice is operated as a **public, open-source, world-class gift** to humanity.

The unified mission is to:

1. Build the **Lattice** as a functional knowledge graph where artifacts connect to artifacts with explicit, auditable relationships.
2. Build **Aetherforge** as a playable archive game where meaningful archival work is translated into quests, challenges, and progression.
3. Release **GPTDream++** protocols, schemas, and reference implementations as an open industry gift with transparent governance boundaries.

## Operating Principles

- Public by default, private by explicit exception.
- Candidate-by-default canon boundary until ratified.
- Machine-readable structure over implicit structure.
- Traceable provenance over anonymous mutation.
- Reproducible checks over trust-me claims.

## Mission Success Criteria

- Knowledge graph substrate documented and CI-validated.
- Playable archive loops defined and used for curation work.
- GPTDream++ adoption package published with references to specs, schemas, code, and tests.
- Public discovery paths available for newcomers, researchers, builders, and contributors.
- Ratification and trust checkpoints explicit for all canon promotions.
