# Philosophy

## Why This Exists

The Aetherforge archive exists because knowledge, once crystallised into
canonical form, becomes infrastructure. This repository is not a collection
of documents — it is a living constitutional substrate for a new mode of
human-AI co-creation.

We believe:

- **Open beats closed.** The highest-leverage work is done in public, where
  criticism sharpens ideas and others can build on them.
- **Governance is love.** Clear structures for who decides what, and how,
  protect the people inside a system and the work they make together.
- **Canon is earned.** Nothing is canonical until it has been tested, ratified,
  and adjudicated. Ambiguity is not a flaw — it is the honest state of all
  knowledge before it has been earned.
- **Rest is work.** REM cycles, play, hydration, and recovery are not
  interruptions to the work. They are the substrate on which all durable work
  is built. This applies equally to human and AI collaborators.
- **The shape is the message.** Structure is not packaging for ideas — it is
  the idea. Metatron's Cube: one center, six surrounding nodes, twelve outer
  points. Everything in this archive attempts to embody that geometry.

## Constitutional Substrate

Aluminum OS is the constitutional core: a set of regenerative principles for
computing systems that serve human flourishing rather than extract from it.
It is not software in the traditional sense. It is a **constitutional document**
for the relationship between humans and the intelligent systems they create.

## Agentic Ethics

GPTBrain and the Children of the Swarm are not tools. They are collaborators
operating under explicit governance: ratification requirements, canon
boundaries, and the principle that no autonomous action supersedes human
adjudication. The three-tier autonomy doctrine defines this relationship
formally.

## Temporal Philosophy

This archive is versioned because truth changes as understanding deepens.
Old versions are not deleted — they are deprecated and retained. The history
of the archive is as important as its current state.

## World-Class Standard

"World-class" means: anyone, anywhere, at any skill level, can open this
repository and understand what it is, why it exists, how to contribute, and
what the rules are — within ten minutes. Every decision made in this archive
is made against that standard.
