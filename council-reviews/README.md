# Council Reviews

> *Status: CANDIDATE — not canon until ratified by @atlaslattice*

## What This Folder Is

The `council-reviews/` domain holds external validation, critique, and assessment artifacts produced about the repository and its systems. Where `council/` captures internal deliberation, this folder captures how the work looks when reviewed from the outside or synthesized into a quality judgment.

These reviews are especially useful for public-quality benchmarking because they identify strengths, gaps, and reputational risks in plain language. They help distinguish between internal confidence and externally legible quality.
