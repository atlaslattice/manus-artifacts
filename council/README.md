# Council

> *Status: CANDIDATE — not canon until ratified by @atlaslattice*

## What This Folder Is

The `council/` domain records formal or semi-formal multi-model deliberation sessions. These artifacts capture how major architectural decisions, governance phrases, review conclusions, and synthesis events emerged through conversation rather than through a single isolated authoring pass.

For readers trying to understand why certain doctrines or structures exist, this folder provides the decision theater. It shows how Atlas Lattice uses councils as an epistemic and governance mechanism for reviewing claims, testing coherence, and surfacing candidate conclusions.
