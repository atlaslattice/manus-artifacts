## 4. Preserved CILO-v0.2 Requirements

### INV-L1 — Bio-Closure Invariance

```text
Achieve and maintain ≥98% overall mass closure for air, water, nutrients, and organics for 50+ people over ≥5 years with minimal resupply. Quantify and control trace gas accumulation, microbial succession, and nutrient stoichiometry under 1/6g and regolith-derived inputs.
```

### INV-L2 — Living System Stability

```text
Design a regenerative permaculture + bioreactor + mycology CLSS that remains dynamically stable against blooms, crashes, mutations, and cascading failures in a closed, radiation-exposed volume. Define containment and failover mechanisms.
```

### INV-L3 — Dart Payload Integrity

```text
Guarantee >99.9% survival and functionality of 0.5–3 ton payloads through lunar ram-acceleration, transit, and maglock docking. Protect against thermal shock, vibration spectra, and electrostatic dust abrasion.
```

Ledgerwake patch:

```text
Replace “guarantee” with “target and validate” until a payload survivability test campaign exists.
```

### INV-L4 — Dust-Immune Robotics

```text
Achieve reliable long-duration autonomous operation of Optimus-class swarm for fine assembly and maintenance in pervasive abrasive, charged lunar regolith. Quantify MTBF and dust ingress protection levels.
```

### INV-L5 — Lava Tube Structural Invariance

```text
Certify long-term geological stability of chosen lava tube under habitat loading, ISRU excavation, and human activity. Develop non-invasive validation methods with high confidence bounds.
```

### INV-L6 — Power Sovereignty / Nuclear

```text
Deliver ≥500 kW continuous with N+2 or better redundancy for ≥10 years using modular fission systems. Define failure isolation, maintenance intervals, and dust/radiation hardening requirements.
```

### INV-L7 — Dust System Invariance

```text
Prevent chronic performance degradation of critical systems from lunar dust over decades. Prove sufficiency of multi-layer mitigation: electrostatic, piezo, active cleaning, and zoning.
```

### INV-L8 — Psychological Sovereignty

```text
Guarantee acceptable long-term cognitive, emotional, and social performance for crews in extreme isolation. Quantify efficacy of ritual, play, culture, and Burning Man-style interventions with retention and productivity metrics.
```

Ledgerwake patch:

```text
Replace “guarantee” with “measure, support, and maintain within defined thresholds.”
```

### INV-L9 — Thermal Balance Invariance

```text
Manage ≥500 kW waste heat rejection in a lava tube environment without unacceptable condensation, thermal gradients, or external radiator mass. Solve the coupled heat-mass transfer problem in partial vacuum / regolith interfaces.
```

### INV-L10 — Uncrewed Compute Sovereignty

```text
Enable profitable, ultra-secure compute operations with zero on-site humans for ≥12 months. Architecturally guarantee physical security, thermal stability, and autonomous maintenance of high-density GPU clusters.
```

Ledgerwake patch:

```text
Profitability and zero-human runtime are hypotheses requiring pilot evidence.
```

### INV-L11 — Mass and Logistics Closure

```text
Achieve full mass balance with high-cadence Lunar HAVOK darts. Identify and eliminate hidden mass sinks: spares, redundancy, growth margin, and waste in the two-tier logistics model.
```

### INV-L12 — ISRU Structural Reliability

```text
Prove regolith-based additive manufacturing can produce consistent, load-bearing, vacuum-compatible structural elements with known fatigue life under lunar thermal cycling and radiation.
```

### INV-L13 — Biological Containment

```text
Guarantee zero uncontrolled pathogen escape or harmful mutation in integrated bioreactor, mycology, and human systems. Architecturally enforce food safety and nutritional stability.
```

Ledgerwake patch:

```text
Zero-risk biological language is unsafe. Convert to containment probability thresholds, monitoring requirements, and isolation/fallback modes.
```

### INV-L14 — Governance Latency Invariance

```text
Prevent governance failure under multi-second Earth latency. Design constitutional mechanisms that maintain legitimacy, rapid decision-making, and conflict resolution in isolated conditions.
```

### INV-L15 — Radiation Protection Invariance

```text
Prove lava tube plus regolith shielding delivers acceptable crew radiation dose over multi-year missions, including SPE events and secondary activation products.
```

### INV-L16 — Economic Value Invariance

```text
Demonstrate sustainable premium pricing, e.g. 2–5x, for lunar ultra-secure compute with realistic utilization, customer acquisition, and latency tolerance curves.
```

### INV-L17 — Robotic Dexterity Floor

```text
Define and achieve minimum dexterity, perception, and cognitive thresholds for Optimus or equivalent systems to reliably execute modular assembly and maintenance in lunar conditions.
```

### INV-L18 — Lunar Launch Economics

```text
Prove Lunar HAVOK dart system delivers superior cost-per-kg and cadence compared to continued Earth-launched Starship cargo after all infrastructure and maintenance overhead.
```

### INV-L19 — Cascading Failure Prevention

```text
Architect the system such that no single failure or credible double failure leads to habitat loss. Define and enforce minimum independent protection layers for all critical functions.
```

### INV-L20 — Human Talent Invariance

```text
Design cultural, incentive, and sovereignty structures that attract and retain exceptional talent for multi-year rotations despite superior terrestrial compensation options. Foundational to INV-1.
```
