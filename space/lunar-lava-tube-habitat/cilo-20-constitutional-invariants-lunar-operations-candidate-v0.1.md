## 3. Preserved CILO List

### INV-L1 — Absolute Bio-Closure / CLSS 99.5%+

Problem:

```text
Achieve extremely high material closure for air, water, nutrients, and food for 50+ people over multi-year operation in 1/6g, while controlling trace contaminants, microbial drift, and nutrient cycling instability.
```

Ledgerwake requirement translation:

```text
Define closure targets by subsystem; test trace accumulation; model resupply fallback; avoid treating 99.5% as guaranteed until validated.
```

### INV-L2 — Regenerative CLSS Resilience

Problem:

```text
Determine whether permaculture, bioreactor, and mycology stacks can maintain dynamic stability against fungal bloom, algal crash, pathogen mutation, radiation, altered gravity fluid dynamics, and regolith-derived inputs.
```

Requirement translation:

```text
Build biological failure tree and containment protocols before crew dependency.
```

### INV-L3 — Lunar HAVOK Dart Integrity / Payload INV-1

Problem:

```text
Quantify payload survival rate for 1–3 ton darts under launch, transit, docking, thermal shock, vibration, and dust exposure.
```

Requirement translation:

```text
Payload survivability test campaign required before CLSS, compute, or human-critical parts rely on dart delivery.
```

### INV-L4 — Optimus Swarm Autonomy / Dust-Immunity

Problem:

```text
Achieve long-duration autonomous robotic assembly and maintenance despite abrasive electrostatic lunar regolith, vacuum, fine manipulation requirements, and 1/6g dynamics.
```

Requirement translation:

```text
Define mean time between critical failures, maintenance loops, and dust-tolerance test environments.
```

### INV-L5 — Lava Tube Structural Invariance

Problem:

```text
Certify seismic risk, roof collapse probability, thermal cycling behavior, and long-term structural stability of candidate lava tubes without excessive human exploration risk.
```

Requirement translation:

```text
Site-selection criteria, robotic inspection, ground-penetrating radar, structural simulation, and exclusion zones required.
```

### INV-L6 — Nuclear Power System Invariance / 10+ Year Uptime

Problem:

```text
Evaluate whether 6–10 Kilopower-derived reactors can provide 500+ kW continuous power for 10+ years with N+2 redundancy under lunar dust, radiation, and maintenance constraints.
```

Requirement translation:

```text
Reactor count, thermal rejection, shielding, maintainability, and launch approval need independent model receipts.
```

### INV-L7 — Absolute Dust Mitigation

Problem:

```text
Prevent chronic degradation of seals, solar arrays, radiators, moving parts, and active zones from lunar dust over decades.
```

Requirement translation:

```text
Dust mitigation is a critical system, not an accessory. Electrostatic, gel, cleaning, airlock, and maintenance layers need tests.
```

### INV-L8 — Psychological Sovereignty / 50-Person Resilience

Problem:

```text
Measure and mitigate cognitive, emotional, and social degradation curves under isolation. Test whether ritual, play, entertainment, and culture improve retention/productivity under lunar stress.
```

Requirement translation:

```text
Layer 11 culture becomes a human-factors subsystem with metrics, not a vibes layer.
```

### INV-L9 — Thermal Invariance / 500 kW+ Rejection

Problem:

```text
Manage 500 kW+ waste heat inside or near a thermally massive lava tube, including condensation, gradients, and radiator requirements.
```

Requirement translation:

```text
Thermal model is a critical blocker for compute-first revenue and nuclear power assumptions.
```

### INV-L10 — Autonomous Compute Sovereignty / Zero-Human Revenue

Problem:

```text
Determine whether revenue-generating secure compute can run for 6–18 months with zero humans onsite, while avoiding thermal runaway, physical security failure, and maintenance breakdown.
```

Requirement translation:

```text
Zero-human revenue is a hypothesis requiring a secure compute pilot and operations model.
```

### INV-L11 — Mass Closure and Logistics Invariance

```text

Mass closure register required before timeline acceleration claims.
```
