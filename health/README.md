# Health

> *Status: CANDIDATE — not canon until ratified by @atlaslattice*

## What This Folder Is

The `health/` domain contains health-rights, patient-sovereignty, and facility research artifacts. It is where Atlas Lattice's governance and sovereignty logic touch medical reality: documenting rights, complaint processes, care environments, and the practical constraints surrounding treatment access and bodily autonomy.

This folder is both personal and infrastructural. It shows how constitutional thinking in the archive is applied to real-world care systems, especially around patient agency, legal protections, and health data sovereignty.
