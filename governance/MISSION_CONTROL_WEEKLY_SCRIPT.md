# Mission Control Weekly Script
Status: Candidate
Date: 2026-05-28

15-minute weekly governance ritual plus quarterly review agenda.

## Weekly 15-minute script

1. Minute 0-3: quality gates and incident scan
2. Minute 3-7: candidate promotion and adjudication queue status
3. Minute 7-11: KG connectivity and validation receipt review
4. Minute 11-15: assign next-week quests and owners

## Quarterly review agenda

- KPI trend review
- Governance risk heatmap updates
- Retention and succession drill outcomes
- Canon promotion and revocation retrospective
