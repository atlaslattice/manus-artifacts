# Canon Revocation Process
Status: Candidate
Date: 2026-05-28

Process for revoking canon status when trust or evidence thresholds fail.

## Revocation triggers

- Evidence fraud or provenance break
- Ratification procedural invalidation
- Critical conflict unresolved beyond SLA

## Evidence threshold

At least one of:
- Direct contradiction from higher-tier evidence
- Missing mandatory canon trust fields
- Council-adjudicated integrity failure

## Public notice steps

1. Publish revocation notice with reason and affected artifacts.
2. Update register status and link superseding candidate.
3. Route remediation through weekly mission-control and quarterly review.
