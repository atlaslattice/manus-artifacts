# Council Review Workflow
Status: Candidate
Date: 2026-05-26

This workflow defines the step-by-step path for council review of high-importance artifacts.
It is the core governance engine for submission, triage, review, vote, adjudication, and publication.

## Workflow stages

### 1. Submission

The submitting steward provides:

- artifact path and title
- lifecycle state requested
- summary of what the artifact does
- supporting evidence, lineage, and known risks

### 2. Triage

Governance determines whether the artifact is:

- routine candidate publication
- canon promotion candidate
- deprecation or conflict case
- high-stakes artifact requiring external review

### 3. Review

Reviewers examine:

- scope and coherence
- provenance and evidence quality
- lifecycle correctness
- impact on existing canon or project doctrine
- public trust implications

### 4. Vote

When a vote is required, record:

- attendees
- quorum status
- votes for and against
- abstentions and recusals
- disposition

### 5. Adjudication

If canon authority or material conflict is involved, final adjudication is performed by @atlaslattice.
Adjudication confirms the governing outcome and any required follow-on actions.

### 6. Publication

Publish the result through:

- lifecycle label changes
- changelog updates if warranted
- canon decision log entry where required
- lineage, archival, or deprecation updates

## Decision record format

Use the structure defined in [../docs/CANON_DECISION_LOG_FORMAT.md](../docs/CANON_DECISION_LOG_FORMAT.md).
Every substantive council outcome should be linkable and durable.

## Timing guidance

- routine candidate reviews: as needed
- canon review windows: preferably batched quarterly
- urgent incidents: immediate convening

## Review standard

Council review is not just content critique.
It is the process that binds doctrine, stewardship, and public legitimacy together.
