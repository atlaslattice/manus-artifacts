# Decision Rights Matrix
Status: Candidate
Date: 2026-05-26

This matrix clarifies who can act on archive artifacts by role.
It complements the stewardship map by distinguishing maintenance responsibility from authority.

| Role | Create | Edit | Promote to Candidate | Promote to Canon | Archive | Deprecate | Adjudicate conflicts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Contributor | Yes | Yes, within scope | No | No | No | No | No |
| Domain steward | Yes | Yes | Yes | Recommend only | Yes, with record | Recommend or execute for low-risk candidate cases | No |
| Governance steward | Yes | Yes | Yes for governance artifacts | Recommend only | Yes with notice | Yes with council escalation when needed | No |
| Full council | No direct drafting requirement | Review role | Ratifies exceptional cases | Yes, by ratification | Yes for canon-linked cases | Yes for canon-linked cases | Reviews |
| @atlaslattice | Yes | Yes | Yes | Final adjudication required | Yes | Yes | Yes |

## Notes

- No artifact becomes canon without both full council ratification and adjudication by @atlaslattice.
- Domain stewards own readiness; they do not unilaterally create canon.
- Emergency actions should be recorded and normalized into governance records afterward.
