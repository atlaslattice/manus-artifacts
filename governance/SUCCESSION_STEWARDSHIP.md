# Succession Stewardship
Status: Candidate
Date: 2026-05-26

This document defines how stewardship is transferred safely when roles change.
Succession safety is essential for a repository whose value depends on continuity, not just content accumulation.

## Handoff protocol

When stewardship transfers, the outgoing steward should provide:

- current domain priorities
- outstanding risks
- known canon or promotion candidates
- review cadence expectations
- key lineage and retention issues

## Knowledge transfer checklist

- [ ] domain inventory reviewed
- [ ] open issues and unresolved questions handed over
- [ ] decision rights clarified
- [ ] backup steward confirmed
- [ ] emergency contacts or escalation route identified

## Emergency continuity plan

If a steward becomes unexpectedly unavailable:

1. backup steward becomes acting steward
2. governance reviews critical pending decisions
3. high-trust changes pause until continuity is restored
4. mission control records the gap and remediation plan

## Success principle

A stewardship transfer is complete only when the incoming steward can explain the domain's current state, next review window, and top unresolved risks without relying on undocumented context.
