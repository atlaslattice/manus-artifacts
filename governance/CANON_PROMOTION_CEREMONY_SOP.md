# Canon Promotion Ceremony SOP
Status: Candidate
Date: 2026-05-28

Operational SOP for promoting candidate artifacts toward canon status.

## Initiation

- Promotion request submitted with evidence bundle
- Council reviewer assigned
- Adjudicator notified

## Quorum requirements

- Council quorum must be satisfied per meeting packet
- No unresolved P0/P1 governance incidents

## Ceremony steps

1. Validate required trust fields and provenance.
2. Record vote outcome and ratification event ID.
3. Publish candidate-to-canon release notes.
4. Update canon candidate register.
